    Nahw 201 - Study Notes

    Date: 2026-10-19

    Session Overview

    This session introduced Maf'ool Ma'ahu (مَفْعُولٌ مَعَهُ), the fourth type of Maf'ool covered in the Mansoobaat section. The class focused on:

    • The definition of Maf'ool Ma'ahu and the nature of the Waaw that precedes it
    • The two conditions that determine when you may choose between Maf'ool Ma'ahu and Ma'toof
    • When Nasb (منصوب) becomes obligatory (mutayyan)
    • The rule governing 'Atf upon Zameer Muttasil, and the role of Zameer Munfasil in making 'Atf permissible
    • Application examples: Fa'il absent in lafz but present in ma'na

    Grammar Rules Covered

    1. Definition of Maf'ool Ma'ahu (مَفْعُولٌ مَعَهُ)

    Maf'ool Ma'ahu is a Maf'ool (object) that comes after a Waaw, where that Waaw carries the meaning of مَعَ (ma'a — "with/along with"), not the meaning of "and." It denotes musaahabat (مُصَاحَبَة — companionship/co-occurrence), indicating that the Fa'il and Maf'ool Ma'ahu occurred together.

    • Example: جَاءَ الْبَرْدُ وَالْجُبَّاتِ — "The cold came along with the jackets"
    • Fa'il = الْبَرْدُ (the cold); Maf'ool Ma'ahu = الْجُبَّاتِ (the jackets)
    • The Waaw here = مَعَ (with), not "and"
    • Example: جِئْتُ أَنَا وَزَيْدًا — "I came along with Zayd"
    • The Fa'il (تُ) and Zayd co-occurred; Zayd is Maf'ool Ma'ahu → منصوب

    Key distinction: Maf'ool Ma'ahu looks like a Fa'il but is technically a Maf'ool; it is منصوب (two dammah → fathah) because it carries Maf'ooliyyat, not Faa'iliyyat.

    2. The Two Conditions (إِنْ كَانَ الْفِعْلُ مَوْجُودًا وَجَازَ الْعَطْفُ)

    When a noun follows the Waaw of Ma'iyyah, two conditions determine whether you have a choice between Nasb (Maf'ool Ma'ahu) or Rafa'/other ('Atf):

    Condition Requirement
    1. Fa'il is mawjood (موجود) The Fa'il must be explicitly present (lafzan)
    2. 'Atf is jaa'iz (جائز) 'Atf (conjunction) upon the Ma'toof 'Alayh must be permissible

    If both conditions are met → جَازَ فِيهِ وَجْهَانِ (two options are valid): 1. Make it منصوب on the basis of Maf'ool Ma'ahu (Maf'ooliyyat) 2. Make it Ma'toof — it takes the i'raab of its Ma'toof 'Alayh

    If 'Atf is NOT jaa'iz → نَصْبٌ مُتَعَيَّن (Nasb is obligatory) — it must be Maf'ool Ma'ahu, منصوب.

    If the Fa'il is ma'nan (مَعْنًا) only — present in meaning but absent in lafz — and 'Atf IS jaa'iz → 'Atf is mutayyan (obligatory); Maf'ool Ma'ahu is not valid.

    3. When is 'Atf Not Jaa'iz? — The Zameer Muttasil Rule

    Rule (الأَصْل): 'Atf of an Ism Zaahir (ظاهر) upon a Zameer Muttasil (ضَمِيرٌ مُتَّصِل) is not permissible unless a Zameer Munfasil (ضَمِيرٌ مُنْفَصِل) is inserted between the Ma'toof 'Alayh (Zameer Muttasil) and the Ma'toof (Ism Zaahir).

    Why? Ism Zaahir is strong; Zameer Muttasil is weak. There is no mutaabaqah (match/compatibility) between them for 'Atf unless the Zameer Muttasil is "backed up" by a Zameer Munfasil, which strengthens it.

    Examples:

    Sentence 'Atf Allowed? Reason
    جَاءَ زَيْدٌ وَخَالِدٌ ✅ Yes Both Ma'toof and Ma'toof 'Alayh are Ism Zaahir
    جِئْتُ وَخَالِدًا ❌ No Ma'toof 'Alayh is تُ (Zameer Muttasil); no Zameer Munfasil present → Nasb mutayyan
    جِئْتُ أَنَا وَخَالِدٌ ✅ Yes أَنَا (Zameer Munfasil) inserted → 'Atf now permissible; خَالِدٌ is Ma'toof upon أَنَا/تُ → مرفوع
    جِئْتِ أَنْتِ وَخَالِدٌ ✅ Yes أَنْتِ is the required Zameer Munfasil for تِ
    جِئْتُمْ أَنْتُمْ وَخَالِدٌ ✅ Yes أَنْتُمْ is the required Zameer Munfasil for تُمْ

    The Zameer Munfasil must match (mutaabaqah) the Zameer Muttasil it is reinforcing.

    4. I'raab of Ma'toof — Always Follows Ma'toof 'Alayh

    Ma'toof has no independent i'raab; it always takes the i'raab of its Ma'toof 'Alayh.

    Ma'toof 'Alayh State Ma'toof State
    مرفوع مرفوع
    منصوب منصوب
    مجرور مجرور

    Examples: - ضَرَبْتُ زَيْدًا وَخَالِدًا — خَالِدًا is Ma'toof; زَيْدًا is Ma'toof 'Alayh → both منصوب (objects of ضَرَبَ) - جَاءَ زَيْدٌ وَخَالِدٌ — خَالِدٌ Ma'toof upon زَيْدٌ (مرفوع Fa'il) → مرفوع - مَرَرْتُ بِزَيْدٍ وَخَالِدٍ — خَالِدٍ Ma'toof upon زَيْدٍ (مجرور with بِ) → مجرور

    5. Fa'il Mawjood Ma'nan (مَعْنًا) — Present in Meaning, Absent in Lafz

    When the Fa'il is implied in meaning but not explicitly stated in lafz, and 'Atf IS permissible → 'Atf is mutayyan (obligatory); the option of Maf'ool Ma'ahu does not apply.

    Example: مَالَكَ وَزَيْدًا — "What is the matter with you and Zayd?" (lit. "What do you and Zayd do?") - Full meaning: مَا تَصْنَعُ أَنْتَ وَزَيْدٌ - The Fa'il is implied (أَنْتَ), not explicit in lafz - 'Atf here is not jaa'iz because Ma'toof 'Alayh is كَاف (Zameer Muttasil); no Zameer Munfasil → Nasb mutayyan - If you add أَنْتَ: مَا لَكَ أَنْتَ وَزَيْدٌ → 'Atf becomes permissible → زَيْدٌ is Ma'toof (مرفوع), matching كاف/أنت

    Example: مَشَى أَنُوكَ وَعَمْرٌو / مَا شَأْنُكَ وَعَمْرًا - شَأْنُ is Mudaaf; كَاف is Mudaaf Ilayh (مجرور) - 'Atf upon كَاف (Zameer Muttasil) → not allowed without Zameer Munfasil - Add أَنْتَ: مَا شَأْنُكَ أَنْتَ وَعَمْرٌو → 'Atf allowed; عَمْرٌو takes i'raab of Ma'toof 'Alayh - Ma'toof 'Alayh (كَاف) is مجرور → عَمْرٌو = مجرور (عَمْرٍو)

    Example plural: مَا شَأْنُكُمْ أَنْتُمْ وَعَمْرٌو - Zameer Munfasil required = أَنْتُمْ (matching كُمْ)

    6. Summary Decision Tree

    Noun after Waaw of Ma'iyyah
│
├── Is Fa'il present LAFZAN?
│   ├── YES → Is 'Atf jaa'iz?
│   │           ├── YES → Two options: Maf'ool Ma'ahu (منصوب) OR Ma'toof (follows Ma'toof 'Alayh)
│   │           └── NO  → Nasb mutayyan (must be Maf'ool Ma'ahu, منصوب)
│   │
│   └── NO (Fa'il only Ma'nan) → Is 'Atf jaa'iz?
│               ├── YES → 'Atf mutayyan (must be Ma'toof)
│               └── NO  → Nasb mutayyan (must be Maf'ool Ma'ahu, منصوب)
│
'Atf is NOT jaa'iz when:
  Ma'toof 'Alayh is Zameer Muttasil AND no Zameer Munfasil is present

    Important Vocabulary

    Arabic Term Meaning
    مَفْعُولٌ مَعَهُ Maf'ool Ma'ahu — "object of accompaniment"; noun following Waaw of companionship
    مُصَاحَبَة Musaahabat — companionship, co-occurrence
    وَاوُ الْمَعِيَّة Waaw of Ma'iyyah — the Waaw meaning "with/along with" (not "and")
    حَرْفُ عَطْف Harf 'Atf — conjunction particle (e.g., وَ meaning "and")
    عَطْف 'Atf — grammatical conjunction; connecting one noun to another
    مَعْطُوف Ma'toof — the word being conjoined (follows Ma'toof 'Alayh in i'raab)
    مَعْطُوفٌ عَلَيْه Ma'toof 'Alayh — the word the Ma'toof is conjoined to
    جَائِز Jaa'iz — permissible, allowed
    مُتَعَيَّن Mutayyan — fixed, obligatory (only one option)
    مَوْجُود Mawjood — present, existing
    لَفْظًا Lafzan — explicitly/verbally present
    مَعْنًا Ma'nan — present in meaning only
    ضَمِيرٌ مُتَّصِل Zameer Muttasil — attached/connected pronoun (e.g., تُ، كَ، نَا)
    ضَمِيرٌ مُنْفَصِل Zameer Munfasil — detached/separate pronoun (e.g., أَنَا، أَنْتَ، أَنْتُمْ)
    اسْمٌ ظَاهِر Ism Zaahir — an explicit noun (not a pronoun)
    مَنْصُوبَات Mansoobaat — the chapter of nouns that take Nasb (فتحة)
    مُضَاف Mudaaf — the first word in an Idaafah construction
    مُضَافٌ إِلَيْه Mudaaf Ilayh — the second word in Idaafah; always مجرور
    مُتَابَقَة Mutaabaqah — agreement/compatibility between two grammatical elements
    تَوَقُّف Tawaqquف — to pause/withhold judgment (mentioned as the "safest" answer when uncertain)

    I'rab (Sentence Analysis) Examples

    Example 1: جَاءَ الْبَرْدُ وَالْجُبَّاتِ

    Word I'raab Reason
    جَاءَ فِعْلٌ مَاضٍ Past tense verb
    الْبَرْدُ فَاعِلٌ مَرْفُوع Subject of جَاءَ
    وَ وَاوُ الْمَعِيَّة Waaw meaning "with," not "and"
    الْجُبَّاتِ مَفْعُولٌ مَعَهُ مَنْصُوب Mansoob; co-occurred with the Fa'il (the jackets came with the cold)

    Example 2: جِئْتُ أَنَا وَزَيْدًا

    Word I'raab Reason
    جِئْتُ فِعْلٌ + فَاعِل (تُ) تُ is Zameer Muttasil, Fa'il مرفوع محلاً
    أَنَا ضَمِيرٌ مُنْفَصِل تَوْكِيد Reinforces تُ; makes 'Atf permissible; both refer to same person
    وَ وَاوُ الْمَعِيَّة (or 'Atf if conditions met)
    زَيْدًا مَفْعُولٌ مَعَهُ مَنْصُوب OR if 'Atf considered: Ma'toof upon أَنَا/تُ → مرفوع زَيْدٌ

    With أَنَا present, two options are valid: زَيْدًا (Maf'ool Ma'ahu) or زَيْدٌ (Ma'toof). Without أَنَا (جِئْتُ وَزَيْدًا), Nasb is mutayyan.

    Word I'raab Reason
    مَا اسْمُ اسْتِفْهَام Interrogative; Mubtada'
    لَكَ جَارٌّ وَمَجْرُور لِ + كَاف; Khabar
    وَ وَاوُ الْمَعِيَّة
    زَيْدًا مَفْعُولٌ مَعَهُ مَنْصُوب مُتَعَيَّن Nasb obligatory; 'Atf upon كَاف (Zameer Muttasil) not allowed; no Zameer Munfasil present

    Practice Examples

    Q: In جِئْتُ وَخَالِدًا — why is خَالِدًا منصوب? A: 'Atf is not permissible because Ma'toof 'Alayh would be تُ (Zameer Muttasil) and no Zameer Munfasil (أَنَا) is present. Therefore Nasb is mutayyan — خَالِدًا is Maf'ool Ma'ahu.

    Q: In جِئْتُمْ أَنْتُمْ — if you want to add وَخَالِد, what i'raab does خَالِد take? A: أَنْتُمْ is the Zameer Munfasil, making 'Atf permissible. Ma'toof 'Alayh is تُمْ (Fa'il, مرفوع), so خَالِدٌ is مرفوع.

    Q: In مَا شَأْنُكُمْ وَعَمْرٍو — is 'Atf permissible without any addition? A: No. كُمْ is Zameer Muttasil (Mudaaf Ilayh, مجرور). 'Atf directly upon it is not allowed. You must add أَنْتُمْ: مَا شَأْنُكُمْ أَنْتُمْ وَعَمْرٍو. Now عَمْرٍو is Ma'toof → مجرور (matching كُمْ).

    Q: ضَرَبْتُ زَيْدًا وَخَالِدًا — what is the i'raab of خَالِدًا and why? A: خَالِدًا is Ma'toof upon زَيْدًا (Maf'ool bih, منصوب). Ma'toof follows Ma'toof 'Alayh → خَالِدًا is منصوب.

    Q: جَاءَ زَيْدٌ وَخَالِدٌ — same question for خَالِدٌ. A: Ma'toof upon زَيْدٌ (Fa'il, مرفوع) → خَالِدٌ مرفوع. Both are Fa'ils of جَاءَ.

    Key Takeaways

    1. Maf'ool Ma'ahu is always منصوب. The Waaw before it is Waaw of Ma'iyyah (meaning "with"), not Harf 'Atf (meaning "and").

    2. Two conditions for having a choice (Maf'ool Ma'ahu or Ma'toof): Fa'il must be present lafzan, and 'Atf must be jaa'iz. If either condition fails, one option becomes obligatory (mutayyan).

    3. 'Atf upon Zameer Muttasil is not allowed unless a matching Zameer Munfasil is inserted. This is the most common reason 'Atf becomes impermissible in these constructions.

    4. Zameer Munfasil must match the Zameer Muttasil it reinforces: تُ → أَنَا, تِ → أَنْتِ, تُمْ → أَنْتُمْ, كَ → أَنْتَ, كُمْ → أَنْتُمْ, etc.

    5. Ma'toof has no independent i'raab — it always mirrors its Ma'toof 'Alayh. Always identify the Ma'toof 'Alayh and its i'raab state first.

    6. When Fa'il is ma'nan (implied, not explicit) and 'Atf is permissible, 'Atf is mutayyan — Maf'ool Ma'ahu is not an option in this case.

    7. Practical rule: When you see وَ after a verb with a Zameer Fa'il, first ask: is there a Zameer Munfasil? If yes → two options. If no → Nasb is the only valid choice.
