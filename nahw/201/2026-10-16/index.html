    Nahw 201 - Study Notes

    Date: 2026-10-16

    Session Overview

    This session continued the topic of Mansubat (words in the accusative case). The previous topic, Maf'ul al-Mutlaq, was completed. This session covered the following topics:

    1. Finishing details on Munada (vocative) — معرّف باللام, Tarkhim, Mandub
    2. Maf'ul Fih (Zarf — adverb of time and place)
    3. Maf'ul Lahu (adverb of reason/cause)

    Maf'ul Ma'ahu and Hal were deferred to the next session (Monday).

    Grammar Rules Covered

    1. Munada with لام (Definite Article)

    • If the Munada (the one being called) is معرّف باللام (made definite by ال), you cannot call upon it directly.
    • Instead, insert أَيُّهَا (for masculine) or أَيَّتُهَا (for feminine) between the Harf Nida and the Munada.
    • Example: يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ — because you cannot say يَا الناسُ directly.
    • Example: يَا أَيُّهَا الرَّجُلُ / يَا أَيَّتُهَا الْمَرْأَةُ

    2. Tarkhim (تَرْخِيم) — Softening the Name

    • Definition: Dropping the last one or two letters of the Munada for lightness (تخفيف) or out of affection.
    • Examples:
    • مالك → يَا مَالُ
    • منصور → يَا مَنْصُو (dropping ر)
    • عثمان → يَا عُثْمُ (dropping ان)
    • Harakah on the Murakham (trimmed) Munada: You may give either:
    • Dhamma (e.g., يَا هَارُ), or
    • Harakata Asliyya — the original vowel of that letter (e.g., يَا هَارِ, since the Ra in حارِث is maksur)

    3. Mandub (النَّدْبَة)

    • يَا is used for both Nida (calling) and Mandub (mourning/lamenting over someone).
    • وا is exclusively for Mandub (mourning).
    • Example: وَا زَيْدَاه — lamenting the death of Zaid
    • The grammatical rules for Mandub follow the same rules as Munada.

    4. Shibhu Mudaf (شِبْهُ الْمُضَاف)

    • Definition: A word that resembles a Mudaf (first part of a genitive construct) in that it is incomplete in meaning without a following word.
    • Most commonly: Ism Fa'il and Ism Maf'ul used in an active/operative sense.
    • Example: يَا طَالِعًا جَبَلًا — "O one climbing a mountain!"
    • طَالِعًا = Ism Fa'il, Mansoob due to Nida (as Shibhu Mudaf)
    • جَبَلًا = Mansoob as Maf'ul Bih of طَالِع (due to the Amal of Ism Fa'il, not due to Nida)
    • Example: يَا قَارِئًا كِتَابًا — "O one reading a book!"
    • قَارِئًا = Shibhu Mudaf, Mansoob
    • كِتَابًا = Mansoob as Maf'ul Bih of قَارِئ

    Key distinction: In a true Mudaf structure (يَا صَاحِبَ الْكِتَابِ), the Mudaf is Mansoob and the Mudaf Ilayh is Majrur. In Shibhu Mudaf, the operative Ism Fa'il governs its own Maf'ul as Mansoob independently.

    5. I'rab of Nakira Munada

    Type Description I'rab Example
    Nakira Muayyannah Indefinite but the specific person addressed is known Marfu' (مضموم) يَا رَجُلُ
    Nakira Ghayru Muayyannah Indefinite and person is unspecified Mansoob (مفتوح) يَا رَجُلًا
    • Nakira Muayyannah is treated like Ma'rifa because the referent is known, so its I'rab resembles Ma'rifa (Marfu').

    6. Maf'ul Fih / Zarf (ظَرْف — الْمَفْعُول فِيه)

    Definition: وَهُوَ اسْمٌ مَا وَقَعَ فِعْلٌ فِيهِ مِنَ الزَّمَانِ وَالْمَكَانِ — A noun denoting the time or place in which the action occurred.

    Also called Zarf. Always Mansoob, with في understood (muqaddar/mahzuf).

    Types:

    A. Zarf Zaman (ظَرْف زَمَان) — Adverb of Time

    Sub-type Meaning Ruling
    Mubham (مُبْهَم) Limitless/undefined time — e.g., دَهْر (era) Mansoob, في muqaddar
    Mahdud (مَحْدُود) Defined/limited time — e.g., يَوْم, لَيْلَة, شَهْر, سَنَة Mansoob, في muqaddar
    • Both types: في is always hidden (mahzuf).
    • Example: صُمْتُ دَهْرًا = صُمْتُ فِي دَهْرٍ
    • Example: سَافَرْتُ شَهْرًا = سَافَرْتُ فِي شَهْرٍ

    B. Zarf Makan (ظَرْف مَكَان) — Adverb of Place

    Sub-type Meaning Ruling
    Mubham (مُبْهَم) Undefined/boundless place — e.g., خَلْف, أَمَام, فَوْق, تَحْت Mansoob, في muqaddar (hidden)
    Mahdud (مَحْدُود) Defined/bounded place — e.g., دَار, سُوق, مَسْجِد في appears explicitly; Zarf is Majrur
    • Example (Mubham): جَلَسْتُ خَلْفَكَ — "I sat behind you" (no defined limit → في hidden, mansoob)
    • Example (Mahdud): جَلَسْتُ فِي الدَّارِ — "I sat in the house" (defined space → في appears, majrur)

    Summary Table:

    Zarf Type في I'rab of Zarf
    Zaman Mubham Hidden Mansoob
    Zaman Mahdud Hidden Mansoob
    Makan Mubham Hidden Mansoob
    Makan Mahdud Visible Majrur (via في)

    7. Maf'ul Lahu / Maf'ul Li-ajlih (الْمَفْعُول لَهُ / لِأَجْلِهِ)

    Definition: وَهُوَ اسْمٌ مَا لِأَجْلِهِ يَقَعُ الْفِعْلُ الْمَذْكُورُ قَبْلَهُ — A noun that states the reason or motive for which the preceding action was done.

    • Always Mansoob, with لام (لام التعليل) understood (mahzuf).
    • Examples:
    • قَعَدْتُ عَنِ الْحَرْبِ جُبُنًا = قَعَدْتُ عَنِ الْحَرْبِ لِلْجُبُنِ — "I stayed away from war out of cowardice"
    • Quranic example: لَا تَقْتُلُوا أَوْلَادَكُمْ خَشْيَةَ إِمْلَاقٍ — "Do not kill your children out of fear of poverty"
      • خَشْيَةَ = Maf'ul Lahu (Mansoob), إِمْلَاق = Mudaf Ilayh (Majrur)

    Important Vocabulary

    Arabic Term Meaning
    مَنْصُوبَات Words in the accusative/nasb case

    حَرَكَة أَصْلِيَّة The original/underlying vowel of a letter
    نَكِرَة مُعَيَّنَة Indefinite noun with a known/specified referent

    I'rab (Sentence Analysis) Examples

    يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ

    Word I'rab
    يَا حرف نداء، مبني على السكون
    أَيُّ منادى مبني على الضم في محل نصب
    هَا حرف تنبيه
    النَّاسُ بدل أو عطف بيان مرفوع وعلامة رفعه الضمة

    يَا طَالِعًا جَبَلًا

    Word I'rab
    يَا حرف نداء
    طَالِعًا منادى شبه مضاف، منصوب وعلامة نصبه الفتحة
    جَبَلًا مفعول به لاسم الفاعل طالع، منصوب — ليس بسبب النداء

    صُمْتُ دَهْرًا

    Word I'rab
    صُمْتُ فعل وفاعل
    دَهْرًا مفعول فيه (ظرف زمان مبهم)، منصوب، وفي مقدرة — أي: صمتُ فِي دَهْرٍ

    جَلَسْتُ خَلْفَكَ

    Word I'rab
    جَلَسْتُ فعل وفاعل
    خَلْفَكَ ظرف مكان مبهم، منصوب، وفي مقدرة — أي: جلستُ فِي خَلْفِكَ

    جَلَسْتُ فِي الدَّارِ

    Word I'rab
    جَلَسْتُ فعل وفاعل
    فِي حرف جر ظاهر
    الدَّارِ ظرف مكان محدود، مجرور بفي

    قَعَدْتُ عَنِ الْحَرْبِ جُبُنًا

    Word I'rab
    قَعَدْتُ فعل وفاعل
    عَنِ الْحَرْبِ جار ومجرور
    جُبُنًا مفعول لأجله، منصوب، لام مقدرة — أي: لِلْجُبُنِ

    لَا تَقْتُلُوا أَوْلَادَكُمْ خَشْيَةَ إِمْلَاقٍ

    Word I'rab
    لَا تَقْتُلُوا فعل نهي وفاعل
    أَوْلَادَكُمْ مفعول به، منصوب
    خَشْيَةَ مفعول لأجله، منصوب، مضاف
    إِمْلَاقٍ مضاف إليه، مجرور

    Practice Examples

    Munada — identify type and give correct I'rab:

    1. يَا زَيْدُ — Ma'rifa (Alam) → Mansoob, Marfu' in pronunciation → مبني على الضم
    2. يَا رَجُلُ (addressing a specific man in front of you) — Nakira Muayyannah → Marfu' (مضموم)
    3. يَا رَجُلًا (calling out to any man, unspecified) — Nakira Ghayru Muayyannah → Mansoob (مفتوح)
    4. يَا صَاحِبَ الْكِتَابِ — Munada Mudaf → Mansoob; الكتاب Majrur
    5. يَا قَارِئًا كِتَابًا — Shibhu Mudaf → قارئًا Mansoob (Nida); كتابًا Mansoob (Maf'ul Bih of Ism Fa'il)

    Tarkhim drill:

    Original Murakham Possible I'rab
    حَارِث يَا حَارُ Dhamma (ya haru)
    حَارِث يَا حَارِ Harakata Asliyya — kasra (ya hari)
    مَنْصُور يَا مَنْصُو drop ر
    عُثْمَان يَا عُثْمُ drop ان

    Zarf — identify and parse:

    1. سَافَرْتُ شَهْرًا → Zarf Zaman Mahdud, Mansoob, في mahzuf
    2. جَلَسْتُ أَمَامَكَ → Zarf Makan Mubham, Mansoob, في mahzuf
    3. جَلَسْتُ فِي الْمَسْجِدِ → Zarf Makan Mahdud, في ظاهرة, Majrur

    Maf'ul Lahu — identify:

    1. ضَرَبْتُهُ تَأْدِيبًا → reason = تأديبًا (to discipline), Mansoob, لام mahzuf
    2. جِئْتُ إِكْرَامًا لَكَ → reason = إكرامًا, Mansoob

    Key Takeaways

    1. When Munada has ال, never call directly — use أَيُّهَا (m.) or أَيَّتُهَا (f.) as an intermediary.
    2. Tarkhim is permissible in Nida — drop the last letter(s) and give either Dhamma or Harakata Asliyya to the new last letter.
    3. Nakira Muayyannah behaves like Ma'rifa (Marfu'), because the referent is known even if the word is grammatically indefinite.
    4. Shibhu Mudaf is an Ism Fa'il (or similar) that requires a complement — both words are Mansoob, but for different reasons: the Ism Fa'il is Mansoob due to Nida, its Maf'ul is Mansoob due to the Amal of the Ism Fa'il.
    5. Zarf is always Mansoob with في understood — except Zarf Makan Mahdud, where في appears explicitly and the noun becomes Majrur.
    6. Maf'ul Lahu answers the question "why?" — it is Mansoob with لام التعليل implied, and is distinct from Maf'ul Mutlaq, Maf'ul Bih, and Maf'ul Fih.
    7. Masdar is the root source; all Mushtaqat (Ism Fa'il, Ism Maf'ul, Ism Tafdhil, etc.) are derived from the Masdar — not the other way around.
