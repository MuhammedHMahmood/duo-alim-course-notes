**Date:** 2026-09-23

## Session Overview

This session covered two major topics: (1) Mustahab (recommended) timings for the five daily prayers and Witr, including scholarly differences between Hanafi and Shafi'i schools; and (2) the Adhan and Iqamah — their origin, rulings, conditions, and related masail. The session concluded with a brief introduction to the upcoming topic of Shurut al-Salah (pre-conditions of prayer).

---

## Key Themes

1. **Mustahab timings** — recommended times within the valid window for each of the five daily prayers and Witr, with practical guidance for modern contexts
2. **Madhab differences on timing** — particularly Fajr (Isfar vs. Ghalas), Asr (one shadow-length vs. two), and their respective evidential bases
3. **Origin and legal status of Adhan** — the dream of Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Zayd and how the wording was established
4. **Adhan vs. Iqamah — structure and variations** — Tarjih, Taqrar, Tarassul, and Hadar; differences between what was taken from Bilal (RA) and Abu Mahzura (RA)
5. **Conditions and rulings around Adhan** — facing Qibla, state of Wudu, calling before time, and Qada salah
6. **Combining prayers in travel** — a detailed side discussion on the Hanafi position and the Asr time difference in various madhabs

## Fiqh Rulings & Evidence

### Witr Salah

- **Start time:** Same boundary as Isha (when Shafaq disappears), but Isha must be prayed first in order (tartib).
- **Recommended time:** Last portion of the night, after Tahajjud.
- **Rule:** If confident of waking for Tahajjud, delay Witr until then. If not confident, pray before sleeping. If one prays Witr before sleeping and then wakes up, Tahajjud can still be prayed, but making Witr the last salah is the ideal habit.

---

### Mustahab Timings for the Five Prayers

| Prayer | Mustahab Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Fajr** | Isfar — delay until daylight spreads | Hanafi position; allows time to re-pray if salah becomes Fasid with Tiwal al-Mufassal |
| **Zuhr** | Ibrad — delay in summer until it cools; pray early in winter | Sunnah: more people attend when it is cooler |
| **Asr** | Delay, but not until the sun turns yellow/pale | Gives time for Nawafil before Asr; once Asr Fard is prayed, no more Nawafil |
| **Maghrib** | Tajil — pray immediately after sunset | Hadith: "My Ummah will be on Khair as long as they do not delay Maghrib" |
| **Isha** | Delay up to one-third of the night | Hadith (Tirmizi, Ibn Majah): Prophet ﷺ said he would have delayed it if it were not a hardship; follow masjid timing if praying in jama'ah |

**Dalil for Fajr Isfar:** Hadith in Abu Dawud and Tirmizi — "Pray Fajr in Isfar, for it has more reward."

**Shafi'i position on Fajr:** Ghalas (complete darkness). Dalil: Hadith in Bukhari. Imam Shafi'i's general principle is that every salah in its earliest time is Mustahab.

**Important note on madhab differences:** Both Hanafi and Shafi'i positions are grounded in authentic Hadith. Neither should be disparaged. The Hadith cited by Imam Shafi'i (narrated by Abdullah ibn Mas'ud in Tirmizi) is the very same Sahabi from whom many Hanafi evidences derive — yet Ahnaf did not take this specific hadith for this ruling. Both positions rest on coherent usuli principles.

---

### Asr Start Time — Madhab Difference

| Madhab | When Asr begins |
|---|---|
| Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali | When shadow = original length of object (1x) |
| Hanafi (Imam Abu Hanifa) | When shadow = twice the original length of object (2x) |
| Imam Muhammad & Imam Abu Yusuf | Shadow = 1x (aligned with majority) |

This produces roughly a 1–1.5 hour difference depending on season and location.

**Practical ruling for traveler/follower of a different school:** If one's local masjid follows Hanbali/Shafi'i timing, praying with the imam is valid — one need not repeat the prayer. If praying alone, follow the Hanafi timing (Muftabih).

---

### No Nawafil After Fajr Fard and After Asr Fard

- After the Fard of Fajr and after the Fard of Asr: **no Nawafil may be prayed.**
- If one prays Fard at home then arrives at a masjid where Jama'ah is ongoing (Zuhr, for example), one may join — this becomes a Nafl for them. **Exception:** do not join if it is Fajr, Asr, or Maghrib time.
- **Maghrib exception:** Nawafil cannot be in odd numbers; Maghrib and Witr are the only odd-rakah prayers. Therefore no Nafl Jama'ah during Maghrib time.
- **Tahiyyat al-Masjid after Asr/Fajr Fard:** Not allowed per Ahnaf; one may sit and make Azkar instead.
- **Qada prayers:** The only prayer permitted in post-Fajr/post-Asr restricted times is Qada of missed Fara'id (Qada al-Umri or Qada of that day).

---

### Maghrib — No Sunnah Before Jama'ah

Praying Sunnah rakaat after the Maghrib Adhan and before Jama'ah commences is not the Hanafi practice, as it causes delay in Maghrib Jama'ah. The Hadith commanding prompt Maghrib prayer is the basis.

---

### Combining Prayers in Travel (Safar) — Hanafi Position

**Muftabih opinion:** Do not combine prayers by pulling one forward. Instead:
- Delay Zuhr to near the end of its time; pray Asr at its earliest — use the same Wudu for both.
- Delay Maghrib; pray Isha at its earliest.

**If unable to combine on a flight (airline restrictions, no ability to make Wudu):**
- Opinion 1 (dominant/Muftabih): Let the salah become Qada; pray both as Qasr prayers upon reaching the destination.
- Opinion 2: Pray however possible on the flight, but still repeat upon arrival.
- **Not valid:** Temporarily "becoming Shafi'i" to justify combining — a person cannot switch madhabs for convenience.

**Hajj exception (informational):** At Arafat, Asr is pulled forward to Zuhr time (Tajil). At Muzdalifah, Maghrib is delayed to Isha time (Ta'khir) — both forms are observed in Hajj, illustrating the range of the discussion.

**Changing the Muftabih ruling:** Would require an Ijma of scholars across the Ummah (not just local ulama), acknowledging the hardship of modern travel. Until such consensus, the existing ruling stands.

---

### Adhan — Legal Status and Scope

- **Status:** Sunnah (established by strong Sunnah, not Fard)
- **For which prayers:** Five daily Fara'id and Jumu'ah only.
- **No Adhan for:** Witr, Eid (Idayn), Janazah, Kusuf/Khusuf (eclipse), Istisqa (rain prayer), Tarawih, Sunnah/Nawafil prayers.
- **Exception:** The pre-Tahajjud Adhan at the Haramayn (Makkah and Madinah) is based on the practice at the time of Rasulullah ﷺ.

**Consequence of abandonment:** If the people of a city collectively abandon Adhan, Imam Muhammad (RA) says the Muslim ruler must fight them. Imam Abu Yusuf (RA) says they should be imprisoned and beaten — this is considered a grave communal sin.

---

### Tarjih in Adhan

| Position | Ruling |
|---|---|
| **Ahnaf** | No Tarjih — Adhan is called at a consistent pace and volume throughout |
| **Shafi'i** | Tarjih is Masnoon — Shahadatayn said softly first, then repeated loudly |

**Hanafi Dalil:** The Adhan of Bilal (RA), who gave Adhan consistently without Tarjih throughout his life.
**Shafi'i Dalil:** Hadith of Abu Mahzura in Tirmizi.
**Hanafi counter:** The Prophet ﷺ asking Abu Mahzura to repeat was interpreted by him as Tarjih, but Ahnaf understand it as a command to raise his voice — not a command to institute Tarjih.

---

### Iqamah — Words and Structure

**Hanafi position:** Words of Iqamah are the same as Adhan, with:
- Allahu Akbar at the beginning said twice (not four times)
- All phrases repeated twice
- Addition of "Qad qamatis-salah" (twice) after "Hayya 'alal falah"

**Four Muazzins of Rasulullah ﷺ and what each madhab derived from them:**

| Muazzin | Adhan Style | Iqamah Style | Madhab that follows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bilal (RA) | No Tarjih | No Taqrar (phrases once) | Imam Ahmad took both from Bilal |
| Abu Mahzura (RA) | With Tarjih | With Taqrar (phrases twice) | — |
| Abdullah ibn Maktum (RA) | — | — | — |
| Sa'd al-Qaraz (RA) | — | — | — |

**Hanafi synthesis:** Adhan of Bilal (no Tarjih) + Iqamah of Abu Mahzura (with Taqrar).
**Shafi'i synthesis:** Iqamah of Bilal (no Taqrar).

---

### Tarassul in Adhan vs. Hadar in Iqamah

- **Adhan:** Tarassul is Masnoon — pause between words, do not rush.
- **Iqamah:** Hadar is Masnoon — say it quickly and continuously.
- **Dalil:** Hadith in Tirmizi — Prophet ﷺ told Bilal (RA): "When you call Adhan, do Tarassul; when you call Iqamah, do Hadar."

---

### Adhan Facing the Qibla

- **Ruling:** The Mu'azzin must face the Qibla when calling Adhan and Iqamah. Dalil: Hadith in Abu Dawud.
- **If done facing another direction intentionally:** Major sin.
- **If done unintentionally (without knowledge):** The Adhan/Iqamah is valid (need not be repeated) but is against the Sunnah.

---

### Turning for Hayya 'alas-Salah and Hayya 'alal-Falah

- **Ruling (Sunnah):** Mu'azzin turns to the right when saying "Hayya 'alas-salah" and to the left when saying "Hayya 'alal-falah."
- **Dalil:** Hadith in Abu Dawud.

---

### Adhan and Iqamah for Qada Prayers

- **Single missed salah:** Call both Adhan and Iqamah before making it up.
- **Multiple missed prayers:** Call one Adhan, then a separate Iqamah before each individual prayer.
- **Dalil:** Hadith in Tirmizi from Ghazwat al-Ahzab — the Prophet ﷺ missed multiple prayers and had one Adhan called with multiple Iqamahs, then prayed them all.
- **Practical note:** If making up prayers in a masjid, calling Adhan may cause confusion (people may think it is a current Fard time). In that case, use judgment or do it at home.

---

### Adhan and Iqamah Without Wudu

| Action | Ruling |
|---|---|
| Adhan without Wudu | Not ideal; should be avoided — but not Makruh and Adhan need not be repeated |
| Iqamah without Wudu | **Makruh** — must be in a state of Wudu |

**Reason for stricter ruling on Iqamah:** The Mu'azzin calls Iqamah as the first person announcing readiness for prayer. If he then leaves to make Wudu after calling Iqamah, it causes delay between Iqamah and the start of salah and creates disruption in the masjid.
**Dalil:** Hadith in Tirmizi.
**In both cases:** The Adhan/Iqamah is not repeated.

---

### Adhan Called Before Its Time

- **General ruling:** If Adhan is called before the time of that salah has entered, it must be **repeated** once the time enters — because the entire purpose of Adhan is to call people to a specific prayer at its time.
- **Dalil for repetition:** Hadith in Abu Dawud — Bilal (RA) called Adhan at night (before time), and the Prophet ﷺ commanded him to repeat it.
- **Exception (Imam Abu Yusuf and Imam Shafi'i):** Fajr Adhan may be called before its time — based on the two-Adhan practice in Makkah and Madinah (first Adhan for Tahajjud by Bilal RA, second for Fajr by Abdullah ibn Maktum RA). **Dalil:** Hadith in Bukhari and Tirmizi.
- **Muftabih Hanafi opinion:** Even Fajr Adhan must be repeated if called before time.

---

### Tasweeb in Fajr Adhan

- **Definition:** Adding "al-Salatu khayrun min al-nawm" (Prayer is better than sleep) after "Hayya 'alal-falah" in the Fajr Adhan.
- **Ruling:** Allowed and practiced in Fajr Adhan.
- **Origin:** Bilal (RA) came to wake the Prophet ﷺ for Fajr and found him sleeping. He said this phrase; the Prophet ﷺ approved it and ordered it added to the Fajr Adhan.
- **Dalil:** Hadith in Tabrani and Ibn Majah.

## Conditions & Categories

### Prayers with No Nawafil Allowed After Fard

1. **Fajr** — no Nawafil after its Fard
2. **Asr** — no Nawafil after its Fard
3. **Maghrib time** — no Nafl Jama'ah (since Nawafil must be even-numbered; Maghrib is odd)

### Four Muazzins at the Time of Rasulullah ﷺ

1. Bilal ibn Rabah (RA)
2. Abdullah ibn Maktum (RA) — the blind Sahabi
3. Sa'd al-Qaraz (RA)
4. Abu Mahzura (RA)

### Tiwal al-Mufassal

Surahs from Surah al-Hujurat (49) to the end of the Quran. The Hanafi reasoning for Isfar (delaying Fajr): there should be enough time remaining that if the salah becomes Fasid, these longer Surahs can still be read in a repeated salah before Fajr time expires.

### Prayers for Which There is No Adhan

- Witr
- Eid prayers (Idayn)
- Janazah
- Kusuf / Khusuf (solar/lunar eclipse prayer)
- Istisqa (rain prayer)
- Tarawih
- Any Nafl or Sunnah prayer
- (Exception: Pre-Tahajjud Adhan in Haramayn only)

## Scholarly Opinions

### On Fajr Timing

| Scholar | Opinion | Dalil |
|---|---|---|
| **Ahnaf** | Isfar — delay until daylight spreads | Hadith in Abu Dawud and Tirmizi |
| **Imam Shafi'i** | Ghalas — pray in darkness/very early dawn | Hadith in Bukhari; principle that every salah is best at its earliest |
| **Imam Shafi'i (general)** | All prayers best at their earliest time | Hadith of Abdullah ibn Mas'ud in Tirmizi |

### On Asr Timing

| Scholar | Shadow Length for Asr Start |
|---|---|
| Imam Abu Hanifa (RA) | 2x the object's length |
| Imam Muhammad (RA) & Imam Abu Yusuf (RA) | 1x (aligned with majority) |
| Imam Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali | 1x |

### On Tarjih in Adhan

| Scholar | Opinion |
|---|---|
| Ahnaf | No Tarjih; Adhan at uniform pace and volume |
| Imam Shafi'i | Tarjih is Masnoon |

### On Iqamah Wording

| Scholar | Opinion |
|---|---|
| Ahnaf | Same as Adhan with Taqrar (phrases twice), drawn from Abu Mahzura (RA) |
| Imam Shafi'i | Follows Bilal's Iqamah (no Taqrar — phrases once) |
| Imam Ahmad | Both Adhan and Iqamah follow Bilal (RA) |

### On Abandoning Adhan (Communal Sin)

| Scholar | Ruling |
|---|---|
| Imam Muhammad (RA) | The ruler must fight the people of that city |
| Imam Abu Yusuf (RA) | They should be beaten and jailed |

### On Fajr Adhan Before Its Time

| Scholar | Ruling |
|---|---|
| Ahnaf (Muftabih) | Must be repeated once Fajr time enters |
| Imam Abu Yusuf (RA) | Permissible for Fajr (based on two-Adhan practice of Madinah) |
| Imam Shafi'i | Same as Imam Abu Yusuf — permissible for Fajr |

## Lessons & Practical Application

1. **Respect all four Madhabs.** Every position in the major Madhabs is grounded in Quran and authentic Hadith. Arguing that another Madhab is "wrong" is effectively rejecting the Hadith that madhab relied upon. Learn the Dalil behind each opinion.

2. **Practical Fajr guidance:** Delay Fajr until daylight spreads (Isfar). This gives buffer time: if something invalidates the salah, there is still time to repeat it with Tiwal al-Mufassal Surahs.

3. **Witr discipline:** Train yourself to make Witr the last prayer of the night. If you reliably wake for Tahajjud, delay Witr until then. If not, pray it before sleeping rather than risk missing it.

4. **Asr and Nawafil:** Take advantage of the window before Asr Fard to pray Nawafil. Once Asr Fard is prayed, that window closes. If you delayed Asr to 6 PM but started time was 5 PM, use that extra hour before Asr for Nawafil.

5. **Masjid Iqamah etiquette:** The Mu'azzin (or person calling Iqamah) must be in a state of Wudu before calling it. Leaving after Iqamah to make Wudu disrupts the congregation and is Makruh.

6. **Adhan for Qada:** When making up missed prayers at home, call Adhan once and Iqamah for each prayer individually. This is the Sunnah even for solo Qada.

7. **Masjid timing:** When praying in jama'ah, follow your masjid's timing regardless of madhab. The key principle: there must be no second jama'ah in the same masjid once the first is done — this causes unnecessary fitna between Muslims.

8. **Travel and combining prayers:** The Hanafi Muftabih ruling is not to combine by pulling a prayer forward. Delay Zuhr to near its end, pray Asr at its earliest, with the same Wudu. If this genuinely cannot be done on a flight, the dominant opinion is to make Qada upon arrival. Do not casually adopt another madhab's ruling for convenience.

9. **Upcoming topic — Shurut al-Salah:** The conditions that must be fulfilled before salah is valid. Combined with Arkan al-Salah, these form the 14 Fara'id of salah. Study this carefully as it forms the backbone of prayer validity.
