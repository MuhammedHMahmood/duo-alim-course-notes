## Session Overview

This session covered three main topics: (1) a review and completion of the **Masa'il of 12** (twelve conditions related to Hadath that occur after the Tashahud sitting), (2) the beginning of **Babu Qada al-Fawaait** (the chapter on making up missed prayers) from Mukhtasar al-Quduri (page 95), including the rulings on Tartib and Sahib-i-Tarteeb, and (3) an introduction to the **Makruh times for Salah**, including the three absolute prohibition times and two additional Nawafil-restriction times.

---

## Key Themes

1. **Masa'il of 12:** Conditions arising after the Tashahud sitting that do not directly constitute Hadath but are related to it — the debate between Imam Abu Hanifa (rahimullah) and Sahibain regarding validity of Salah.
2. **Qada Salah and Tartib:** The obligation to make up missed prayers in order and the rules governing when that order (Tartib) is waived.
3. **Sahib-i-Tarteeb:** The status of a person with five or fewer qada prayers upon them and its implications.
4. **Makruh Times for Salah:** The three absolute prohibition times and the two times Nawafil are restricted, with their evidential basis from Hadith.
5. **Foundational Madhab Methodology:** Brief discussion of how the four madhabs apply distinct Usuli principles to arrive at different rulings.

## Fiqh Rulings & Evidence

### A. Masa'il of 12 (Recap)

**The Rule:** If any of these twelve conditions occurs **after** a person has sat for the minimum duration of the Tashahud, then:

- **Imam Abu Hanifa (rahimullah):** The Salah becomes **Batil** (null).
- **Sahibain** (Imam Abu Yusuf and Imam Muhammad, rahimahumullah): The Salah is **complete** and does not need to be repeated.
- **Mufta bihi:** The opinion of **Sahibain** — Salah is valid and complete.

If any of these conditions occurs **before** the Tashahud sitting duration is reached, the Salah is Batil and must be repeated by agreement.

**The Twelve Conditions:**

| # | Condition | Core Reason for Potential Invalidity |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Person praying with Tayammum sees water during Tashahud | Tayammum is invalidated upon sight of usable water (if accessible and sufficient) |
| 2 | Person doing Masa al-Khuffayn — the permitted duration (24 hrs Muqim / 72 hrs Musafir) expires during Tashahud | Masa period expired; Wudu on feet is now lost |
| 3 | Person wearing socks removes them with Amal-e-Kathir during Tashahud | Socks fully removed; must wash feet |
| 4 | An Ummi learns a Surah (e.g., by hearing recitation) during Tashahud | Salah of an Ummi has different conditions; now he is no longer Ummi |
| 5 | Person praying naked (due to having no clothing) finds clothing during Tashahud | Covering the Satr (Awrah) is a condition (Shart) of Salah |
| 6 | Person praying with Ishara (due to illness) regains strength to perform Ruku and Sujud during Tashahud | He is now capable of full physical prayer |
| 7 | Sahib-i-Tarteeb remembers a missed salah during Tashahud | Current Salah may be out of order if Tartib applies |
| 8 | Imam had Hadath during Tashahud and appointed an Ummi as Khalifa | An Ummi cannot lead a congregation in normal Salah |
| 9 | Person praying Fajr — the sun rose during/after the Tashahud sitting | Fajr time ended; sun rising is one of the three absolute Makruh times |
| 10 | Person praying Jum'ah — Asr time began during Tashahud | Jum'ah time (same as Dhuhr) ended |
| 11 | Person doing Masa over a cast/bandage — it fell off during Tashahud and the wound is found healed | The justification for the concession (injury) no longer exists |
| 12 | Disabled person praying with dispensation — disability is removed during Tashahud (analogous to Istihada for women) | The condition that permitted the concession is gone |

> **Note on Condition 8 (Ummi Khalifa):** The reason the Salah is still considered complete is that at the point of the Tashahud, there is no remaining recitation (Qira'ah) or Fard action required from either Imam or Muqtadi — so the invalidity of appointing an Ummi does not affect what has already been completed.

### B. Babu Qada al-Fawaait — Making Up Missed Prayers

**Text of Imam Quduri (rahimullah):**
> وَمَن فَاتَتُهُ صَلَاةٌ قَضَاهَا إِذَا ذَكَرَهَا وَقَدَّمَهَا عَلَى صَلَاةِ الْوَقْتِ إِلَّا أَن يَخَافَ فَوْتَ صَلَاةِ الْوَقْتِ فَيُقَدِّمُ صَلَاةَ الْوَقْتِ عَلَى الْفَائِتَةِ ثُمَّ يَقْضِيهَا

**Translation:** "Whoever has a missed prayer should make it up when he remembers it and pray it before the current time's prayer — except if he fears the current prayer will also be missed, in which case he prays the current prayer first, then makes up the missed one."

**Ruling 1 — Immediate Qada upon remembrance:**
A missed Fard Salah remains obligatory upon the person. It does not expire. He must pray it as soon as he remembers.

**Dalil:**
- **Hadith (Bukhari, narrated by Anas رضي الله عنه):** The Prophet ﷺ said: *"If anyone forgets a prayer, he should pray that prayer when he remembers it. There is no expiation except to pray the same."*
- **Quran (Surah Taha, Ayah 14):** *"Establish prayer for My remembrance."*

**Ruling 2 — Tartib (Sequential Order):**
> وَمَن فَاتَتُهُ صَلَوَاتٌ رَتَّبَهَا فِي الْقَضَاءِ كَمَا وَجَبَتْ فِي الْأَصْلِ

A person who has multiple missed prayers must make them up **in the same order** they were originally obligatory. Example: if Fajr, Dhuhr, and Asr are missed, he prays Fajr → Dhuhr → Asr → then the current prayer.

- **Ahnaf position:** Tartib is **Wajib** for a Sahib-i-Tarteeb.
- **Shafi'i position:** Tartib is only **Mustahab** (recommended), not binding.

**Consequence of breaking Tartib (Ahnaf):** If a Sahib-i-Tarteeb had enough time and awareness and prayed the current Salah before his qada, he must do **I'ada** (repeat) of the current Salah after praying the qada.

**Dalil for obligation of Tartib:** The Prophet ﷺ used the **Sigha of Amr** (command form) in the Hadith above, which in Usul al-Fiqh establishes obligation. *(Also supported by a narration in Baihaqi.)*

**Ruling 3 — Kasrat al-Qada (Tartib drops when prayers exceed five):**
> إِلَّا أَن تَزِيدَ الْفَوَائِتُ عَلَى خَمْسِ صَلَوَاتٍ فَيَسْقُطُ التَّرْتِيبُ فِيهَا

When the number of missed prayers **exceeds five**, Tartib drops. The person is no longer Sahib-i-Tarteeb. He may pray in any order and does not need to perform qada before the current Salah.

- **Mufta bihi:** Tartib drops when the **sixth salah becomes qada** (after the sixth prayer's time expires) — not at the moment the sixth prayer's time enters (which was the second narration from Imam Muhammad in Al-Jami').

**Ruling 4 — Tartib returns when count falls back to five or below:**
If a person had six qada prayers and prays any one of them, bringing the count to five, **Tartib immediately returns**. He is again a Sahib-i-Tarteeb.

### C. Makruh Times for Salah

**Three Absolute Prohibition Times** — No Salah of any kind is permitted:

| # | Time | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | طلوع الشمس (Tulu' al-Shams) | The exact period of sunrise — from when the sun begins to rise until it is fully above the horizon |
| 2 | غروب الشمس (Ghurub al-Shams) | The exact period of sunset — from when the sun begins to set until it completely disappears |
| 3 | الاستواء (Istiwa' / Zawal) | When the sun is exactly at its zenith (midday peak), until it passes the meridian |

At these three times, the following are **all prohibited:**
- Fard Salah, Nafil Salah, Qada Salah
- Salat al-Janazah
- Sajda-e-Tilawat

**Exception (Imam Abu Yusuf rahimullah):** Salat al-Jum'ah may be **begun** at the exact moment of Zawal — since Jum'ah time begins at Zawal.

**Dalil (Hadith in Muslim, narrated by Uqba bin Amir رضي الله عنه):**
> "There were three times at which the Messenger of Allah ﷺ forbade us to pray or bury our dead: when the sun begins to rise till it is fully up; when the sun is at its height at midday till it passes over the meridian; and when the sun draws near to setting till it completely sets."

**Two Additional Times — Nawafil Restricted (not an absolute prohibition):**

| # | Time | What is Prohibited | What is Permitted |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | After Fajr Salah until sunrise | Nawafil | Salat al-Janazah, Sajda-e-Tilawat, Qada Salah |
| 2 | After Asr Salah until sunset | Nawafil | Salat al-Janazah, Sajda-e-Tilawat, Qada Salah |

> **Note:** The two Rak'at Sunnah of Fajr (before the Fard) may still be prayed after Subh Sadiq. Nawafil before Maghrib Fard are also Makruh in the Hanafi madhab (though permitted in other madhabs). The proper action when Maghrib time enters is to immediately pray the Fard Salah.

**Dalil for restriction of Sajda-e-Tilawat (Abu Dawood, narrated by Abu Tamima al-Hujaymi رضي الله عنه):**
> "Ibn Umar رضي الله عنه prohibited me from prostrating after the dawn prayer, saying: I prayed behind the Prophet ﷺ, Abu Bakr, Umar, and Uthman, and they would not prostrate on account of recitation of the Quran until the sun had completely risen."

**Asr Salah — Special Rule:**
Asr remains valid to pray until sunset. If a person begins his Asr Salah before sunset, he may complete it even if the sun sets mid-prayer.

**Dalil (Bukhari):** *"Whoever could get one Rak'ah of Asr prayer before sunset, he has got the Asr prayer."*

## Conditions & Categories

### Conditions for Tayammum-related Invalidation (Masa'ila 1)
For Tayammum to be invalidated upon seeing water, the following conditions apply:
1. The water must be **sufficient** for Wudu (a single glass is insufficient).
2. The person must **not be extremely thirsty** (thirst quenching takes priority if water is limited).
3. The person must be **physically able to reach** the water (no danger, enemies, animals, or impassable terrain blocking access).

### Sahib-i-Tarteeb — Definition and Status
| Category | Definition | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| **Sahib-i-Tarteeb** | Has 0–5 qada prayers upon them | Tartib is **Wajib** — must pray qada in order before current Salah |
| **Not Sahib-i-Tarteeb** | Has 6 or more qada prayers upon them | Tartib is **Saaqit** (dropped) — may pray in any order |

### Three Conditions Permitting Current Salah Before Qada
Even for a Sahib-i-Tarteeb, the current Salah takes priority if **any one** of these is present:

| # | Condition | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Insufficient time (Qillat al-Waqt)** | Praying qada first would cause the current Salah to also become qada |
| 2 | **Forgetfulness (Nisyan)** | He genuinely forgot he had a qada due — no I'ada needed even if he prayed current Salah first |
| 3 | **Abundance of missed prayers (Kasrat-i-Qada)** | When qada prayers exceed five, Tartib drops entirely |

### Unconscious Person — Qada Obligation
| Duration of Unconsciousness | Qada Ruling |
|---|---|
| 5 prayers or fewer | **Qada is obligatory** upon regaining consciousness |
| 6 prayers or more | **No Qada** — the obligation is waived |

> **Dalil:** Ibn Umar رضي الله عنه was unconscious for more than one day and night and did not make qada. Ali رضي الله عنه and Ammar bin Yasir رضي الله عنه were unconscious for less than a day and night and did make qada.

**Important nuance:** If a person repeatedly regains and loses consciousness, the benefit of waiver only applies to periods of **continuous** unconsciousness. Each time consciousness is fully regained, the qada obligation restarts.

## Scholarly Opinions

### On Masa'il of 12

| Position | Scholar(s) | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| Salah is **Batil** | Imam Abu Hanifa (rahimullah) | These conditions after Tashahud still nullify the Salah |
| Salah is **complete** | Sahibain — Imam Abu Yusuf & Imam Muhammad (rahimahumullah) | If Tashahud duration completed, Salah is valid |
| **Mufta bihi** | Sahibain | Operative opinion in the Hanafi madhab |

### On Tartib in Qada

| Position | Scholar(s) | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| **Wajib** | Ahnaf (Hanafi madhab) | Tartib is obligatory for Sahib-i-Tarteeb; breaking it requires I'ada |
| **Mustahab** | Shafi'i madhab | Tartib is recommended but not binding; no I'ada required if broken |

### On the Threshold for Tartib Dropping (Ahnaf — internal difference)

| Position | Scholar | When Tartib drops |
|---|---|---|
| **First narration (Mufta bihi)** | Imam Muhammad (rahimullah) in Al-Jami' | After the **sixth Salah's time expires** (6 are fully qada) |
| Second narration | Imam Muhammad (rahimullah) | When the **time of the sixth Salah enters** (even before it becomes qada) |

### On Makruh Times for Salah

| Position | Scholar(s) | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| Absolute prohibition in three times; Nawafil restricted after Fajr/Asr | **Ahnaf** | No Salah (Fard or Nafil) in three times; no Nawafil after Fajr or before Maghrib |
| Fard Salah may be prayed at any time; Nawafil for Tawaf in Makkah also permitted | **Imam Shafi'i (rahimullah)** | Based on different Hadith (Nasa'i) |
| Jum'ah may begin at exact moment of Zawal | **Imam Abu Yusuf (rahimullah)** | Sole exception within Ahnaf at the Zawal time |

### On Doubt About Wudu Mid-Salah

| Scenario | Ruling (Ahnaf, from Radd al-Muhtar) |
|---|---|
| First time it occurs | Break Salah, make Wudu, restart |
| Recurring habit (Waswasa) | Continue Salah; consider oneself in state of Wudu. This is from Shaytan and should be dismissed |

## Lessons & Practical Application

1. **Missed Salah is never "expired" — it remains a debt.** Imam Quduri (rahimullah) deliberately used the word *Fawait* (circumstantially missed) rather than *Matruk* (willfully abandoned) to reflect that a Muslim cannot knowingly abandon Salah. This choice of wording is a legal and moral statement: make up your Salah as soon as possible.

2. **Tartib is not a technicality — it is Wajib for Sahib-i-Tarteeb.** If you are Hanafi and have five or fewer missed prayers, you must pray them in order before the current prayer. If you knowingly pray Dhuhr while you have a missed Fajr (with time to spare), you must repeat Dhuhr after making up Fajr.

3. **The five-Salah threshold is practical — know where you stand.** Keeping track of your qada count matters. Once you cross five, Tartib drops and you gain flexibility. Once you bring it back to five by praying any qada, Tartib returns. This creates an incentive to steadily reduce your qada backlog.

4. **The three Makruh times are clear-cut prohibitions backed by explicit Hadith.** Makruh Tahrimi — treat it as Haram in practice for Ibadat. Do not start a Salah, offer Janazah, or make Sajda-e-Tilawat during sunrise, sunset, or midday zenith.

5. **After Fajr and Asr, do not pray Nawafil — but Qada is fine.** This is a common practical situation: the time after Fajr before sunrise is not "free time for Nawafil" in the Hanafi madhab. Use it for dhikr, or if you have qada prayers, this is a valid time to pray them.

6. **If you start Asr before sunset, complete it.** Even if the sun begins to set as you pray, continue. The Salah is valid. The Makruh time only prevents *starting* in the danger zone, not completing a Salah already begun.

7. **Waswasa (doubtful whispers about Wudu) should be resisted.** The ruling is clear: first doubt — act on it. Recurring doubt — dismiss it as Shaytan and continue. Treating every whisper as real would make Salah impossible and was never the intent of the Shari'ah.

8. **The four madhabs derive their differences from Usuli principles, not arbitrary disagreement.** When Ahnaf treat something as Wajib (e.g., Tartib) and Shafi'is treat it as Mustahab, the difference reflects different approaches to weighing Hadith, Quranic verses, and the actions of the Sahabah. All four are grounded in Quran, Sunnah, and the actions of the Companions — they are all valid scholarly positions arrived at through rigorous Ijtihad.

9. **Roadmap for deeper study:** Quduri → Hidayah (deeper dalail) → Zahir al-Riwayah (e.g., Bada'i al-Sana'i) → Bidayat al-Mujtahid (comparative four-madhab analysis) → Usul al-Shashi (Hanafi Usul al-Fiqh foundations).
