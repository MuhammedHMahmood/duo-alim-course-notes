## Session Overview

This session covered the i'rab of ism Kana and its sisters, khabar Inna and its sisters, Ma and La (Mushabbeha to Laisa), and La Nafil Jins. A significant portion was devoted to the special Mubtada formed from a Siga Sifat that comes after Ma Nafiya or Istifham and gives Rafa to an Ism Zahir — and how to distinguish it from Khabar Muqaddam. The session concluded with a comparative analysis of the two types of La: the one that negates the entire genus (Nafil Jins) versus the one that resembles Laisa.

---

## Grammar Rules Covered

### 1. Ism of Kana and Sisters → Marfu'

- The أفعال ناقصة (incomplete verbs) — كان and its sisters — operate upon a Mubtada-Khabar structure.
- They give a portion of that structure a new grammatical role: the Mubtada becomes **Ism Kana** and remains **Marfu'**.
- Example: **وَكَانَ اللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرًا** — اللَّهُ is Ism Kana → Marfu'.

### 2. Khabar of Inna and Sisters → Marfu'

- The sisters of Inna operate in reverse: they make the Mubtada Mansoob (Ism Inna) and the Khabar remains **Marfu'**.
- Example: **إِنَّ اللَّهَ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٌ** — سَمِيعٌ is Khabar Inna → Marfu'.
- Example: **إِنَّ اللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٌ** — حَكِيمٌ is Khabar Inna → Marfu'.

### 3. Khabar Muqaddam (Khabar Preceding Ism)

- For Kana and its sisters (the first nine أفعال ناقصة), it is **permissible** to advance the Khabar over the Ism:
  - Example: **كَانَ قَائِمًا زَيْدٌ** — Khabar (قَائِمًا) precedes Ism (زَيْدٌ).
- For أفعال ناقصة that begin with **ما** (e.g., مَا زَالَ، مَا دَامَ), the Khabar **cannot** be made Muqaddam.
  - Wrong: قَائِمًا مَا زَالَ زَيْدٌ — this is not permissible.
- For **لَيْسَ**, there is **Ikhtilaf** (scholarly disagreement): some grammarians permit the Khabar to precede it, others do not.

### 4. Ma and La Mushabbeha to Laisa

- **ما** and **لا** (in their Mushabbeha form) function like لَيْسَ:
  - They come upon a Jumlah Ismiya (Mubtada-Khabar structure).
  - They make the Mubtada **Marfu'** (as Ism) and the Khabar **Mansoob**.
- The basis of their resemblance to Laisa is twofold:
  1. They **negate** like Laisa negates.
  2. They operate upon a Jumlah Ismiya and affect the i'rab of its components.
- **ما** can come with both **Ma'rifa** and **Nakira**.
- **لا** (Mushabbeha) comes **only with Nakira**.
- Quranic examples:
  - **مَا هَٰذَا بَشَرًا** — هَٰذَا is Ism Ma (Marfu'), بَشَرًا is Khabar (Mansoob).
  - **وَمَا رَبُّكَ بِظَلَّامٍ لِّلْعَبِيدِ** — رَبُّكَ is Ism Ma (Marfu').

### 5. The Special Mubtada from Siga Sifat (After Nafi / Istifham)

This is a special provision where a word that appears to be Musnad (Khabar) actually functions as **Mubtada**, and what appears to be Musnad Ilayh (Mubtada) is actually **Khabar**.

**Three conditions must all be met:**
1. The word is a **Siga Sifat** — i.e., اسم فاعل، اسم مفعول، صفة مشبهة، or اسم تفضيل.
2. It comes **after ما نافية or Istifham** (e.g., هل).
3. It gives **Rafa to an Ism Zahir** (i.e., it is the عامل that raised the following explicit noun).

**When all three conditions are met:** the Sifat is Mubtada (special type), and the Ism Zahir it raised is Khabar.

**Key diagnostic test — agreement (مطابقة):**
- If the Sifat **matches** the following noun (in number/gender), then it is **NOT doing Amal** — it is **Khabar Muqaddam**, and the following noun is **Mubtada Mu'akhkhar**.
- If the Sifat is **Mufrad (singular)** and does **not match** the following noun (e.g., the noun is Tasniya), then it **IS doing Amal** (giving Rafa) — it is the **special Mubtada**, and the following noun is the **Khabar**.

This parallels the rule that an عامل does not match its مَعْمُول — just as we say ذَهَبَ الزَّيْدَانِ (not ذَهَبَا الزَّيْدَانِ), an active Sifat remains Mufrad.

### 6. La Nafil Jins vs. La Mushabbeha to Laisa

| Feature | لا Nafil Jins | لا Mushabbeha to Laisa |
|---|---|---|
| Ism | Mabni ala al-Fath (Fathah, no tanwin) | Marfu' (Damma/tanwin) |
| Scope of negation | Negates the **entire genus** (كل الجنس) | Negates **one individual** of the genus |
| Can Bal follow? | No — denying the whole genus, Bal is impossible | Yes — Bal + an alternative is valid |
| Example | لَا طَالِبَةَ فِي الْفَصْلِ (no student whatsoever) | لَا طَالِبٌ فِي الْفَصْلِ بَلْ طَالِبَانِ (one student absent, two are there) |

- Note: لَا رَجُلٌ أَفْضَلَ مِنْكَ (with Rafa) can carry either meaning depending on context, but لَا رَجُلَ أَفْضَلَ مِنْكَ (with Fathah, Nafil Jins) strictly means no man at all is better than you.

## I'rab (Sentence Analysis) Examples

### Example 1: كَانَ قَائِمًا زَيْدٌ
- **كَانَ** — فعل ناقص ماض
- **قَائِمًا** — خَبَرُ كَانَ، مُقَدَّمٌ، مَنْصُوبٌ
- **زَيْدٌ** — إِسْمُ كَانَ، مُؤَخَّرٌ، مَرْفُوعٌ

### Example 2: إِنَّ اللَّهَ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٌ
- **إِنَّ** — حرف توكيد ونصب
- **اللَّهَ** — إِسْمُ إِنَّ، مَنْصُوبٌ
- **سَمِيعٌ** — خَبَرُ إِنَّ، مَرْفُوعٌ
- **عَلِيمٌ** — خَبَرٌ ثَانٍ، مَرْفُوعٌ

### Example 3: مَا هَٰذَا بَشَرًا
- **مَا** — حرف نفي يعمل عمل ليس
- **هَٰذَا** — إِسْمُ مَا، مَرْفُوعٌ
- **بَشَرًا** — خَبَرُ مَا، مَنْصُوبٌ

### Example 4: مَا صَائِمٌ الزَّيْدَانِ (Special Mubtada case)
- **مَا** — نافية
- **صَائِمٌ** — مُبْتَدَأٌ (special type — Siga Sifat, after Ma Nafiya, Mufrad = giving Rafa to Zahir), مَرْفُوعٌ
- **الزَّيْدَانِ** — خَبَرٌ مَرْفُوعٌ (raised by صَائِمٌ acting as Amil)

### Example 5: مَا صَائِمَانِ الزَّيْدَانِ (Khabar Muqaddam case)
- **مَا** — نافية
- **صَائِمَانِ** — خَبَرٌ مُقَدَّمٌ، مَرْفُوعٌ (matches الزَّيْدَانِ in number → not Amil → Khabar)
- **الزَّيْدَانِ** — مُبْتَدَأٌ مُؤَخَّرٌ، مَرْفُوعٌ

### Example 6: لَا طَالِبَةَ فِي الْفَصْلِ (Nafil Jins)
- **لَا** — لا النافية للجنس
- **طَالِبَةَ** — إِسْمُ لَا، مَبْنِيٌّ عَلَى الْفَتْحِ (negates the entire genus: "no student whatsoever")
- **فِي الْفَصْلِ** — خَبَرُ لَا، جَارٌّ وَمَجْرُورٌ

### Example 7: لَا طَالِبٌ فِي الْفَصْلِ بَلْ طَالِبَانِ (La Mushabbeha)
- **لَا** — مُشَابِهَةٌ لَـ"لَيْسَ"
- **طَالِبٌ** — إِسْمُ لَا، مَرْفُوعٌ (negates one individual, not the whole genus — Bal is valid)
- **فِي الْفَصْلِ** — خَبَرُ لَا

## Practice Examples

**Identifying the type of La:**

1. لَا رَجُلَ أَفْضَلَ مِنْكَ
   - Fathah on رَجُلَ → La Nafil Jins → "No man at all is better than you."

2. لَا رَجُلٌ أَفْضَلَ مِنْكَ
   - Damma on رَجُلٌ → La Mushabbeha → "One (specific) man is not better than you."

**Applying the Siga Sifat diagnostic:**

3. هَلْ جَالِسٌ الزَّيْدَانِ؟
   - جَالِسٌ = Mufrad, الزَّيْدَانِ = Tasniya → no agreement → جَالِسٌ is Amil (giving Rafa) → جَالِسٌ is **Mubtada**, الزَّيْدَانِ is **Khabar**.

4. هَلْ جَالِسَانِ الزَّيْدَانِ؟
   - جَالِسَانِ matches الزَّيْدَانِ (both Tasniya) → not Amil → جَالِسَانِ is **Khabar Muqaddam**, الزَّيْدَانِ is **Mubtada Mu'akhkhar**.

**Checking Khabar Muqaddam permissibility:**

5. قَائِمًا مَا زَالَ زَيْدٌ — **Incorrect.** Khabar cannot be Muqaddam over a فعل ناقص that begins with ما.

6. كَانَ قَائِمًا زَيْدٌ — **Correct.** Khabar Muqaddam is permitted for the first nine أفعال ناقصة.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Ism Kana and ism of its sisters are always Marfu'; khabar of Inna and its sisters are always Marfu'** — these are the two symmetrical rules governing the two major groups of أفعال ناقصة and Inna's sisters.

2. **Khabar Muqaddam is permitted for the first nine أفعال ناقصة** but is **prohibited for those beginning with ما** (مَا زَالَ، مَا دَامَ etc.). The ruling on Laisa is a matter of scholarly disagreement.

3. **ما and لا Mushabbeha to Laisa** function like Laisa: Mubtada stays Marfu' (Ism), Khabar becomes Mansoob. ما takes both Ma'rifa and Nakira; لا Mushabbeha takes only Nakira.

4. **The special Mubtada rule** (Siga Sifat after Nafi/Istifham that gives Rafa to Ism Zahir) is a grammatical exception where what looks like a Musnad (Khabar) is actually Mubtada. The key diagnostic is **agreement**: if the Sifat is Mufrad and the following Ism is Tasniya/Jama', the Sifat is the Amil and therefore the special Mubtada. If the Sifat matches in number, it is Khabar Muqaddam.

5. **لا Nafil Jins (Mabni ala Fath) negates the entire genus** — Bal cannot follow it. **لا Mushabbeha (Marfu' ism) negates one individual** — Bal can follow it to name an alternative. Context determines which reading applies when the sentence is ambiguous.

6. The principle that **an عامل does not match its مَعْمُول** (as in ذَهَبَ الزَّيْدَانِ, not ذَهَبَا) is the same principle that explains why the special Mubtada Sifat is always Mufrad when actively governing the Ism Zahir.
