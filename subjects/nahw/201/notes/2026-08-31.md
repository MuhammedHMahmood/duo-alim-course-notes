# Nahw 201 - Study Notes
**Date:** 2026-08-31

---

## Session Overview

This session covered the chapter on **Al-Ism Al-Munsarif wa Ghayru Munsarif** (الاسم المنصرف وغير المنصرف) — the fully-declinable noun versus the partially-declinable noun. The teacher introduced the definitions and grammatical rulings (hukm) of each type, then examined two of the nine asbab (causes) that render a noun Ghair Munsarif in detail: **Adl** (عدل) and **Wasf** (وصف). For Adl, both sub-types (Tahqiqi and Taqdiri) were explained with examples. For Wasf, the critical distinction between Wasf Asli and Wasf Ardi was established.

---

## Grammar Rules Covered

### 1. Munsarif (منصرف) — The Fully Declinable Noun

**Definition:** A noun that does **not** have two of the nine asbab (or one sabab that equals two). It "turns" freely to all three harakaat.

- Also called **Ism Mutamakkin** (اسم متمكن) — the "established/spacious" noun, because it gives room to all harakaat.
- **Hukm:** Accepts all three harakaat (Damma, Fatha, Kasra) **along with Tanwin**.
- Example: **زيد** (Zayd) — no two asbab found; fully declinable.

---

### 2. Ghair Munsarif (غير منصرف) — The Partially Declinable Noun

**Definition:** A noun in which **two of the nine asbab** are present, OR **one sabab that is so strong it counts as two**.

- **Hukm:** Does **not** accept two things:
  1. **Kasra** (كسرة) — Fatha substitutes for it in the Jarr position.
  2. **Tanwin** (تنوين) — dropped entirely.

**Demonstration with أحمد (Ahmad):**

| Case | Correct | Wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Raf' | جَاءَنِي أَحْمَدُ | ~~أَحْمَدٌ~~ |
| Nasb | رَأَيْتُ أَحْمَدَ | ~~أَحْمَدًا~~ |
| Jarr | مَرَرْتُ بِأَحْمَدَ | ~~بِأَحْمَدٍ~~ |

Note: In the Jarr position, Fatha (أحمدَ) replaces the expected Kasra.

---

### 3. The Nine Asbab (الأسباب التسعة)

A noun becomes Ghair Munsarif when two of the following are present. Covered in this session: **Adl** and **Wasf**. The full nine include: Alamiyya, Ta'nith, Ajam, Jam', Tarkeeb, Alif-Nun Zaidatan, Wazn Fi'l, Wasf, and Adl.

---

### 4. Adl (عدل) — Deviation from Original Pattern

**Definition:** The change of a noun from its **original sigha (pattern/form)** to a different sigha, whether that change is verified by evidence (Tahqiqi) or merely assumed (Taqdiri).

> أَمَّا الْعَدْلُ فَهُوَ تَغَيُّرُ اللَّفْظِ مِنْ صِيغَتِهِ الْأَصْلِيَّةِ إِلَى صِيغَةٍ أُخْرَى تَحْقِيقًا أَوْ تَقْدِيرًا

**Critical rule:** Adl does **not** combine with **Wazn Fi'l** as its paired second sabab. When Adl is present, the second sabab must be found among the remaining seven asbab.

- Adl pairs with **Alamiyya** → e.g., عُمَر, زُفَر
- Adl pairs with **Wasf** → e.g., ثُلَاث, مَثْلَث, أُخَر

#### A. Adl Tahqiqi (عدل تحقيقي) — Verified Deviation

There IS a dalil (evidence) pointing to the original form. The meaning itself serves as the proof.

**Example: ثُلَاث / مَثْلَث (Sulas / Maslas)**
- Meaning: "three, three" / "three, three" — repetition in the meaning.
- Rule: Repetition in meaning indicates repetition in the original word form.
- Therefore: the original form was **سَلَاسَةٌ سَلَاسَةٌ** (Salasatun Salasatun).
- The word underwent internal transformation → became ثُلَاث / مَثْلَث.
- Two asbab: **Wasf + Adl Tahqiqi** → Ghair Munsarif.

#### B. Adl Taqdiri (عدل تقديري) — Assumed Deviation

There is **no dalil**. Grammarians could not find the original form, so they **assumed** (qaddara) one in order to account for why Arabs read the word as Ghair Munsarif.

**Example: عُمَر (Umar)**
- Arabs read it as Ghair Munsarif.
- No two apparent asbab visible.
- Only Alamiyya (one sabab) was found.
- Grammarians assumed: the original form was **عَامِر** (Aamir) → it deviated to **عُمَر**.
- This assumption (taqdeer) of Adl supplies the missing second sabab.
- Two asbab: **Alam + Adl Taqdiri** → Ghair Munsarif.

**Example: زُفَر (Zufar)**
- Assumed original: **زَافِر** (Zafir).
- Two asbab: **Alam + Adl Taqdiri** → Ghair Munsarif.

**How many words fall under Adl Taqdiri?** Only **15 words** total — extremely rare. Most are found only in very ancient Arabic texts. The full list:

عُمَر، زُفَر، زُحَل، دُشَم، جُمَح، قُزَح، ذُلَف، جُهَر، بُلَع، مُضَر، هُبَل، هُزَل، كُثَم (+ 2 others)

Students are not required to memorize all 15 — awareness of the category and its common examples (Umar, Zufar) is sufficient for most study contexts.

### 5. Wasf (وصف) — Adjectival Origin

**Definition:** A noun that was **created/designed from the outset to describe** something (i.e., a true adjective by original designation).

> وَشَرْطُهُ أَنْ يَكُونَ وَصْفًا فِي أَصْلِ الْوَضْعِ

**Critical rule:** Wasf as a sabab **never combines with Alamiyya (Alam)**. These two asbab are mutually exclusive.

> أَمَّا الْوَصْفُ فَلَا يَجْتَمِعُ مَعَ الْعَلَمِيَّةِ أَصْلًا

#### Wasf Asli (وصف أصلي) — Permanent/Original Descriptor

- The word was **born as a descriptor** and remains so whether it stands alone or inside a sentence.
- Even if the word later begins to be used as a noun (Ism), its original Wasf nature is not erased.
- **"Once Wasf, forever Wasf"** — the rule of Asliyyat.
- Examples: **أَحْمَر** (red), **أَخْضَر** (green), **أَسْوَد** (black), **أَرْقَم** (speckled)

**Example: أَسْوَد / أَرْقَم (Aswad / Arqam)**
- Originally descriptors for color/markings.
- Later became **proper names for types of black snakes** — i.e., Ismiyyat became dominant.
- Despite this, their **Wasf Asliyya** is preserved → they remain Ghair Munsarif.
- Two asbab: **Wasf (Asli) + [second sabab]** → Ghair Munsarif.
- The shift from Wasf to Ism usage does not remove the original Wasf quality.

#### Wasf Ardi (وصف عرضي) — Incidental/Temporary Descriptor

- A word that functions as a descriptor **only within a specific syntactic structure (tarkeeb)**.
- Remove it from that structure and it is **no longer a Wasf** — it reverts to being a plain noun or number.
- This does **not** qualify as the Wasf sabab for Ghair Munsarif.

**Example: أَرْبَع (Arba') in مَرَرْتُ بِنِسْوَةٍ أَرْبَعٍ**
- Inside this sentence: Arba' is a Sifa for Niswah → Wasf Ardi.
- Standalone: Arba' is simply a number, not a Wasf.
- Because it is Wasf Ardi (not Asli), **Arba' is Munsarif** here (takes Tanwin: أَرْبَعٍ).

## I'rab (Sentence Analysis) Examples

### Example 1 — Ghair Munsarif in all three positions (أحمد — Ahmad)

| Position | Sentence | I'rab | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raf' | جَاءَنِي أَحْمَدُ | Fa'il, Marfu', Damma | No Tanwin |
| Nasb | رَأَيْتُ أَحْمَدَ | Maf'ul bihi, Mansub, Fatha | No Tanwin |
| Jarr | مَرَرْتُ بِأَحْمَدَ | Majrur, Fatha **instead of Kasra** | Fatah substitutes for Kasra; no Tanwin |

### Example 2 — Munsarif (زيد — Zayd)

| Position | Sentence | I'rab | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raf' | جَاءَ زَيْدٌ | Fa'il, Marfu', Damma + Tanwin | Fully declines |
| Nasb | رَأَيْتُ زَيْدًا | Maf'ul bihi, Mansub, Fatha + Tanwin | Tanwin present |
| Jarr | مَرَرْتُ بِزَيْدٍ | Majrur, Kasra + Tanwin | Accepts Kasra |

### Example 3 — Wasf Ardi vs. Asli (أربع — Arba')

| Context | Form | Reading | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| مَرَرْتُ بِنِسْوَةٍ أَرْبَعٍ | Sifa for Niswah | أَرْبَعٍ (with Tanwin + Kasra) | Wasf Ardi → Munsarif |
| أَرْبَع alone (a number) | Independent noun | أَرْبَعٌ / أَرْبَعٍ | Not Wasf at all → Munsarif |

## Key Takeaways

1. **Munsarif vs. Ghair Munsarif** is determined by the presence or absence of **two of the nine asbab** (or one sabab so strong it counts as two). Munsarif = no two asbab found; Ghair Munsarif = two asbab (or one equivalent) found.

2. **The hukm of Ghair Munsarif** is the rejection of **Kasra** and **Tanwin**. Fatha substitutes for Kasra in the Jarr position. This is a firm rule with no exceptions within standard usage.

3. **Adl Tahqiqi** is provable — the meaning provides the dalil (as with ثُلَاث: repetition in meaning proves repetition in the original form سَلَاسَةٌ سَلَاسَةٌ).

4. **Adl Taqdiri** is an assumed designation — grammarians invented the Adl sabab to explain why Arabs read certain words (like عُمَر) as Ghair Munsarif when no other two asbab were visible. Only 15 such words exist.

5. **Adl never pairs with Wazn Fi'l** as its second sabab. When looking for the second sabab to accompany Adl, Wazn Fi'l is excluded. Search among the remaining seven.

6. **Wasf must be Asli (original/permanent) to function as a sabab.** Wasf Ardi — adjective function that only applies inside a particular sentence structure — does not count. A word that began life as a descriptor retains that Wasf Asliyya permanently, even if it later shifts to common use as a noun (e.g., أَسْوَد becoming a name for black snakes).

7. **Wasf and Alamiyya never co-occur** as the two asbab for Ghair Munsarif. Wasf pairs with other asbab (like Adl), not with Alam.

8. **Arabs (ahl al-lisan) are the ultimate authority.** In cases like عُمَر, the reason for Ghair Munsarif was not initially known — grammarians worked backward from Arab usage to construct a logical account. We follow the Arabs; the grammatical analysis serves to explain, not prescribe.
