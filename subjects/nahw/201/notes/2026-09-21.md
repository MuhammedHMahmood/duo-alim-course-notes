## Grammar Rules Covered

### Rule 1: Fa'il Must Always Be Wahid (Singular) When It Is Mu'akhkhar

When the **fa'il (subject/doer)** comes **after** the verb (i.e., it is **mu'akhkhar**), the verb must be in the **singular (wahid)** form — regardless of whether the fa'il is dual (tasniya) or plural (jama').

- The verb does not change for number when fa'il follows it.
- The verb does change for **gender**: if fa'il is mu'annas, verb is mu'annas wahid; if fa'il is mudhakkar, verb is mudhakkar wahid.

**Examples:**
| Sentence | Correct? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| ذَهَبَتِ الطَّالِبَاتُ | ✅ Correct | Fa'il is jama' mu'annas, verb is wahid mu'annas |
| ذَهَبْنَ الطَّالِبَاتُ | ❌ Wrong | Verb must be wahid when fa'il is mu'akhkhar |
| دَخَلَتَا الْبِنْتَانِ الْمَسْجِدَ | ✅ Correct | Fa'il is tasniya, verb matches in tasniya |

> **Note:** The case of al-Bintani dakhalata al-masjida was confirmed correct because the verb matches the dual subject — this reflects the rule that the verb takes the tasniya form when fa'il is dual.

---

### Rule 2: Fa'il Must Be Wahid When Muqaddam — Clarification

When **fa'il is muqaddam** (placed before the verb in an unusual structure), the verb **must still be wahid**. This is **not optional** — it is obligatory (wajib).

- Wahid mudhakkar or wahid mu'annas depends on the gender of the fa'il.
- The fa'il being jama' or tasniya does not change the verb to match in those cases where the fa'il precedes.

---

### Rule 3: Jama' Taksir (Broken Plural) Is Treated Like Mu'annas Ghair Haqiqi

**Text referenced in class:**
> وَجَمْعُ التَّكْسِيرِ كَالْمُؤَنَّثِ الْغَيْرِ الْحَقِيقِيِّ

Jama' Taksir (broken plural) is treated like **non-real feminine (mu'annas ghair haqiqi)**. This means you may treat it as singular feminine, and both forms are acceptable:

| Example | Acceptability |
|---|---|
| قَامَ الرِّجَالُ | ✅ Correct (treated as mudhakkar jama') |
| قَامَتِ الرِّجَالُ | ✅ Correct (treated as mu'annas, per the rule) |
| نَامَتِ الْأَوْلَادُ | ✅ Correct |
| الطُّلَّابُ أَسْلَمَتْ | ✅ Correct |
| الطُّلَّابُ أَسْلَمُوا | ✅ Correct |

#### Critical Distinction: 'Aqil vs. Ghair 'Aqil in Jama' Taksir

| Type | Ruling |
|---|---|
| Jama' Taksir **Ghair 'Aqil** (non-living/non-rational) — e.g. الْكُتُبُ | **Lazim (obligatory)** to treat as mu'annas singular. No choice. |
| Jama' Taksir **'Aqil** (living/rational) — e.g. الْأَوْلَادُ، الرِّجَالُ | **Optional (ikhtiyari)** — may treat as mu'annas or as jama' mudhakkar |

**Example:**
- الْكُتُبُ جَمِيلَةٌ — must use feminine singular (هَذِهِ الْكُتُبُ جَمِيلَةٌ), not optional
- نَامَتِ الْأَوْلَادُ — acceptable; نَامَ الْأَوْلَادُ — also acceptable

### Rule 4: Fasl (Separation) Between Fi'l and Fa'il Allows Mudhakkar Verb for Mu'annas Fa'il

When any word comes **between the verb and its fa'il**, the verb may be **muzakkar** even if the fa'il is mu'annas.

**Example:**
- أَسْلَمَ الْيَوْمَ سَارَةُ ✅ — الْيَوْمَ (today) is a fasl between the verb and Sara (mu'annas)
- Without fasl: أَسْلَمَتْ سَارَةُ (mu'annas verb is required)

### Rule 5: Naib al-Fa'il — Introduction (New Topic)

**Definition:** When the fi'l is **majhul (passive)**, the fa'il is dropped and the **maf'ul (object)** is raised to take its place. This maf'ul is now called **Naib al-Fa'il** (نَائِبُ الْفَاعِلِ).

**Text from the book:**
> وَقَدْ يُحْذَفُ الْفَاعِلُ وَيُقَامُ الْمَفْعُولُ مَقَامَهُ إِذَا كَانَ الْفِعْلُ مَجْهُولًا

Translation: *"The fa'il may be dropped and the maf'ul placed in its position when the verb is passive (majhul)."*

**Naib al-Fa'il is the second category of the Marfu'at** (after fa'il).

**Why drop the fa'il?**
1. The doer is unknown.
2. You intentionally wish to conceal the doer (e.g., to protect your good deed — not saying "I built this masjid" but "This masjid was built").

**Examples:**

| Active (Ma'lum) | Passive (Majhul) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ضَرَبْتُ زَيْدًا | ضُرِبَ زَيْدٌ | Zayd was struck |
| قَرَأْتُ الْكِتَابَ | قُرِئَ الْكِتَابُ | The book was read |
| أَطْعَمْتُ هَذَا الْفَقِيرَ | أُطْعِمَ هَذَا الْفَقِيرُ | This poor man was fed |
| بَنَيْتُ هَذَا الْمَسْجِدَ | بُنِيَ هَذَا الْمَسْجِدُ | This masjid was built |

---

### Rule 6: All Rules of Fa'il Apply to Naib al-Fa'il

Every rule you learned about fa'il applies identically to naib al-fa'il.

**Example with fasl:**
- ضُرِبَ الْيَوْمَ فَاطِمَةُ — verb is mudhakkar (ضُرِبَ) even though naib al-fa'il (فَاطِمَةُ) is mu'annas, because الْيَوْمَ creates a fasl between the verb and naib al-fa'il
- Without fasl: ضُرِبَتْ فَاطِمَةُ (verb must be mu'annas)

|---|---|
| ذَهَبَتْ | Fi'l madi, ta' al-ta'nith al-sakinah attached; verb is wahid mu'annas |
| الطَّالِبَاتُ | Fa'il, marfu', 'alama rafi'ihi al-damma; jama' mu'annas salim |

### Example 2: دَخَلَتَا الْبِنْتَانِ الْمَسْجِدَ
| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| دَخَلَتَا | Fi'l madi + alif al-tasniya; verb matches dual fa'il |
| الْبِنْتَانِ | Fa'il, marfu', 'alama rafi'ihi al-alif (tasniya) |
| الْمَسْجِدَ | Maf'ul bih, mansub |

### Example 3: ضُرِبَ زَيْدٌ
| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| ضُرِبَ | Fi'l madi majhul (passive) |
| زَيْدٌ | Naib al-fa'il, marfu', 'alama rafi'ihi al-damma |

### Example 4: ضُرِبَ الْيَوْمَ فَاطِمَةُ
| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| ضُرِبَ | Fi'l madi majhul, mudhakkar (allowed due to fasl) |
| الْيَوْمَ | Zarf zaman, mansub; acts as fasl between fi'l and naib al-fa'il |
| فَاطِمَةُ | Naib al-fa'il, marfu', 'alama rafi'ihi al-damma; mu'annas |

## Key Takeaways

1. **Verb number when fa'il is mu'akhkhar:** The verb is always wahid (singular) regardless of whether the fa'il is singular, dual, or plural — number agreement is suspended; only gender agreement remains.

2. **Jama' Taksir rule summary:**
   - All jama' taksir = treated like mu'annas ghair haqiqi (per the text).
   - **Ghair 'aqil** (non-living): obligatory (lazim) to treat as singular feminine.
   - **'Aqil** (living/rational): optional (ikhtiyari) — can treat as singular feminine OR as plural masculine.

3. **Fasl permits mudhakkar verb:** Any word separating the verb from a mu'annas fa'il (or naib al-fa'il) allows the verb to be mudhakkar. Without fasl, the verb must match the gender of the fa'il.

4. **Naib al-Fa'il is the second category of Marfu'at.** When the verb is passive (majhul), the fa'il is dropped and the maf'ul takes its place in rafa', now called naib al-fa'il.

5. **All rules of fa'il apply to naib al-fa'il** — including the fasl rule, gender agreement, and the wahid requirement.

6. **Why use the passive?** To omit the doer — either because the doer is unknown, or to deliberately avoid mentioning yourself (e.g., concealing a good deed: بُنِيَ الْمَسْجِدُ instead of بَنَيْتُ الْمَسْجِدَ).
