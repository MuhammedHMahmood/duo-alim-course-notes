## Session Overview

This session was a review and error-correction class focused on the nine categories of I'rab (إعراب). Students were given sentences with grammatical errors to identify and correct, covering concepts such as Taqdeeri I'rab, Mudaaf-Mudaaf Ilayh structures, Mabni vs. Ma'rab distinctions, and special cases like Ism Manqoos and Ghayr Munsarif. The sheikh also clarified the conceptual difference between Mabni and Taqdeeri I'rab, which generated significant discussion.

---

## Grammar Rules Covered

### 1. Taqdeeri I'rab — Hidden Vowel Signs
When an ism is **Mudaaf toward Yaa Mutakallim** (the first-person possessive ي), I'rab is **Taqdeeri** (hidden) in all three cases (Rafa', Nasab, Jarr).
- The word does not visibly change its ending.
- Example: كِتَابِي — it remains كِتَابِي regardless of its grammatical position.

### 2. Noon Drop in Tasniya and Jama' Mudhakkar Salim When Mudaaf
When a **Tasniya** (dual) or **Jama' Mudhakkar Salim** (sound masculine plural) is Mudaaf, the **Noon is dropped (Noon Mahjoof/Noon Biqaya drops)**.
- Incorrect: وَلَدَانِ حَامِدٍ (noon retained)
- Correct: وَلَدَا حَامِدٍ (noon dropped)
- Incorrect: مُسْلِمُونَ بَاكِسْتَانَ
- Correct: مُسْلِمُو بَاكِسْتَانَ (noon drops because it is Mudaaf)

### 3. Ism Manqoos — Deficient Noun I'rab
An **Ism Manqoos** is a noun ending in ي preceded by Kasra (e.g., قَاضِي). Its I'rab rules:
- In **Rafa'** and **Jarr**: I'rab is Taqdeeri (hidden) — the ending appears unchanged.
- In **Nasab**: I'rab is **Zaahir (visible)** — the Fatha appears: قَاضِيَ.
- Therefore: رَأَيْتُ الْقَاضِيَ (not القاضي) because رَأَيْتُ requires Nasab (Maf'ool Bih).

### 4. Khabar of Inna is Marfoo'
After **إِنَّ** and its sisters, the **Ism** takes Nasab and the **Khabar** takes **Rafa'**.
- إِنَّ هُنَّ مُسْلِمَاتٌ — مُسْلِمَاتٌ is Khabar, therefore Marfoo' (with Dhamma, written with Tanween Damm).
- It is NOT Maf'ool, so it does NOT take Kasra/Fatha.
- Special note: when Jama' Mu'annath Salim is Mansub, it takes **Kasra** instead of Fatha (e.g., إِنَّ الْمُسْلِمَاتِ صَالِحَاتٌ).

### 5. Mabni vs. Ma'rab — Key Distinction

| Category | Definition | I'rab |
|---|---|---|
| **Mabni** | Word that does NOT accept I'rab; its last haraka never changes | No I'rab applied; Amil is irrelevant |
| **Ma'rab** | Word that accepts I'rab; haraka changes according to Amil | I'rab applied (either Zaahir or Taqdeeri) |
| **Taqdeeri** | Sub-type of Ma'rab: I'rab comes but is hidden/unable to appear | Word appears unchanged but is still Ma'rab |

**Categories classified as Mabni:**
- Zamaa'ir (pronouns)
- Asmaa' ul-Ishaara (demonstrative nouns) — e.g., هَذَا، هَؤُلَاءِ
- Asmaa' ul-Mawsoola (relative nouns) — e.g., الَّذِي
- Asmaa' ul-Istifhaam (interrogative nouns)
- Asmaa' ul-Sharat (conditional nouns)
- Ba'dhul Dhuroof (some adverbs)

**Examples of Mabni:**
- هَؤُلَاءِ: جَاءَ هَؤُلَاءِ / رَأَيْتُ هَؤُلَاءِ / مَرَرْتُ بِهَؤُلَاءِ — ending never changes (always Kasra by birth)
- الَّذِي: جَاءَ الَّذِي / رَأَيْتُ الَّذِي / مَرَرْتُ بِالَّذِي — Sukoon never changes

**Taqdeeri example (Ma'rab, not Mabni):**
- مُوسَى: جَاءَ مُوسَى / رَأَيْتُ مُوسَى — ending appears unchanged but I'rab is Taqdeeri, NOT Mabni

### 6. Ghayr Munsarif — Non-Diptote (Briefly Mentioned)
One of the nine categories of I'rab. Full treatment deferred to the next chapter — the class has not yet covered what Ghayr Munsarif is, so its I'rab rules were not discussed in this session.

## I'rab (Sentence Analysis) Examples

### Example 1 — Yaa Mutakallim (Taqdeeri I'rab)
**Sentence:** رَأَيْتُ كِتَابِيَّ فِي بَيْتِكَ
- Error: كِتَابِيَّ → Correct: كِتَابِي
- **Rule:** When Ism is Mudaaf toward Yaa Mutakallim, I'rab is Taqdeeri in all three cases.
- كِتَابِي appears the same in Rafa', Nasab, and Jarr — the haraka is hidden.

---

### Example 2 — Noun Drop in Tasniya Mudaaf
**Sentence:** قَرَأَ وَلَدَانِ حَامِدٍ سُورَةَ يَاسِين سَبْعِينَ مَرَّةً
- **Errors:**
  1. وَلَدَانِ حَامِدٍ — Noon must drop because وَلَدَا is Mudaaf toward حَامِدٍ
  2. حَامِدٍ remains Majroor (Mudaaf Ilayh) — correct as-is; it is neither حَامِدًا nor حَامِدٌ
- **Correct:** قَرَأَ وَلَدَا حَامِدٍ سُورَةَ يَاسِين سَبْعِينَ مَرَّةً
- I'rab breakdown:
  - قَرَأَ — Fi'l Madi
  - وَلَدَا — Faa'il, Marfoo', Tasniya (Mudaaf, noon dropped)
  - حَامِدٍ — Mudaaf Ilayh, Majroor
  - سُورَةَ — Maf'ool Bih, Mansub
  - يَاسِين — Mudaaf Ilayh (or Badal), Majroor (Ghayr Munsarif — no Tanween)
  - سَبْعِينَ مَرَّةً — Maf'ool Mutlaq or Zarf, Mansub

---

### Example 3 — Ism Manqoos in Nasab
**Sentence:** رَأَيْتُ الْقَاضِي
- Error: القاضي → Correct: الْقَاضِيَ
- **Rule:** Ism Manqoos shows visible Fatha (Nasab) — unlike Rafa' and Jarr where I'rab is Taqdeeri.
- رَأَيْتُ requires Nasab for its Maf'ool Bih → الْقَاضِيَ

---

### Example 4 — Jama' Mudhakkar Salim Mudaaf
**Sentence:** بَنَى مُسْلِمُونَ بَاكِسْتَانَ هَذَا الْمَسْجِدَ
- Error: مُسْلِمُونَ → Correct: مُسْلِمُو (Noon drops; Mudaaf toward بَاكِسْتَان)
- **Correct:** بَنَى مُسْلِمُو بَاكِسْتَانَ هَذَا الْمَسْجِدَ
- Note: بَاكِسْتَان is a proper name (Mabni/Alam), already Ma'rifah — no need to add ال or ي.
- Note: بَاكِسْتَان is Ghayr Munsarif (no Tanween), so it cannot become بَاكِسْتَانِيّ in this context.

---

### Example 5 — Khabar of Inna
**Sentence:** إِنَّ هُنَّ مُسْلِمَاتٌ
- This sentence is **correct as written**.
- هُنَّ — Ism of Inna, Mansub (but Mabni — pronoun)
- مُسْلِمَاتٌ — Khabar of Inna, **Marfoo'** (Tanween Damm)
- **Common mistake:** thinking مُسْلِمَات is Maf'ool Bih and should take Kasra → incorrect; it is Khabar, which is Marfoo'.
- Contrast: إِنَّ الْمُسْلِمَاتِ صَالِحَاتٌ — here الْمُسْلِمَاتِ is Ism of Inna, Mansub → Jama' Mu'annath Salim takes Kasra (not Fatha) in Nasab.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Taqdeeri ≠ Mabni** — Though the outcome (word appearing unchanged) looks the same, they are fundamentally different: Taqdeeri is a sub-type of Ma'rab (I'rab exists but is hidden); Mabni means no I'rab at all. Mabni words are classified as such by grammarians into fixed categories (pronouns, demonstratives, relative nouns, etc.).

2. **Noon always drops in Mudaaf** — Whether it is the Noon of Tasniya or the Noon of Jama' Mudhakkar Salim, once the word is Mudaaf, the Noon is dropped. Never retain the Noon in a Mudaaf.

3. **Ism Manqoos shows I'rab only in Nasab** — In Rafa' and Jarr, the Fatha/Kasra on the ي cannot appear (Taqdeeri). In Nasab, the Fatha appears: قَاضِيَ. Students must remember to write the Fatha in Nasab.

4. **Khabar of Inna is always Marfoo'** — Do not confuse it with Maf'ool Bih. إِنَّ makes its own Ism Mansub; the Khabar that follows remains in Rafa'.

5. **Jama' Mu'annath Salim takes Kasra in Nasab** — Unlike most nouns that take Fatha in Nasab, JMS (e.g., مُسْلِمَات) takes Kasra when Mansub (as Ism of Inna, or Maf'ool Bih, etc.).
