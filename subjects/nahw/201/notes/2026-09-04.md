## Session Overview

This session covered the **third sabab (cause) of Ghayr Munsarif** — Ta'nīth (femininity). The teacher explained the two types of Ta'nīth (Lafẓī and Ma'nawī), the conditions under which a word becomes Ghayr Munsarif on the basis of Ta'nīth combined with 'Alamiyyat, and worked through numerous examples including names of people and places. The discussion included a detailed breakdown of the special sub-conditions that apply when a Ta'nīth Ma'nawī word is Thulāthī (three-letter).

---

## Grammar Rules Covered

### Background: Ghayr Munsarif Requires Two Asbāb
- A word becomes Ghayr Munsarif when **two causes (asbāb)** are simultaneously present.
- The total asbāb of Ghayr Munsarif are **nine**; previously two were covered; today is the **third**.
- Ghayr Munsarif does **not** take kasrah — it takes fatḥah even in jarr position.
  - Example: عَن عَائِشَةَ (not عَن عَائِشَةِ) because عائشة is Ghayr Munsarif.

---

### The Third Sabab: Ta'nīth (التأنيث)

Ta'nīth alone is not sufficient — it must combine with **'Alamiyyat (being a proper noun)** as the second sabab.

---

### Type 1: Ta'nīth Lafẓī (تأنيث لفظي)
A word that carries a **visible (ẓāhir) sign/marker of femininity**. The three alamāt (markers) of ta'nīth are:
1. **Tā' marbūṭah** (ة) — e.g., فَاطِمَة، حَفْصَة
2. **Alif maqṣūrah** (ى) — e.g., حُبْلى
3. **Alif mamdūdah** (اء) — e.g., حَمْرَاء

**Rule for Ta'nīth Lafẓī:**
- If a word has a visible ta'nīth marker **and** is an 'Alam (proper noun) → **Ghayr Munsarif**
- If it has the marker but is **not** an 'Alam → **Munsarif**

> Example: مَدْرَسَةٌ — has tā' marbūṭah (ta'nīth lafẓī) but is NOT an 'Alam → **Munsarif**
> Example: جَامِعَة (university as common noun) — NOT an 'Alam → **Munsarif**
> Example: حَفْصَة — ta'nīth lafẓī + 'Alam → **Ghayr Munsarif**

---

### Type 2: Ta'nīth Ma'nawī (تأنيث معنوي)
A word with **no visible ta'nīth marker** but which is used **exclusively for females**. Its femininity is established by usage/meaning, not by a written sign.

Examples: زَيْنَب، مَرْيَم، هِنْد

**Basis:** These names have never been used for men, so they are considered feminine by convention.

---

### Conditions for Ta'nīth Ma'nawī to Cause Ghayr Munsarif

The conditions depend on whether the word is **Ghair Thulāthī** or **Thulāthī**:

#### Case A: Ghair Thulāthī (غير ثلاثي) — more than three letters
- Condition: Must be an **'Alam**
- That alone is sufficient → **Ghayr Munsarif**
- Example: زَيْنَب (four letters, ta'nīth ma'nawī, 'alam) → **Ghayr Munsarif**

#### Case B: Thulāthī (ثلاثي) — exactly three letters
Three sub-conditions are checked in order:

| Sub-condition | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1. Mutaḥarrik al-Awsaṭ | Middle letter must have a vowel (ḥarakah) |
| 2. If Sākin al-Awsaṭ | Middle letter is vowelless → must be **'Ajamī (non-Arabic)** |
| 3. If Arabic + Sākin | All three violations present → treated as both Munsarif and Ghayr Munsarif (choice) |

**Decision tree for Thulāthī + Ta'nīth Ma'nawī + 'Alam:**

```
Is middle letter mutaḥarrik?
  YES → Ghayr Munsarif (e.g., سَقَر)
  NO (sākin) → Is the word 'Ajamī (non-Arabic)?
                  YES → Ghayr Munsarif (e.g., جُور)
                  NO (Arabic) → Both/Either (e.g., هِنْد)
```

---

### Summary Table of All Scenarios

| Type | Thulāthī? | Awsaṭ | Arabic/Ajamī | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ta'nīth Lafẓī | Any | — | — | Ghayr Munsarif (if 'Alam) |
| Ta'nīth Ma'nawī | Ghair Thulāthī | — | — | Ghayr Munsarif (if 'Alam) |
| Ta'nīth Ma'nawī | Thulāthī | Mutaḥarrik | — | Ghayr Munsarif |
| Ta'nīth Ma'nawī | Thulāthī | Sākin | 'Ajamī | Ghayr Munsarif |
| Ta'nīth Ma'nawī | Thulāthī | Sākin | 'Arabī | Munsarif OR Ghayr Munsarif (both valid) |

## I'rab (Sentence Analysis) Examples

### عَن عَائِشَةَ
- عَن: ḥarf jarr
- عَائِشَة: 'alam + ta'nīth lafẓī (tā' marbūṭah) → Ghayr Munsarif
- Expected kasrah after ḥarf jarr → replaced by **fatḥah**: عَائِشَةَ (not عَائِشَةِ)

### مَدْرَسَةٌ
- Has tā' marbūṭah (ta'nīth lafẓī) ✓
- Is it 'Alam? ✗
- Only one sabab → **Munsarif** (takes tanwīn and full declension)

### جَامِعَة (university, common noun)
- Ta'nīth lafẓī ✓ | 'Alam ✗ → **Munsarif**

### حَفْصَة
- Ta'nīth lafẓī (tā' marbūṭah) ✓ | 'Alam ✓ → **Ghayr Munsarif**

### زَيْنَب
- Ta'nīth ma'nawī (no visible marker, but exclusively a female name) ✓
- Ghair thulāthī (four letters: ز-ي-ن-ب) ✓ | 'Alam ✓ → **Ghayr Munsarif**

### سَقَر (name of a level of Jahannam, referenced in القرآن: وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا سَقَرُ)
- Ta'nīth ma'nawī ✓ | 'Alam ✓
- Thulāthī (س-ق-ر) | Middle letter (qāf) is **mutaḥarrik** (has fatḥah)
- → **Ghayr Munsarif**

### هِنْد
- Ta'nīth ma'nawī ✓ | 'Alam ✓
- Thulāthī (ه-ن-د) | Middle letter (nūn) is **sākin** ✗
- Arabic word (not 'Ajamī) ✗
- All three violations present → **both Munsarif and Ghayr Munsarif are valid**

### جُور (name of a place, non-Arabic origin)
- Ta'nīth ma'nawī ✓ | 'Alam ✓
- Thulāthī | Sākin al-awsaṭ | but **'Ajamī** (non-Arabic) ✓
- → **Ghayr Munsarif** (مَرَرْتُ بِجُورَ)

### مَكَّة
- 'Alam ✓ | Ta'nīth lafẓī (tā' marbūṭah) ✓ → **Ghayr Munsarif** (two asbāb)

### قَاهِرَة (Cairo)
- 'Alam ✓ | Ta'nīth lafẓī (tā' marbūṭah) ✓
- Also 'Ajamī in name usage → **Ghayr Munsarif**

### مِصْر (Egypt, also a Qur'anic word)
- 'Alam ✓ | Ta'nīth ma'nawī ✓
- Thulāthī (م-ص-ر) | Middle letter (ṣād) is **sākin**
- Non-Arabic ('Ajamī) ✓ → **Ghayr Munsarif**
- (Multiple asbāb can accumulate — a word having three or four asbāb is possible)

### پَاكِسْتَان (Pakistan)
- 'Alam ✓ | 'Ajamī ✓ → **Ghayr Munsarif** (two asbāb; 'Ajam is a separate sabab to be studied next session)

## Practice Examples

**Classify each as Munsarif or Ghayr Munsarif and state the asbāb:**

1. **فَاطِمَة** — Ta'nīth lafẓī + 'Alam → Ghayr Munsarif
2. **مَرْيَم** — Ta'nīth ma'nawī + 'Alam + Ghair Thulāthī → Ghayr Munsarif
3. **حُبْلى** (pregnant woman, common noun) — Ta'nīth lafẓī but NOT 'Alam → Munsarif
4. **سَقَر** — Ta'nīth ma'nawī + 'Alam + Thulāthī + Mutaḥarrik awsaṭ → Ghayr Munsarif
5. **هِنْد** — Ta'nīth ma'nawī + 'Alam + Thulāthī + Sākin awsaṭ + 'Arabī → Both valid
6. **جُور** — Ta'nīth ma'nawī + 'Alam + Thulāthī + Sākin awsaṭ + 'Ajamī → Ghayr Munsarif
7. **مِصْر** — 'Alam + Ta'nīth ma'nawī + 'Ajamī (+ Thulāthī Sākin) → Ghayr Munsarif

## Key Takeaways

1. **Two asbāb are required** to make any word Ghayr Munsarif. Ta'nīth alone is insufficient; it must pair with 'Alamiyyat.

2. **Ta'nīth Lafẓī is straightforward** — if a visible marker (ة / ى / اء) is present and the word is an 'Alam, it is Ghayr Munsarif. No further conditions.

3. **Ta'nīth Ma'nawī with Ghair Thulāthī is also straightforward** — four-or-more-letter words like زَيْنَب only need 'Alamiyyat to become Ghayr Munsarif.

4. **Ta'nīth Ma'nawī with Thulāthī requires the most care** — a layered check: Is the middle letter voweled? If not, is it non-Arabic? If both fail, the word sits on the fence between Munsarif and Ghayr Munsarif.

5. **Ghayr Munsarif takes fatḥah in jarr position** instead of kasrah, which is why عَائِشَةَ appears with fatḥah after عَن.

6. **Words can accumulate multiple asbāb** — مِصْر has at least three ('Alam, Ta'nīth, 'Ajamiyyah). Two are sufficient; more do not change the ruling.

7. **The next sabab to be studied is 'Ajamiyyah** (foreign/non-Arabic origin), which was previewed in today's examples involving جُور، مِصْر، and پَاكِسْتَان.

8. **Exam scope:** The main text (matn) is examinable; the hāshiyah (marginal commentary) is not.
