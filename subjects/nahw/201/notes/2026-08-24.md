## Session Overview

This session was largely Q&A-driven, covering key rules around Fi'il-Fa'il agreement (gender and number matching), the declension of Jama' Mu'annath Salim, Ism al-Manqus, and the distinction between Sahih in Nahw vs. Sarf. The teacher encouraged questions throughout and used live sentence correction as the primary teaching method.

---

## Grammar Rules Covered

### 1. Jama' Mu'annath Salim — Unusual I'rab

- **Marfu':** Gets Dammah (natural) → e.g. **الطالباتُ**
- **Majrur:** Gets Kasrah (natural) → e.g. **بالطالباتِ**
- **Mansub:** Does **NOT** get Fathah; instead gets **Kasrah** in the place of Fathah
  - This Kasrah represents/substitutes for Fathah
  - e.g. رَأَيْتُ طَالِبَاتٍ → Kasrah, not Fathah

### 2. Tasniyah vs. Jama' — Distinguishing by Ya

| Ending | Indicates | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ـَيْن (Ya, ma qabl Fathah) | Tasniyah (dual) | الْفَائِزَيْن |
| ـِيْن (Ya, ma qabl Kasrah) | Jama' (plural) | الْمُسْلِمِين |

### 3. Fi'il–Fa'il Agreement: Gender and Number Rules

This is the central rule covered in this session, applicable to **Jumlah Fi'liyyah**.

#### Gender Agreement
- **Always required** — regardless of whether Fa'il comes before or after the Fi'il.
- If Fa'il is Muzakkar → Fi'il must be Muzakkar.
- If Fa'il is Mu'annath → Fi'il must be Mu'annath.

#### Number Agreement

**Case A — Fa'il is Mu'akhkhar (comes after the Fi'il):** *(the default/standard order)*
- Fi'il must match Fa'il in **both gender AND number**.
- Wahid → Wahid, Tathniyah → Tathniyah, Jama' → Jama'

Example:
```
سَأَلَ الطُّلَّابُ أُسْتَاذَهُمُ    (Wahid Muzakkar — Jama' Muzakkar Fa'il)
سَأَلَتِ الطَّالِبَاتُ أُسْتَاذَهُنَّ   (Wahid Mu'annath — Jama' Mu'annath Fa'il)
```

**Case B — Fa'il is Muqaddam (comes before the Fi'il):** *(inverted order)*
- Fi'il **remains Wahid** (singular) regardless of the number of the Fa'il.
- Gender still must match.
- The actual agreement is technically through a Dhamir referring back to the Fa'il.

Example:
```
الطُّلَّابُ سَأَلُوا    ✓ (Fa'il muqaddam, Fi'il matches in number)
الطَّالِبَاتُ سَأَلْنَ   ✓
```
Reversed back:
```
سَأَلَ الطُّلَّابُ      ✓ (Wahid Muzakkar)
سَأَلَتِ الطَّالِبَاتُ   ✓ (Wahid Mu'annath)
سَأَلُوا الطُّلَّابُ    ✗ — WRONG (Jama' fi'il not permitted when Fa'il is mu'akhkhar)
سَأَلْنَ الطَّالِبَاتُ   ✗ — WRONG
```

**Summary table:**

| Fa'il Position | Gender must match? | Number must match? |
|---|---|---|
| Mu'akhkhar (after Fi'il) | Yes | Yes |
| Muqaddam (before Fi'il) | Yes | No — Fi'il stays Wahid |

### 4. Ism al-Manqus

- Definition: Any Ism ending in **Ya Ma Qabl Kasrah** (ي preceded by Kasrah).
- Examples: **قَاضٍ** (Qadi), **الجَانِي** (Al-Jani), **المُحَامِي** (Al-Muhami)
- I'rab behavior:
  - **Marfu' and Majrur:** Harakah is **Taqdiri** (implied/estimated) — the word appears unchanged visually.
  - **Mansub:** Fathah appears explicitly on the Ya → **رَأَيْتُ القَاضِيَ**

### 5. Sahih: Nahw vs. Sarf Definitions

| Discipline | Definition of Sahih |
|---|---|
| **Nahw** | Last letter (Lam al-Kalimah) is **not** a Harf 'Illah (ا، و، ي) |
| **Sarf** | **None** of the letters of the word is a Harf 'Illah anywhere in the word |

- **Zayd (زَيْد):** Sahih in Nahw (last letter Dal, not 'Illah) — but could be Mu'tall in Sarf if examined internally.
- **'Ali (عَلِي):** Mu'tall according to both (ends in Ya, a Harf 'Illah).
- **'Umar (عُمَر):** Sahih according to both.
- A word that is Mu'tall may carry additional ahkam (rules); otherwise the distinction is theoretical.

### 6. Ism al-Manqus in Jumlah: Inna + Jama' Muzakkar Salim

Sentence: إِنَّ الْمُسْلِمُونَ نَشَرُوا الْعِلْمَ فِي الْعَالَمِ

- **Error:** الْمُسْلِمُونَ is Marfu' form; after إِنَّ the Ism must be Mansub.
- **Correction:** إِنَّ الْمُسْلِمِينَ نَشَرُوا الْعِلْمَ فِي الْعَالَمِ ✓
- الْعِلْمَ is Maf'ul bih → Mansub → Fathah ✓ (no change needed)
- نَشَرُوا is Fi'il Jama'; since الْمُسْلِمِينَ is Jama', and it is the Fa'il (coming after the Fi'il implicitly through the Dhamir), this holds.

## I'rab (Sentence Analysis) Examples

### Example 1
**طَبَخَتِ الطَّالِبَاتُ اللَّحْمَ**
*(The female students cooked the meat)*

| Word | I'rab Role | Case | Marker |
|---|---|---|---|
| طَبَخَتْ | Fi'il Madi; Ta = Ta' Ta'nith Sakinah | — | — |
| الطَّالِبَاتُ | Fa'il | Marfu' | Dammah |
| اللَّحْمَ | Maf'ul bih | Mansub | Fathah |

- Ta' al-Ta'nith on the fi'il is required because Fa'il (الطالبات) is Mu'annath.

### Example 2
**رَأَيْتُ طَالِبَاتٍ فِي الْمَدْرَسَةِ**
*(I saw female students in the school)*

| Word | I'rab Role | Case | Marker |
|---|---|---|---|
| طَالِبَاتٍ | Maf'ul bih | Mansub | Kasrah (in place of Fathah) |

### Example 3
**إِنَّ الْفَائِزَيْنِ جَاؤُوا** — Error analysis

- الْفَائِزَيْنِ = Tasniyah (dual), Ya ma qabl Fathah
- جَاؤُوا = Jama' fi'il — mismatch in number when Fa'il is mu'akhkhar
- **Correction Option A:** إِنَّ الْفَائِزَيْنِ جَاءَا (Tasniyah fi'il)
- **Correction Option B:** إِنَّ الْفَائِزِينَ جَاؤُوا (change to Jama')

### Example 4
**الطُّلَّابُ سَأَلُوا أُسْتَاذَهُمُ الْأَسْئِلَةَ الْكَثِيرَةَ**
*(The students asked their teacher many questions — Fa'il Muqaddam)*

vs.

**سَأَلَ الطُّلَّابُ أُسْتَاذَهُمُ الْأَسْئِلَةَ الْكَثِيرَةَ**
*(Standard order — Fi'il Wahid, Fa'il mu'akhkhar)*

### Example 5 — Ism Manqus
**جَاءَ الْقَاضِي** — Marfu', Harakah Taqdiri (Ya unchanged)
**مَرَرْتُ بِالْقَاضِي** — Majrur, Harakah Taqdiri (Ya unchanged)
**رَأَيْتُ الْقَاضِيَ** — Mansub, Fathah appears explicitly on Ya

## Practice Examples

1. Identify the error and correct it:
   - **إِنَّ الْمُسْلِمُونَ نَشَرُوا الْعِلْمَ**
   - Error: المسلمون is Marfu'; after إِنَّ it must be Mansub
   - Correction: **إِنَّ الْمُسْلِمِينَ نَشَرُوا الْعِلْمَ** ✓

2. Choose the correct verb form:
   - الطَّالِبَاتُ _____ أُسْتَاذَهُنَّ: **سَأَلْنَ** (Fa'il muqaddam, both gender + number match)
   - _____ الطَّالِبَاتُ أُسْتَاذَهُنَّ: **سَأَلَتْ** (Fa'il mu'akhkhar, Wahid Mu'annath only)

3. Determine: Is الطُّلَّابُ Sahih or Mu'tall according to Nahw?
   - Last letter = Ba' → not a Harf 'Illah → **Sahih in Nahw**

4. Decline قَاضٍ in all three cases:
   - Marfu': **الْقَاضِي** (Taqdiri Dammah)
   - Mansub: **الْقَاضِيَ** (Fathah on Ya)
   - Majrur: **الْقَاضِي** (Taqdiri Kasrah)

## Key Takeaways

1. **Jama' Mu'annath Salim never takes Fathah** in the Mansub state — Kasrah substitutes for it. Only Dammah (Rafa') and Kasrah (Jarr and Nasb) are possible.

2. **Gender agreement in Jumlah Fi'liyyah is absolute** — Fi'il must always reflect the gender of its Fa'il, whether the Fa'il comes before or after.

3. **Number agreement depends on word order:** when Fa'il is after the Fi'il (standard), both gender and number match; when Fa'il is before the Fi'il (inverted), only gender matches — the Fi'il stays singular (Wahid).

4. **Tasniyah vs. Jama' Muzakkar Salim** are distinguished by the vowel before the Ya: Fathah = dual, Kasrah = plural. Confusing them causes agreement errors.

5. **Ism Manqus** shows its full Harakah only in Nasb (Fathah on Ya); in Rafa' and Jarr the Harakah is Taqdiri (implied, not visible).

6. **Sahih differs between Nahw and Sarf:** Nahw only checks the last letter; Sarf checks all letters.

7. **Tarkib (parsing) aids I'rab** but knowing meaning is often equally or more useful. Many fluent readers apply correct I'rab without formal Tarkib analysis.

8. Recommended reading: **مَاذَا خَسِرَ الْعَالَمُ بِانْحِطَاطِ الْمُسْلِمِينَ** by Sayyid Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi (English: *Islam and the World*) — originally in Arabic; translations exist but the original carries the author's spirit most fully.
