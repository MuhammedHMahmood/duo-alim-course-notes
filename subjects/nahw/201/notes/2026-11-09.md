## Grammar Rules Covered

### Mustasna — Four Categories of I'rab (Completion)

The session opened with review of the first two categories before introducing the third and fourth.

---

#### Category 3: Mustasna Mufarragh (المستثنى المفرَّغ)

**Definition:** When Mustasna comes after إلا in **Kalam Ghayr Mujab** (a negative or interrogative sentence) and the **Mustasna Minhu is not mentioned** (not present in the sentence).

- *Mufarragh* (مُفَرَّغ) literally means "empty/vacant" — the sentence is "empty" of a Mustasna Minhu.
- إلا is present but plays **no role in determining I'rab**.
- The Mustasna gets its **I'rab according to the Amil** (the grammatical agent in the sentence).

**Rule:** To determine what I'rab the Mustasna receives, temporarily **drop إلا** from the sentence and assess what grammatical role the word plays.

**Examples:**

| Sentence | Analysis | I'rab of Zaid |
|---|---|---|
| مَا جَاءَنِي إِلَّا زَيْدٌ | Drop إلا → زيد is Fa'il → Marfu | زيدٌ |
| مَا رَأَيْتُ إِلَّا زَيْدًا | Drop إلا → زيد is Maf'ul → Mansub | زيدًا |
| مَا مَرَرْتُ إِلَّا بِزَيْدٍ | Drop إلا → زيد follows Harf Jar → Majrur | بِزَيْدٍ |

**Key Point:** إلا remains present in the sentence for meaning (exclusion/exception), but it has no effect on I'rab in the Mufarragh case.

---

#### Category 4: Mustasna after غير / سِوى / سَوى / حَاشَ

**Rule:** When Mustasna comes after the words **غير**, **سِوى**, **سَوى**, or **حَاشَ**, the majority of grammarians (عند الأكثر) hold that the Mustasna will be **Majrur**.

**Examples:**

| Construction | Meaning |
|---|---|
| جَاءَ الْقَوْمُ غَيْرَ زَيْدٍ | The people came except Zayd |
| جَاءَ الْقَوْمُ سِوَى زَيْدٍ | The people came except Zayd |
| جَاءَ الْقَوْمُ سَوَى زَيْدٍ | The people came except Zayd |
| جَاءَ الْقَوْمُ حَاشَ زَيْدٍ | The people came except Zayd |

---

#### I'rab of غير Itself — The Key Usul

**Question:** What I'rab does the word **غير** itself receive?

**Rule (Usul):** اِعْرَابُ غَيْرٍ كَاِعْرَابِ الْمُسْتَثْنَى بِإِلَّا
*"The I'rab of غير is like the I'rab of the Mustasna with إلا."*

**Method:** Replace غير with إلا. Whatever I'rab the Mustasna (Zaid) would receive with إلا — that same I'rab is given to غير.

**Worked Example — مَا جَاءَنِي أَحَدٌ غَيْرَ زَيْدٍ:**

1. This is **Kalam Ghayr Mujab** (negated sentence: مَا)
2. Mustasna Minhu is **present** (أَحَدٌ)
3. → Category 2 applies: both Nasb and Badal are permitted
   - **Nasb:** زيدًا (straightforward Mansub)
   - **Badal:** زيدٌ (Badal of أحد, which is Marfu as Fa'il → Badal matches → Marfu)
4. Replace غير with إلا: Zaid gets Nasb **or** Rafa → Therefore غير gets **غَيْرَ** or **غَيْرُ**

**Both are correct:**
- مَا جَاءَنِي أَحَدٌ غَيْرُ زَيْدٍ (Marfu — Badal path)
- مَا جَاءَنِي أَحَدٌ غَيْرَ زَيْدٍ (Mansub — Nasb path)

**Note on the nature of غير:** The word غير is primarily made for **Sifah** (صِفَة — adjectival use), but it is also used for **Istithna** (exception), just as إلا is primarily made for Istithna but is sometimes used as Sifah (e.g., لَوْ كَانَ فِيهِمَا آلِهَةٌ إِلَّا اللهُ لَفَسَدَتَا / لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ).

---

### Summary of Mustasna I'rab — All Four Categories

| Category | Condition | I'rab of Mustasna |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Mutasil, Kalam Mujab** | After إلا, sentence is positive, Mustasna Minhu present, same category | Mansub (Wajib) |
| **2. Munqati' or Kalam Ghayr Mujab** | After إلا, sentence is negative/interrogative, Mustasna Minhu present | Nasb **or** Badal (both allowed) |
| **3. Mufarragh** | After إلا, Kalam Ghayr Mujab, Mustasna Minhu **absent** | According to Amil (إلا has no I'rab effect) |
| **4. After غير / سِوى / سَوى / حَاشَ** | Mustasna follows these particles | Majrur; غير itself takes I'rab per the Usul above |

---

### Khabar of Kana (كَانَ وَأَخَوَاتُهَا) — Introduced

- **Hukm:** Khabar of Kana is **Mansub** (taking Nasb case).
- Its rules are similar to Khabar of Mubtada **except** for one distinction:

**Taqdim (Preceding) of Khabar:** If the Khabar of Kana is a **Ma'rifah** (definite noun), it is **permissible (Ja'iz)** to place it before the Ism of Kana.

- Example: كَانَ الْقَائِمُ زَيْدٌ (Khabar placed before Ism — allowed)
- Contrast: In a normal Mubtada-Khabar sentence without Kana, if Khabar is Ma'rifah, Taqdim is **not** allowed, because without Kana both Mubtada and Khabar are Marfu and distinguishing them becomes difficult. With Kana present, identification is easy via I'rab, so Taqdim is permitted.

**Examples:**
- كَانَ زَيْدٌ قَائِمًا
- كَانَ اللهُ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمًا

---

### Ism of Inna (إِنَّ وَأَخَوَاتُهَا) — Introduced

- **Hukm:** The Ism of Inna and its sisters will be **Mansub**.

**Examples:**
- إِنَّ اللهَ قَوِيٌّ عَزِيزٌ
- إِنَّ اللهَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ
- إِنَّ اللهَ التَّوَّابُ الرَّحِيمُ
- إِنَّ رَبِّي رَحِيمٌ وَدُودٌ
- إِنَّ زَيْدًا قَائِمٌ

## I'rab (Sentence Analysis) Examples

### Example 1 — Mufarragh with Rafa
**مَا جَاءَنِي إِلَّا زَيْدٌ**
- مَا: Harf Nafy
- جَاءَ: Fi'l Madi
- نِي: Damir Muttasil, Maf'ul Bih
- إِلَّا: Adat Istithna (no I'rab effect here — Mufarragh)
- زَيْدٌ: Fa'il, Marfu (Amil = جَاءَ)

### Example 2 — Mufarragh with Nasb
**مَا رَأَيْتُ إِلَّا زَيْدًا**
- إِلَّا: Adat Istithna (no I'rab effect — Mufarragh)
- زَيْدًا: Maf'ul Bih, Mansub (Amil = رَأَيْتُ)

### Example 3 — Mufarragh with Jarr
**مَا مَرَرْتُ إِلَّا بِزَيْدٍ**
- بِ: Harf Jar
- زَيْدٍ: Majrur by بِ (Amil = بِ)

### Example 4 — After غير, Kalam Ghayr Mujab, Badal path
**مَا جَاءَنِي أَحَدٌ غَيْرُ زَيْدٍ**
- أَحَدٌ: Mustasna Minhu, Fa'il, Marfu
- غَيْرُ: Mustasna, Marfu (I'rab of غير = I'rab Mustasna would get with إلا → Badal path → Marfu)
- زَيْدٍ: Mudaf Ilayh of غير, Majrur

### Example 5 — After غير, Nasb path
**مَا جَاءَنِي أَحَدٌ غَيْرَ زَيْدٍ**
- غَيْرَ: Mustasna, Mansub (Nasb path)
- زَيْدٍ: Mudaf Ilayh, Majrur

### Example 6 — Khabar of Kana
**كَانَ زَيْدٌ قَائِمًا**
- كَانَ: Fi'l Madi Naqis
- زَيْدٌ: Ism of Kana, Marfu
- قَائِمًا: Khabar of Kana, Mansub

### Example 7 — Ism of Inna
**إِنَّ زَيْدًا قَائِمٌ**
- إِنَّ: Harf Tawkid wa Nasb
- زَيْدًا: Ism of Inna, Mansub
- قَائِمٌ: Khabar of Inna, Marfu

## Practice Examples

### From class discussion — Determine the I'rab:

**1. قَرَأَ الطُّلَّابُ الْكِتَابَ إِلَّا بَكْرًا**
- Sentence is **Kalam Mujab** (affirmative), Mustasna Minhu present (الطلاب), Mutasil
- → Mustasna **must** be Mansub: **بَكْرًا** ✓
- *Error to avoid:* saying بَكْرٌ — that would be wrong because the sentence is Mujab.

**2. مَا سَلَّمَ عَلَيَّ أَحَدٌ إِلَّا خَالِدٌ / خَالِدًا**
- Kalam Ghayr Mujab, Mustasna Minhu present (أَحَدٌ)
- → Both **خَالِدٌ** (Badal of أحد, Marfu) and **خَالِدًا** (Nasb) are correct.

**3. مَا تَنَاسَى إِلَّا كَلْبًا — Mutasil or Munqati'?**
- Discussed at start of class as Munqati' (disconnected exception).

## Key Takeaways

1. **Mufarragh = no Mustasna Minhu.** When the Mustasna Minhu is absent, the sentence is "empty" (مُفَرَّغ), إلا has no I'rab impact, and the Mustasna takes its I'rab purely from the Amil. The practical test: drop إلا and read the I'rab.

2. **غير / سِوى / سَوى / حَاشَ always make the Mustasna Majrur** (per majority of grammarians — عند الأكثر).

3. **The Usul for I'rab of غير:** Replace غير with إلا → whatever I'rab the Mustasna would receive with إلا, give that same I'rab to غير itself. This elegantly handles all cases including when multiple I'rabs are permitted.

4. **Khabar of Kana is Mansub**, like Khabar of Mubtada, with the extra allowance that if it is Ma'rifah it may be fronted (Taqdim Ja'iz) — which is not permitted in a simple Mubtada-Khabar sentence.
