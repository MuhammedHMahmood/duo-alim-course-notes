    Fiqh 201 - Study Notes

    Date: 2026-10-24

    Session Overview

    This session covered two major topics. The first was the rules of Qira'at (recitation) during Salah — specifically what is obligatory in different types of prayers (Fard, Wajib, Sunnah, Nafil), the differences of opinion among the major Imams, and the proofs for the Hanafi position. The second was an introduction to Sujood al-Sahw (prostrations of forgetfulness) — its conditions, how it is performed, and a detailed set of practical scenarios in which it is or is not required. The class also covered rules on praying Nafil while sitting, starting standing then sitting, and praying Nawafil while traveling.

    Key Themes

    1. Qira'at in Fard Salah: Recitation is obligatory only in the first two raka'at according to the Ahnaf, based on the principle that the first two raka'at are the asl (foundation) and the last two are tabi' (subsidiary/dependent).
    2. Qira'at in Nafil and Witar: Recitation is obligatory in every raka'at, because every two raka'at of Nafil constitute an independent unit (shufa) — itself a complete Salah.
    3. The Shufa concept: Each pair of raka'at in Nafil Salah is treated as a self-contained prayer unit. Completing the first two with a sitting (Qa'ida Ula) and Tashahud closes that shufa.
    4. Sujood al-Sahw: A wajib remedy for unintentional omission or addition of a wajib act in Salah. It does not compensate for a missed fard (which invalidates the Salah) or an intentionally missed wajib.
    5. Imam as Zamin: In congregation, the Imam bears responsibility (zamin) for the Salah of the followers. His mistake triggers Sajda Sahw for all; a muqtadi's mistake does not obligate the Imam or other muqtadis.

    Fiqh Rulings & Evidence

    1. Qira'at in Fard Salah (Hanafi Position)

    Ruling: Qira'at (Surah Fatiha + an additional Surah) is fard in the first two raka'at of every Fard Salah. In the last two raka'at (or last one in Maghrib), the worshipper has three options: 1. Recite Surah Fatiha (best) 2. Make zikr of Allah 3. Remain silent

    Evidence from Quran: - Surah al-Muzzammil 20: "Faqra'u ma tayassara min al-Quran" — "Read as much of the Quran as is easy for you." The sigha al-amr (imperative form) establishes the obligation of recitation. Once this order is fulfilled in the first raka'at, the fardiyet (obligatory demand) of that command is discharged. However, because the first two raka'at are identical in their arkan, what is done in the first must also be done in the second (dalalat al-nass).

    Evidence from Hadith: - Hadith of Abdullah ibn Qatada (r.a.) (Sahih Muslim): "The Prophet (ﷺ) used to recite al-Fatiha followed by another Surah in the first two raka'at of Zuhr and recite only al-Fatiha in the last two raka'at. Sometimes a verse or so was audible. He used to prolong the first raka'at more than the second and similarly in Asr and Fajr." - Hadith of Abu Qatada (r.a.) (Sahih Muslim): The Prophet (ﷺ) recited al-Fatiha and two Surahs in the first raka'at of Zuhr and Asr, and prolonged the first raka'at more than the second. - Hadith regarding Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas and Umar ibn al-Khattab (r.a.): Sa'd said he led prayer in accordance with the Prophet's prayer — he made them stand longer in the first two and shortened in the last two. Umar confirmed this was correct. - Musannaf Ibn Abi Shaybah: An entire chapter (bab) of hadiths establishing that a person may limit himself to Azkar in the last two raka'at of Fard Salah.

    2. Three Proofs that the First Two Raka'at are Asl and the Last Two are Tabi'

    Proof Explanation
    Qasr in travel When shortening Fard Salah, the last two raka'at are dropped — the first two (the asl) remain intact
    Jahr and Sirr In loud prayers (Fajr, Maghrib, Isha), the Imam recites loudly only in the first two raka'at, never the last two
    Length of recitation The first two raka'at have longer recitation (Fatiha + Surah); the last two are shorter (Fatiha only or silence)

    3. Qira'at in Nafil and Witar Salah

    Ruling: Qira'at is wajib in every raka'at of Nafil and Witar Salah.

    Reason for Nafil: Every two raka'at of Nafil constitutes a shufa — a complete, independent prayer unit — even if made with a single intention and single salam for four raka'at. Since each shufa is a salah in itself, full recitation is required in each of its raka'at.

    Evidence for Nafil: Hadith in Abu Dawud, narrated by Abdullah ibn Umar (r.a.): "Prayer by night and day should consist of pairs of raka'at." This establishes the two-raka'at unit as the basic building block of Nafil.

    Evidence for Witar: Hadith in Tirmidhi (from Abdul Aziz ibn Jurayj asking Aisha r.a.): The Prophet (ﷺ) recited: - 1st raka'at: Surah al-A'la (Sabbih isma rabbik al-a'la) - 2nd raka'at: Surah al-Kafirun (Qul ya ayyuhal-kafirun) - 3rd raka'at: Surah al-Ikhlas and the Mu'awidhatain (Surah al-Falaq and al-Nas)

    This proves recitation in every raka'at of Witar.

    4. Broken Nafil Salah — Qada Obligation

    Ruling (Abu Hanifa & Imam Muhammad): If a person began a four-raka'at Nafil and completed the first two with a proper sitting (Qa'ida Ula) and Tashahud, then broke the Salah — qada of only two raka'at is required (the second shufa), since the first shufa is already complete.

    Ruling (Imam Abu Yusuf): Qada of all four raka'at is required.

    Condition: The first shufa is only complete if the person sat for the duration of Tashahud after the second raka'at. If the Qa'ida Ula was not completed, the entire four raka'at must be made up.

    Why qada at all? (Hanafi proof against Imam Shafi'i, who says no qada): - Quran 22:29 (Surah al-Hajj): Obligations (nazar) must be fulfilled — once a person begins Nafil, it becomes an amal upon them. - Quran 47:33 (Surah Muhammad): "Do not invalidate your deeds (amal)."

    5. Nafil Salah While Sitting (Without a Valid Excuse)

    Ruling: Permissible in Nafil Salah even for a person who is fully capable of standing.

    Reward: Half the reward of praying while standing.

    Evidence: Hadith in Tirmidhi, narrated by Imran ibn Husayn (r.a.): "Whoever prays standing — that is most virtuous. Whoever prays sitting — he gets half the reward of the one standing. Whoever prays lying down — he gets half the reward of the one sitting." (i.e., one-quarter of standing reward.)

    Important distinction: This leniency applies only to Nafil. In Fard Salah, Qiyam (standing) is a fard/rukun — it cannot be omitted without a valid excuse.

    6. Starting Nafil Standing Then Sitting Mid-Salah

    Ruling (Imam Abu Hanifa): Permissible — a person may begin Nafil Salah standing and then sit down during it.

    Ruling (Sahibayn — Abu Yusuf & Muhammad): Not permissible without a valid excuse. Once begun standing, it must be completed standing.

    Muftabih (relied-upon) fatwa: The opinion of Imam Abu Hanifa — it is permissible.

    Evidence (Imam Abu Hanifa): Hadith in Bukhari (from Aisha r.a.): In his later years, the Prophet (ﷺ) would begin prayer sitting, and when approximately 30–40 verses remained, he would stand and recite them standing, then bow and prostrate.

    7. Praying Nawafil While Traveling (On a Ride)

    Ruling: A traveler may pray Nawafil while on his mount/vehicle, facing any direction — facing the Qibla is not required.

    Reason: The traveler is in a hurry or on a schedule; requiring him to stop (especially in a caravan) would cause hardship for the entire group.

    Evidence: Hadith in Bukhari, narrated by Jabir ibn Abdullah (r.a.): "The Prophet (ﷺ) used to offer Nawafil while riding, facing a direction other than the Qibla."

    Note: The teacher will confirm the ruling for a person traveling within their own city (less than the travel distance).

    8. Sujood al-Sahw — Introduction and Method

    Definition: Two prostrations performed to compensate for an unintentional addition or omission of a wajib act in Salah.

    Method: 1. Complete the Salah normally 2. Make salam (to the right only) 3. Perform two sujood 4. Sit for Tashahud 5. Make salam again

    Evidence: Hadith in Sahih Muslim, narrated by Imran ibn Husayn (r.a.): The Prophet (ﷺ) prayed only three raka'at of Zuhr, made salam, and went inside. A Companion (al-Khirbaq) came and informed him. The Prophet came out, prayed one additional raka'at, gave salam, then performed two prostrations, and gave salam again.

    Evidence (for adding a raka'at): Hadith narrated by Abdullah (r.a.) (Bukhari): The Prophet (ﷺ) prayed five raka'at in Zuhr. When informed by the Sahaba, he bent his legs and performed two prostrations immediately.

    9. Sujood al-Sahw — When It Is Required

    Scenario Ruling
    Performing three Sujood instead of two (added an action of Salah) Sajda Sahw wajib
    Missing Surah Fatiha (not recited at all) in first two raka'at Sajda Sahw wajib
    Reciting Surah before Surah Fatiha (wrong order) Sajda Sahw wajib
    Missing the Qa'ida al-Ula (first sitting) entirely Sajda Sahw wajib
    Missing Tashahud in Qa'ida al-Ula Sajda Sahw wajib
    Missing Qunoot in Witar Sajda Sahw wajib
    Missing the extra Takbirat (Takbirat al-Zawa'id) in Eid prayer Sajda Sahw wajib
    Imam recites quietly in a Jahri Salah (or loudly in a Sirri Salah) Sajda Sahw wajib
    Waiting for the duration of three Subhanallah while thinking which Surah to recite Sajda Sahw wajib
    Confusion about number of raka'at prayed (waited the duration of three Subhanallah) Sajda Sahw wajib
    Waiting the duration of three Subhanallah in Qa'ida before reciting Darud Sajda Sahw wajib

    10. Sujood al-Sahw — When It Is NOT Required

    Scenario Reason
    Missing a Sunnah act (e.g., Thana, Ta'awwuz, Tasbih in Ruku/Sujood) Sunnah omission does not require Sajda Sahw
    Not reciting Surah Fatiha in the last two raka'at of Fard Salah Permissible to remain silent — not a violation
    Reciting an extra Surah in the last raka'at of Fard by mistake Not wajib in that position anyway
    Forgetting to raise hands during Takbir al-Tahreema Raising hands is Sunnah; only the verbal Takbir is Fard
    Missing Darud (Salawat) in the last Qa'ida Sunnah act
    Missing Sami'allahu liman hamidah Sunnah act
    Multiple wajibat missed in one Salah Only one Sajda Sahw is required regardless of how many wajibat were missed

    11. Fard vs. Wajib vs. Sunnah — Impact on Salah Validity

    Category Missed Unintentionally Missed Intentionally
    Fard (Rukun) Salah is invalid (fasid) — must be repeated Salah is invalid — must be repeated
    Wajib Sajda Sahw compensates — Salah is valid Salah is invalid — must be repeated
    Sunnah No consequence — Salah remains valid No consequence for validity (but rewards diminished)

    12. Qa'ida al-Ula — Missed and Remembered While Rising

    Scenario: A person is performing a three- or four-raka'at Salah, begins to rise for the third raka'at, and suddenly remembers he forgot the first sitting.

    Position When Remembered Ruling
    Closer to the sitting position (less than halfway up) Return and sit immediately; complete the Tashahud; Sajda Sahw required
    Closer to the standing position (more than halfway up) Do not return to sitting; continue standing; Sajda Sahw required at the end

    Reason for not returning when closer to standing: Returning would constitute adding an extra julus (sitting) without completing its purpose, introducing a greater violation than the original one.

    13. Sajda Sahw — Imam and Muqtadi

    Situation Ruling
    Imam makes a mistake requiring Sajda Sahw All muqtadis must perform Sajda Sahw with the Imam
    Imam does not perform Sajda Sahw Muqtadis cannot perform it independently
    Muqtadi makes a mistake (Imam did not) Neither the Imam nor the other muqtadis perform Sajda Sahw — the Imam's sound Salah covers it

    Ruling on Sajda Sahw after Salam (if already made Salam by mistake): As long as the person: - Has not turned their shoulders away from the Qibla, and - Has not performed any act prohibited during Salah (talking, eating, etc.)

    ...they may still perform Sajda Sahw and the Salah remains valid. Even performing Zikr of Allah after the inadvertent Salam does not invalidate this window.

    Conditions & Categories

    Conditions for Sajda Sahw to Be Valid After Salam

    1. The person has not yet turned away from the Qibla (shoulders still facing Qibla).
    2. The person has not spoken to anyone.
    3. The person has not eaten or drunk anything.
    4. The person has not performed any act that breaks Salah (e.g., Wudu broken).

    (Performing Zikr of Allah in this window does not prevent Sajda Sahw.)

    Categories of Raka'at in Fard Salah (Hanafi Framework)

    Category Raka'at Qira'at Status Notes
    Asl (Foundation) 1st and 2nd Fard (Fatiha + Surah) Identical in all arkan; the obligatory basis
    Tabi' (Subsidiary) 3rd (and 4th) Optional (Fatiha / Zikr / Silence) Dropped in Qasr; no loud recitation by Imam

    Categories of Acts in Salah

    Level Examples Missed Unintentionally
    Fard/Rukun Qiyam, Ruku', Sujood, Qa'ida Akhira, Takbir al-Tahreema Salah invalid — must repeat
    Wajib Qa'ida al-Ula, Qunoot, Surah Fatiha in first two raka'at, Tashahud in Qa'ida al-Ula, extra Eid Takbirat, reciting in every raka'at of Nafil/Witar Sajda Sahw required
    Sunnah Thana, Ta'awwuz, Tasbih in Ruku'/Sujood, Raising hands in Takbir, Darud in last Qa'ida, Sami'allahu liman hamidah, Basmala before Fatiha No Sajda Sahw

    Scholarly Opinions

    On Qira'at in Fard Salah

    Scholar Position
    Ahnaf (Imam Abu Hanifa) Fard in first two raka'at only; last two are optional (best to recite Fatiha; zikr or silence also permissible)
    Imam al-Zufar & Hasan al-Basri Once recitation is done in the first raka'at, the fardiyet is entirely discharged — the second raka'at is also technically optional; but Ahnaf add the first two are copies of each other
    Imam al-Shafi'i Fard in every raka'at of Fard Salah — each raka'at is itself a Salah
    Imam Malik Required in the majority (akthar) of raka'at: first three of a four-raka'at Salah; first two of a three-raka'at Salah — the akthar equals the kull (whole) in his legal framework

    On Qada of Broken Nafil Salah (Four Raka'at Started, First Two Completed)

    Scholar Position
    Imam Abu Hanifa & Imam Muhammad Qada of two raka'at only (the incomplete second shufa)
    Imam Abu Yusuf Qada of all four raka'at

    On Starting Nafil Standing Then Sitting Mid-Salah

    Scholar Position
    Imam Abu Hanifa (muftabih) Permissible — cites the Prophet's (ﷺ) practice in his later years
    Sahibayn (Abu Yusuf & Muhammad) Not permissible without a valid excuse — must finish as started

    On Sajda Sahw in Broken Nafil

    Scholar Position
    Ahnaf Qada is wajib whether the Nafil was broken intentionally or unintentionally — beginning a voluntary act obligates its completion
    Imam al-Shafi'i No qada — the Nafil was never obligatory to begin with; breaking it does not change that

    On the Imam as Zamin (Guarantor)

    Scholar Position
    Ahnaf Imam is zamin — his sound Salah covers the followers; a muqtadi's mistake does not require Sajda Sahw from the Imam
    Imam al-Shafi'i Does not consider the Imam as zamin in this sense

    Hanafi dalil: Hadith in Dar Qutni establishing the Imam's guarantor role.

    Lessons & Practical Application

    1. In four-raka'at Fard Salah (Zuhr, Asr, Isha): Ensure full recitation (Fatiha + Surah) in the first two raka'at. In the third and fourth, the best practice is to recite Surah Fatiha, though zikr or silence is also permissible.

    2. In Witar Salah: Recitation is obligatory in all three raka'at — do not treat it like Fard in the last raka'at.

    3. In Nafil Salah: Recitation is obligatory in every raka'at. If you intend four raka'at, ensure you recite in all four.

    4. If you miss Surah Fatiha or recite a Surah before it: Do not panic — Sajda Sahw will rectify it. Continue the Salah and perform Sajda Sahw at the end.

    5. If you forget the Qa'ida al-Ula and are only slightly above sitting position: Return and sit immediately. If you are already more upright (closer to standing), continue standing and do Sajda Sahw — do not force yourself back down.

    6. Never perform Sajda Sahw for a missed Sunnah act (e.g., forgot Thana, Ta'awwuz, Tasbih). It is not required and should not be done.

    7. If multiple wajibat are missed in one Salah: One single Sajda Sahw covers all of them — you do not perform multiple Sajda Sahw.

    8. In congregation: If the Imam makes a mistake and performs Sajda Sahw, follow him — do not remain seated. If you personally made a mistake and the Imam did not perform Sajda Sahw, do not perform it independently; the Imam's valid Salah covers you.

    9. After the inadvertent Salam: You still have a window to perform Sajda Sahw as long as you have not turned away from the Qibla or done anything that breaks Salah (including speaking to people). Use this window.

    10. Praying Nafil while sitting (with no excuse): Permitted, but you receive only half the reward. Whenever possible, pray standing to earn the full reward.

    11. If traveling: You may pray Nawafil on your vehicle facing any direction — this is a specific concession for travelers to avoid holding up the caravan.

    12. Intentional omission of a wajib = Salah is invalid. This is a critical boundary: Sajda Sahw only applies to unintentional errors. Deliberately skipping a wajib requires repeating the entire Salah.
