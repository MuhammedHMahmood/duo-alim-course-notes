    Fiqh 201 - Study Notes

    Date: 2026-09-02

    Session Overview

    This session covered the remaining topics of Tayammum (dry ablution), including the conditions permitting it, distances for seeking water, sickness-related rulings, what invalidates it, and the permissibility of performing multiple prayers with one Tayammum. The second half introduced Masa al-Khuffayn (wiping over leather socks/khuffs), covering its dalil, conditions, duration, method, and what invalidates it. The session concluded with a detailed discussion on the origins of the Hanafi Madhab and the hierarchy of its scholars' opinions.

    Key Themes

    1. Conditions permitting Tayammum — absence of water, fear of harm, sickness, and specific missed prayers (Janazah, Eid)
    2. Distance for seeking water — scholarly opinions on how far one must search before resorting to Tayammum
    3. Sickness rulings — three categories in which Tayammum is permitted due to physical inability
    4. What breaks Tayammum — mirrors what breaks Wudhu, plus finding sufficient water
    5. Performing multiple prayers with one Tayammum — difference between Ahnaf and Imam Shafi'i
    6. Masa al-Khuffayn — its Sunnah basis, conditions, duration (24 hrs / 72 hrs), method, and invalidators
    7. Hanafi Madhab hierarchy — origins, Zahir al-Riwayah, and whose opinion is Mufta Bihi in different domains

    Fiqh Rulings & Evidence

    Tayammum — General Permissibility

    • Tayammum is valid as a replacement for both Wudhu and Ghusl.
    • Conditions for permissibility:
    • No water available within the searchable distance
    • Water is visible but unreachable
    • Reaching water poses danger (from humans or animals)
    • Fear of falling sick from using water, or worsening an existing illness

    • Cold water will cause illness

    • Dalil: Hadith in Abu Dawud — the Prophet (ﷺ) said that the soil/earth is a means of Tahara for Muslims even when water is not available for 10 years. This hadith is also the basis for Ahnaf's position that Tayammum is a complete Tahara in itself, not time-bound.

    Distance for Seeking Water

    • One mile in any direction is the baseline opinion.
    • Five scholarly opinions on the distance:
    • As far as the Adhan can be heard in an open area
    • As far as a loud voice can carry when yelling at full volume
    • Two miles in the direction of travel only
    • Two miles in any one chosen direction
    • Imam Abu Yusuf: Only as far as one's travel companions can still see him — not beyond their line of sight

    Tayammum for Specific Missed Prayers (Non-Traveller)

    Prayer Tayammum Permissible? Reason
    Salatul Janazah Yes (if non-Wali and fears missing it) No replacement exists for Janazah prayer
    Salatul Eid Yes (if fears missing it) No replacement exists for Eid prayer
    Salatul Jumu'ah No Zuhr Salah is its replacement
    • A Wali (next-of-kin) of the deceased cannot use Tayammum for Janazah, because he has the authority to ask the congregation to repeat the prayer.

    Sickness — Three Categories Permitting Tayammum

    1. Water itself is harmful — he knows that using water will make him sick
    2. Movement is harmful — water is not harmful but the physical act of going to water or performing Wudhu/Ghusl may cause him to fall or injure himself; however, if another person (wife, son, anyone) can assist, he must use water
    3. Complete inability to perform Wudhu alone — e.g., severe burns, full cast; two scholarly positions:
    4. Imam Abu Hanifa: He stops praying entirely until recovered, then performs Qada for all missed prayers

    5. Imam Abu Yusuf: He imitates the actions of Salah lying down, then repeats when recovered

    6. Ahnaf's general rule: Tayammum is permissible whenever performing Wudhu causes hardship.

    7. Imam Shafi'i's position: Tayammum is only permissible if a person genuinely fears death from using water.
    8. Dalil for Ahnaf: Quranic verse stating Allah wishes to remove Haraj (hardship) — Tayammum fulfils this purpose during sickness and travel.

    Method of Tayammum

    • Dalil: Hadith in Ibn Majah, narrated by Ammar ibn Yasir (رضي الله عنه) — the Prophet (ﷺ) commanded Muslims to strike the dust with open palms.
    • Method: Strike open palms on the ground, move slightly forward and back, lift hands, rub together to remove excess dust. First strike → wipe face. Second strike → wipe hands.
    • Ahnaf (Tarfain): Two strikes
    • Imam Malik, Imam Ahmad, Ishaaq ibn Rahwayh: Single strike (their dalil is another narration from Ammar ibn Yasir in Abu Dawud mentioning a single strike)

    • Same method applies whether Tayammum is for Wudhu, Janabat, Haid, or Nifas.

    • Dalil for Tayammum covering Haid/Nifas: Hadith in Sunan — a Bedouin woman told the Prophet (ﷺ) her community lives in the desert with no water for months, experiencing Haid and Nifas; he (ﷺ) told her to use the soil/earth (i.e., continue with Tayammum).

    Valid Substances for Tayammum

    Scholar Permissible Substances
    Imam Abu Hanifa & Imam Muhammad (Mufta Bihi) Anything from the genus of the earth that cannot be burned or dissolved in water: soil, sand, stone, gypsum, lime, coal, arsenic. Ashes excluded (can be dissolved). Wood/grass excluded (can burn). Metal excluded (can melt).
    Imam Abu Yusuf Soil and sand only
    Imam Shafi'i Only earth from which vegetation can grow; sand excluded
    • Dalil: Hadith in Bukhari — the Prophet (ﷺ) performed Tayammum from soil.

    Intention in Tayammum

    • Intention (Niyyah) is Fard in Tayammum according to Ahnaf — unlike Wudhu where it is only Mustahab.
    • Dalil: The word "Tayammum" itself comes from "Qasd" (intention/purpose), so the intention is built into the definition of the act.

    What Breaks Tayammum

    1. Everything that breaks Wudhu — because Tayammum is the replacement (Furu') of Wudhu; when the Asl is broken, the Furu' is broken too.
    2. Finding sufficient water:
    3. If water found is only enough to drink → Tayammum remains valid
    4. If water found is sufficient for Wudhu → Tayammum is broken
    5. If Tayammum was made for Ghusl and water found is only sufficient for Wudhu → Tayammum remains valid (not enough for Ghusl)

    Multiple Prayers with One Tayammum

    Scholar Position
    Ahnaf (and most Tawatin) Permissible — Tayammum is a complete Tahara in itself, not bound to a specific prayer or time
    Imam Shafi'i Only valid for the prayer it was made for (and its related Sunnah prayers); must redo Tayammum for the next prayer time
    • Ahnaf's dalil: (1) Hadith — earth is Tahara for Muslims even for 10 years; (2) Hadith of Bedouin woman — told to continue Tayammum for months. These prove it is a full, independent Tahara.

    Masail on Water Discovery and Forgetfulness

    Scenario Tarfain (Abu Hanifa + Muhammad) Abu Yusuf & Shafi'i
    Made Tayammum, forgot he had water, later remembered Need not repeat Salah Must repeat Salah
    Strong dominant opinion (Ghalabat al-Zann) that no water exists → does Tayammum → later finds water Salah valid —
    Has doubt water may exist, does Tayammum without searching → later finds water Must repeat Salah —
    • If travelling with a companion who has water → must ask before performing Tayammum.
    • If companion refuses → Tayammum is valid.
    • Imam Abu Yusuf: Asking is Wajib.
    • Tarfain + Imam Shafi'i: Asking is not Wajib, but should be done.

    Masa al-Khuffayn — Dalil and Scholarly Consensus

    • Dalil: Hadith Mutawatirah (narrated by a very large number of Sahabah).
    • Imam Ahmad: narrations from 40 Sahabah
    • Imam Hasan al-Basri: narrations from 70 Badri Sahabah
    • Allama Ayni: narrations from 67 Sahabah
    • Imam Abu Hanifa's warning: He stated he fears Kufr upon anyone who denies the permissibility of Masa al-Khuffayn, given the mutawatir level of evidence.
    • Dalil also from Wudhu chapter: Hadith of Mughirah ibn Shu'bah (رضي الله عنه) — the Prophet (ﷺ) performed Masa over his Khuffayn.

    Conditions for Masa al-Khuffayn

    1. The Khuff/sock must have been worn while in a state of complete Tahara (full Wudhu including washed feet).
    2. Masa applies only to Hadas (minor impurity requiring Wudhu) — NOT to Janabat (which requires Ghusl).
    3. The sock must cover up to and above the ankle bone.
    4. There must be no hole(s) equivalent to three fingertips or more — whether in one place or multiple smaller holes that collectively reach that size.

    Duration of Masa al-Khuffayn

    Status Duration
    Muqeem (resident) 1 day and 1 night (24 hours)
    Musafir (traveller) 3 days and 3 nights (72 hours)
    • When does the duration begin? From the moment the first Hadas (impurity) occurs after wearing the Khuff in Wudhu — not from when the Khuff was put on.
    • Example: Made Wudhu and wore socks at 6:20 PM. Broke Wudhu at 7:30 PM → duration of 24 hours begins at 7:30 PM.
    • Dalil for duration: Statement of Ali (رضي الله عنه) in response to a question — 3 days/nights for traveller, 1 day/night for resident.
    • Note: A hadith in Abu Dawud mentions 7 days for traveller — classified as Da'if (weak) and not adopted by Ahnaf.

    Method of Performing Masa al-Khuffayn

    • Wet the three middle fingers of the hand.
    • Place them on the top (upper surface) of the sock starting from the toe area.
    • Draw a straight line pulling upward toward the shin, stopping where the sock ends.
    • Fard: Using at least three fingers; wiping the top surface.
    • Important note: The Masa is performed on the top of the sock, not the bottom — even though the bottom is what contacts the ground. This is intentional and is cited by Fuqaha as a proof that Deen is not based on personal reasoning (Aql); we follow how the Prophet (ﷺ) did it.

    What Invalidates Masa al-Khuffayn

    1. Everything that breaks Wudhu → requires redoing Masa (not removing socks, just redo the wiping)
    2. Removing the Khuff/sock — even accidentally or during play
    3. Duration expiring — 24 hours (Muqeem) or 72 hours (Musafir)

    4. Ghusl becoming obligatory (Janabat, etc.) — must remove socks and perform full Ghusl

    5. After invalidation by sock removal or duration expiry: Must wash feet, then re-wear socks. Duration restarts from new Hadas.

    6. Clarification: If Wudhu is intact and only the sock was removed (e.g., accidentally), Wudhu is NOT broken — only the Masa ruling is broken. Simply wash the feet, re-wear the socks, and Wudhu is intact with a fresh duration.

    Conditions & Categories

    Categories of Tayammum Permissibility (Summary)

    Category Condition
    No water within one mile (various opinions on distance) Water unavailable
    Water present but unreachable Access blocked
    Danger in reaching water Human or animal threat
    Sickness — water itself harmful Direct harm from water
    Sickness — movement harmful Risk of injury during Wudhu
    Sickness — complete inability Requires Qada after recovery (Imam Abu Hanifa) or imitation of Salah (Imam Abu Yusuf)
    Janazah Salah at risk of being missed No replacement for Janazah
    Eid Salah at risk of being missed No replacement for Eid

    Khuff Conditions

    Condition Ruling
    Worn in state of full Wudhu Required
    Ankle bone covered Required
    Hole(s) less than three fingertips Masa valid
    Hole(s) equal to or exceeding three fingertips Masa invalid, must wash feet

    Scholarly Opinions

    On Tayammum Method (Strikes)

    • Ahnaf: Two strikes (face + hands)
    • Imam Malik, Imam Ahmad, Ishaaq ibn Rahwayh: One strike

    On Permissible Substances for Tayammum

    • Ahnaf Mufta Bihi: All earth-genus substances that cannot burn or dissolve
    • Imam Abu Yusuf: Soil and sand only
    • Imam Shafi'i: Only earth that can grow vegetation

    On Multiple Prayers with One Tayammum

    • Ahnaf: Unrestricted — Tayammum is complete Tahara
    • Imam Shafi'i: One prayer per Tayammum

    On Asking a Companion for Water

    • Imam Abu Yusuf: Wajib to ask
    • Imam Abu Hanifa, Imam Muhammad, Imam Shafi'i: Not Wajib but should ask

    On Masa al-Khuffayn Method

    • All four Madhabs agree it is permissible; difference lies in sock type and conditions
    • Wiping is on the top of the sock — unanimously established by Sunnah

    Hanafi Madhab — Whose Opinion is Mufta Bihi?

    Domain Mufta Bihi Opinion
    Ibadat (worship) Generally Imam Abu Hanifa
    Mu'amalat / court matters Generally Imam Abu Yusuf (12 years as Qadi)
    Ilm al-Faraid (inheritance) Generally Imam Muhammad
    • The Mufta Bihi opinion is not one scholar's individual ruling — it is the consensus of the majority of the Fuqaha of that generation, applied based on the needs and circumstances of their time.
    • Imam Abu Hanifa himself said: "If my opinion contradicts the Sunnah or the Quran, throw it out."

    Lessons & Practical Application

    1. Take Sharia's concessions. When Allah has given a Rukhsa (concession) — whether Tayammum, Qasr Salah for a traveller, or Masa al-Khuffayn — it is an act of Shukr (gratitude) to Allah to use it. Refusing the concession out of excessive caution is not always more virtuous; it can actually mean not appreciating what Allah has given.

    2. Jumu'ah vs. Janazah/Eid. If you fear missing Jumu'ah, you cannot do Tayammum — go make Wudhu and pray whatever portion you catch, then pray Zuhr if Jumu'ah is fully missed. There is no such fallback for Janazah or Eid, which is why Tayammum is allowed for those.

    3. Ghalabat al-Zann (dominant opinion) matters in Tayammum. If you are highly confident no water exists based on facts (known terrain, desert, open flat land), you may do Tayammum immediately. If you have any doubt, you must search first.

    4. Socks and Masa in practice. When wiping over socks, use three wet fingers on the top surface and draw upward from toes to shin. Do not wipe the bottom of the sock, even though it seems more logical — this is the established Sunnah and is itself a proof that Fiqh is not based on personal reasoning.

    5. Know your duration. Your 24-hour (or 72-hour) clock for Masa al-Khuffayn begins when you first break Wudhu after putting on your socks — not when you put them on. If you remove your socks before the duration is up, wash your feet and start fresh.

    6. Post-surgery and illness rulings. If a person cannot wash certain body parts due to surgery or injury, they should use whatever is possible — whether assisted washing with a wet cloth or Masa on the accessible areas. For parts under a cast, Masa over the cast itself is valid. Individual cases may need specific inquiry from a scholar.

    7. Purpose of learning Fiqh. As emphasised by the teacher: the goal is not to score well on a test, but to preserve the knowledge, act upon it, and convey it to others. Even 10–15 minutes of daily review can make the material permanent.
