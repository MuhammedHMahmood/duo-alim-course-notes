## Nahw 201

**In a nutshell:** The class reviewed all seven types of مفعول and the full list of منصوبات, then covered the rules of استثناء in non-affirmative speech, and completed the chapter on إضافة.

### Key Points

- **حال** answers "how?" and describes the state during an action — it is منصوب but is *not* called مفعول; e.g., اسْتَيْقَظَتْ فَاطِمَةُ بَاكِيَةً.
- **مفعول مطلق** must share the same root (مصدر) as its verb — if the root differs, it cannot be مفعول مطلق.
- **تمييز** removes إبهام (ambiguity) from a noun or pronoun; e.g., اللَّهُمَّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا — عِلْمًا is the تمييز.
- In **كلام غير موجب** (speech with negation, prohibition, or interrogation), the مستثنى after إلا may be either منصوب or a بدل matching the مبدل منه's إعراب — both are valid.
- **مستثنى مفرق** (مستثنى منه absent) takes its إعراب from the عامل, as if إلا is not there.
- In **إضافة معنوية**, the hidden حرف جر is مِنْ (same جنس), فِي (mudaf ilayh is a ظرف), or لام (otherwise); annexing to a معرفة gives the مضاف تعريف, while annexing to a نكرة gives تخصيص.

### Vocabulary

| Arabic Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| مَنْصُوبَات | Words in the accusative/nasb case |
| حَال | Circumstantial accusative — describes state during action |
| تَمْيِيز | Specification; removes ambiguity (إبهام) |
| كَلَام غَيْر مُوجَب | Speech containing negation, prohibition, or interrogation |
| مُسْتَثْنَى مُفَرَّق | Exception where مستثنى منه is absent |
| إِضَافَة لَفْظِيَّة | Formal idafa — مضاف is a صيغة صفت annexed to its own معمول; benefit is تخفيف |

### Easy to Mix Up

- **حال vs. مفعول مطلق:** both are منصوب, but مفعول مطلق *must* share the verb's root — حال has no such requirement.

## Tafseer 201

**In a nutshell:** The class covered Surah Al-Baqarah ayat 104–115, addressing the prohibition of saying "راعنا," the wisdom behind نسخ, the falsehood of exclusive paradise claims by Jews and Christians, and the grave injustice of preventing worship in Allah's houses.

### Key Points

- Allah prohibited the word **رَاعِنَا** because Jews exploited it as a pun-insult against the Prophet ﷺ; believers were commanded to use **أُنْظُرْنَا** instead.
- **نسخ** (abrogation) is not contradiction — Allah replaces a ruling with one better or equal, either to remove hardship or to increase the believers' reward.
- Neither Jews nor Christians have proof for their exclusive paradise claims — Jannah belongs to whoever **أَسْلَمَ وَجْهَهُ لِلَّه** (submits fully to Allah), has إيمان, and does good deeds with إحسان.
- The most unjust person is one who prevents worship in **مَسَاجِد الله** or works to destroy them — their consequence is خِزْي in this world and great punishment in the hereafter.
- Ayah 115 was revealed for a traveler unable to find the قبلة: Allah owns East and West, so wherever one turns, **ثَمَّ وَجْهُ الله**.

### Vocabulary

| Arabic Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| نَسْخ | Abrogation — replacing one ruling with another |
| أُمْنِيَّة / أَمَانِيّ | Wish, fantasy; baseless claim |
| أَسْلَمَ وَجْهَهُ | Submitted his entire self to Allah |
| مُحْسِن | One who does good deeds in the best manner |
| خِزْي | Disgrace, humiliation |
| أَظْلَم | Most unjust (superlative of ظَلَمَ) |

### Easy to Mix Up

- **بَلَى vs. نَعَم:** بَلَى negates a preceding *negative* statement and affirms the positive — it cannot be used to simply say "yes."

## Fiqh 201

**In a nutshell:** The class covered the categories of شهيد, the burial rulings that distinguish them from others, the مرتث exception, who is not prayed upon, and the rulings for praying inside or on top of the كعبة.

### Key Points

- A **شهيد دنيا وآخرة** (battlefield martyr) receives no غسل but is given كفن and صلاة الجنازة, and is buried with their blood and clothing intact; non-cloth items (armor, weapons) must be removed.
- A **مرتث** is a wounded fighter removed from the battlefield while conscious who then received treatment or survived one full prayer time — they lose the hukm of شهيد for burial purposes.
- **صلاة الجنازة is not performed** for بغاة (rebels) or قطاع الطريق (highway robbers) — to publicly express disapproval of their destructive actions.
- According to the Ahnaf, praying **inside the كعبة** is permissible for both فرض and نوافل in any direction; a مقتدي standing directly *ahead* of the imam (back to the imam's face) has an invalid prayer.
- Praying **on top of the كعبة** is valid but makruh, based on a hadith in Tirmidhi listing seven places where the Prophet ﷺ prohibited prayer.

### Vocabulary

| Arabic Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| مُرْتَث | Wounded fighter removed alive from battlefield who regained consciousness |
| بُغَاة | Rebels who rise in armed insurrection against the legitimate Muslim ruler |
| كَفَن | Burial shroud |
| صَلَاة الْغَيْب | Funeral prayer performed for someone buried elsewhere (in absentia) |
| دِيَة | Blood money; financial compensation for a killing |
| مَكْرُوه | Disliked act — valid but blameworthy |
