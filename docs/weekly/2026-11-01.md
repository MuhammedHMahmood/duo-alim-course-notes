## Fiqh 201

**In a nutshell:** This week covered Sahw rulings for standing into an extra rakah and doubting the rakah count, then introduced the progressive prayer concessions for sick persons.

### Key Points

- **Standing for a fifth rakah** before completing its Sajdah: return immediately to sitting, complete Tashahud, and perform سَجْدَةُ السَّهْو to preserve the Fard salah.
- **After completing the Sajdah of the fifth rakah**, the Fard converts to Nafil (تَحَوَّلَتْ صَلَاتُهُ نَفْلًا) and a sixth rakah must be added, since no valid Nafil exists with an odd number of rakahs.
- **Doubt about rakah count** for the first time requires breaking and restarting; if habitual, act on غَالِبُ الظَّنّ (dominant thought) and perform Sajda Sahu at the end.
- **Salatul Mareed** follows a hierarchy: standing → sitting → laying down (side facing Qibla preferred) → إِشَارَة with the head; if even that is impossible, salah must be delayed.
- **Recovering mid-salah** from a weaker state (إِشَارَة) back to standing requires restarting the salah (اسْتِئْنَاف الصَّلَاة); deteriorating to a lower position mid-salah is always permitted without restarting.

### Vocabulary

| Arabic Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| الْقَعْدَةُ الْأَخِيرَة (Qadatul Akhira) | The final sitting before Tasleem |
| غَالِبُ الظَّنّ (Ghalib az-Zann) | Dominant/preponderant thought |
| إِشَارَة (Ishara) | Head-gesture indication replacing Ruku/Sujood |
| اسْتِئْنَاف الصَّلَاة (Isti'naf as-Salah) | Restarting the prayer from the beginning |
| تَحَوَّلَتْ صَلَاتُهُ نَفْلًا | His prayer has converted to voluntary prayer |

### Easy to Mix Up

- **Fifth rakah recalled before vs. after Sajdah:** before — sit down and save the Fard; after — the Fard is gone, convert to Nafil and add a sixth rakah.
