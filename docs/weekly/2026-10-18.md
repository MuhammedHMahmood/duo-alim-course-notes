## Nahw 201

**In a nutshell:** The class finished the المنادى categories, then introduced المفعول فيه (ظرف) and المفعول لأجله.

### Key Points

- **التحذير** (warning) and **الاشتغال** (occupied-verb construction) are two cases where the verb must be dropped obligatorily (حذف وجوبًا), not optionally.
- A **منادى مفرد معرفة** — meaning non-مضاف, not necessarily singular — is مبني على الضمة; مضاف, شبه مضاف, ناكرة غير معيّنة, and معرّف بالـ types are all منصوب.
- When the منادى has الـ, يَا cannot precede it directly — insert **أيُّها** (masc.) or **أيَّتها** (fem.) as a separator.
- **تَرْخِيم** drops the final letter(s) of a منادى for ease; the trimmed form takes either ضمة or its original vowel: يَا حَارُ or يَا حَارِ.
- **المفعول لأجله** states the reason for an action; it is منصوب with لام implied (e.g., قَعَدْتُ جُبُنًا — I stayed back out of cowardice).

### Vocabulary

| Arabic Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| الاشتغال | Occupied-verb construction; fronted noun governed by a dropped verb |
| ناكرة مقصودة | Indefinite but specific addressee — takes مبني على الضمة |
| تَرْخِيم | Dropping final letter(s) of a منادى for ease |
| ظرف زمان / مكان | Adverb of time / place; منصوب with في implied |
| المفعول لأجله | Adverb of reason; منصوب with لام implied |

### Easy to Mix Up

- **ظرف مكان مبهم** (e.g., خَلْف, فَوْق) takes منصوب with في hidden; **ظرف مكان محدود** (e.g., الدَّار) uses an explicit في and the noun is مجرور.

## Tafseer 201

**In a nutshell:** Ayaat 78–86 of Surah Al-Baqarah exposed the crimes of the ignorant Jewish masses, their corrupt scholars, and the covenant Banu Isra'eel broke.

### Key Points

- The **أُمِّيُّون** had no knowledge of the Torah — only fabricated **أَمَانِيّ** (wishful thinking) fed to them by their rabbis, such as the claim that hellfire would only touch them for a few days.
- The corrupt scholars committed two simultaneous crimes: **تحريف الكتاب** (distorting scripture with their own hands) and **الكذب على الله** (attributing fabrications to Allah) — all for forbidden worldly gain (سُحْت).
- **بَلَى** (Ayah 80) is a particle that negates the prior false claim and affirms the truth — directly refuting their assertion about limited punishment.
- Whoever earns evil until **أَحَاطَتْ بِهِ خَطِيئَتُه** (sin encircles him from every side, closing all paths of salvation) is a permanent resident of the Fire.
- The **مِيثَاق** taken from Banu Isra'eel included tawheed, honoring parents, caring for orphans and the poor, kind speech, salah, and zakah — all of which they violated.

### Vocabulary

| Arabic Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| أُمِّيّ | Illiterate person; one who cannot read or verify scripture |
| أَمَانِيّ | Wishful thinking; unfounded false hopes |
| بَلَى | Negates a prior claim and affirms what follows |
| تَحْرِيف | Deliberate distortion of scripture |
| سُحْت | Forbidden, illicit gain |
| مِيثَاق | Solemn binding covenant |

## Fiqh 201

**In a nutshell:** The class completed the Masa'il of 12, opened the chapter on making up missed prayers, and covered the prohibited times for salah.

### Key Points

- The **مسائل الاثنا عشر** rule: if any of twelve specified conditions arises *after* the tashahud sitting is complete, the operative Hanafi opinion (Sahibain) is that the salah is **valid**; before that point, salah is batil by agreement.
- A **صاحب ترتيب** (five or fewer missed prayers) must pray qada in order before the current salah — **ترتيب** is wajib in the Hanafi madhab and drops only once missed prayers exceed five.
- The three **absolute prohibition times** are: sunrise (طلوع الشمس), sunset (غروب الشمس), and the sun's exact zenith (الاستواء) — no fard, nafl, janazah, or sajda-e-tilawat is permitted at these times.
- After Fajr salah and after Asr salah, **نوافل** are prohibited, but qada prayers and salat al-janazah remain permitted.
- A person unconscious for five prayers or fewer must make qada upon recovery; unconscious for six or more, the obligation is waived.

### Vocabulary

| Arabic Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| صاحب ترتيب | One with five or fewer missed prayers; must pray qada in order |
| ترتيب | Obligatory sequential ordering of qada before the current salah |
| كثرة القضاء | More than five missed prayers — causes tartib to drop |
| الاستواء / زوال | Sun at its exact zenith — one of three absolute prohibition times |
| بناء | Continuing salah after wudu breaks, under 13 conditions |

### Easy to Mix Up

- The **three absolute prohibition times** ban *all* salah; the post-Fajr and post-Asr restriction only bans *nawafil* — qada and janazah prayers are still valid after Fajr and Asr.
