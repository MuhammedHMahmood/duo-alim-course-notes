# Tafseer 201 - Study Notes
**Date:** 2026-09-17
**Surah Covered:** Surah Al-Baqarah, Ayat 34–39 (Page 50)

---

## Session Overview

This session covered Surah Al-Baqarah ayat 34–39, focusing on the command to the angels to prostrate before Adam (AS), the refusal of Iblis, the settlement of Adam and Hawa in Jannah, the temptation and descent to earth, Adam's repentance and its acceptance, and the closing ayat establishing the principle of guidance and its consequences.

---

## Key Themes and Topics

1. **The Prostration of Angels to Adam (AS)** — another layer of honor added to Adam following the previous session's theme of his being granted the khilafah and 'ilm.
2. **Iblis's Refusal** — his arrogance, his dislike of Allah's command, and how this made him a kafir.
3. **Life in Jannah** — Adam and Hawa's settlement in paradise, its freedoms and the single restriction.
4. **The Forbidden Tree** — differing scholarly interpretations; what is and is not established in the Quran.
5. **The Temptation and Exit from Jannah** — Shaytan's role, the dual responsibility of Adam and Hawa (refuting the notion that Hawa alone was responsible).
6. **The Descent (Ihbat)** — who was commanded to descend and the enmity established between humanity and Shaytan.
7. **Tawbah of Adam (AS)** — how the words of repentance were revealed to Adam, the distinction between *taba ila* and *taba 'ala*, and lessons for seeking forgiveness.
8. **The Universal Principle** — whoever follows Allah's guidance has no fear or grief; whoever denies it is among the people of Hellfire.

## Main Tafseer Points and Explanations

### 1. Connection to Previous Ayat (al-Munasabah)
The previous ayat established that Allah honored Adam (AS) with *al-khilafah* on earth and granted him *'ilmun ghazir* — knowledge the angels could not encompass. This new set of ayat adds yet another layer of honor: Allah commanded the angels to prostrate before Adam. The sheikh notes this is the most *azhar* (obvious and clear) manifestation of *tashrifat wa takrim* (respect and honor) given to the human species, represented in the origin of humanity — Adam (AS).

### 2. The Command to Prostrate (Ayah 34)
**وَإِذْ قُلْنَا لِلْمَلَائِكَةِ اسْجُدُوا لِآدَمَ**

- Allah commanded all the angels: *usjudu li-adam* — prostrate to Adam.
- This prostration was **sujud tahiyyah wa ta'zim** (greeting and respect), **not** *sujud 'ibadah* (worship).
- Prostration of worship is exclusively for Allah; no other prostration of worship is permissible.
- All angels obeyed — from Jibreel down to every angel.

### 3. Iblis's Refusal
**فَسَجَدُوا إِلَّا إِبْلِيسَ أَبَى وَاسْتَكْبَرَ وَكَانَ مِنَ الْكَافِرِينَ**

- Iblis (*aba*) refused and (*istakbara*) became arrogant.
- He *istaqbaha amrallah* — he found Allah's command reprehensible/disliked it.
- Because of this *iba'* (refusal) and *istikbar* (arrogance), he became *minal kafirin* — among the disbelievers.
- **Important note on Iblis's nature:** He was from the *jinn*, not from the angels. The Quran makes this explicit: *"wa kana minal jinni fa-fasaqa 'an amri rabbih"* (Surah Al-Kahf). Despite being a jinn, he had elevated himself through worship to dwell with the angels in the heavens, until Allah tested him at the creation of Adam.

### 4. Settlement in Jannah (Ayah 35)
**وَقُلْنَا يَا آدَمُ اسْكُنْ أَنْتَ وَزَوْجُكَ الْجَنَّةَ**

- After honoring Adam, Allah settled him and his wife Hawa in paradise.
- They were told: *kula minha raghadan* — eat from it in abundance, in plenty, from wherever you wish.
- One restriction only: **do not approach one specific tree.**
- If they were to approach it, they would be among the *zalimin* — those who wrong themselves by disobeying Allah.
- *Zulm* here means injustice to oneself through disobedience — a broader meaning than mere injustice.

### 5. The Forbidden Tree — Scholarly Interpretations
The Quran does not specify which tree it was. Scholarly opinions include:
- **Ibn Abbas (RA):** It was *al-karmah* — the grapevine (from which wine comes).
- **Others:** It was the wheat plant.
- **Conclusion:** No single interpretation is definitively established from the Quran or a sahih hadith. *Allah knows best.*

### 6. Iblis Tempts Adam and Hawa (Ayah 36)
**فَأَزَلَّهُمَا الشَّيْطَانُ عَنْهَا**

- Shaytan caused them both to slip (*azallahuma*) — the pronoun *huma* refers to **both** Adam and Hawa.
- He tempted them (*aghwahuma bil-akli minha*) to eat from the forbidden tree.
- As a result, *akhrajahuma mimma kana fihi* — he took them out of what they had been in (the blessings and delights of Jannah).

**On the pronoun *'anha*:** Two valid interpretations exist:
- If *'anha* refers to *al-shajarah* (the tree): Shaytan tempted them to eat from the tree, which caused their exit from Jannah.
- If *'anha* refers to *al-jannah* (paradise): Shaytan distanced them from Jannah itself.
Both interpretations are linguistically and contextually valid.

**Critical clarification — Was only Hawa responsible?**
This was explicitly addressed and rejected. The Quran uses *huma* (dual — both) throughout: *fa-azallahuma*, *fa-aghwahuma*, *fa-akala minha* (Surah Al-A'raf uses the dual form). Furthermore, Surah Ta-Ha states clearly: *"wa 'asa adamu rabbahu fa-ghawa"* — Adam (AS) disobeyed his Lord. Attributing the sin solely to Hawa as a woman is a **wrong and baseless interpretation** with no Quranic or hadith support.

**On how Shaytan tempted them:** The Quran does not specify the method. Stories of Shaytan taking the form of a snake or an old man are not established definitively. Since Allah has given Shaytan the ability to take different forms, such stories are possible but cannot be stated with certainty. We must adhere strictly to the wording of the Quran and sahih hadith.

### 7. The Descent — Ihbat (Ayah 36–37)
**اهْبِطُوا مِنْهَا جَمِيعًا**

- The plural command *ihbitu* (descend — you all) indicates more than two: **Adam, Hawa, and Iblis** were all commanded to descend.
- *Ba'dukum li-ba'din 'aduww* — some of you are enemies to others: Shaytan is the enemy of Adam's descendants. This parallels: *"inna ash-shaytana lakum 'aduwwun fattakhidhuhu 'aduwwa"* (Surah Fatir 35:6).
- *Lakum fil-ardi mustaqarrun wa mata'un ila hin* — for you on earth is a place of settlement and enjoyment until an appointed time (*ajal*).
- Tradition holds Adam (AS) descended in Sri Lanka (historically part of the Indian subcontinent). *Allah knows best.*

### 8. Adam's Repentance and Its Acceptance (Ayah 37)
**فَتَلَقَّى آدَمُ مِن رَّبِّهِ كَلِمَاتٍ فَتَابَ عَلَيْهِ**

- Adam (AS) continuously supplicated day and night seeking forgiveness.
- Allah, in His mercy, *alhama* — inspired/revealed to Adam specific words of supplication.
- Adam received these *kalimat* (words) and supplicated with them.
- The mufassirun identify these words as the supplication in Surah Al-A'raf 7:23:
  > *"Rabbana zalamna anfusana wa in lam taghfir lana wa tarhamna la-nakunanna minal-khasirin"*
  > "Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy on us, we will surely be among the losers."
- *Fataba alayhi* — Allah accepted the repentance of Adam. Hawa's tawbah is also accepted, as she is under Adam — they both supplicated (*rabbana zalamna* uses the dual).
- *Innallaha huwa at-tawwabur-rahim* — Allah is immensely accepting of repentance and vast in mercy.

### 9. The Repeated Command to Descend (Ayah 38)
The command to descend is repeated for emphasis (*ta'kid*) and to clarify that Adam's and his descendants' permanent residence is the earth, not Jannah. This repetition also establishes the universal principle that follows.

### 10. The Universal Principle of Guidance (Ayat 38–39)
**فَإِمَّا يَأْتِيَنَّكُم مِّنِّي هُدًى فَمَن تَبِعَ هُدَايَ فَلَا خَوْفٌ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا هُمْ يَحْزَنُونَ**

- If guidance comes from Allah — through messengers or revealed books — those who follow it will have **no fear and no grief in the Hereafter**.
- Those who disbelieve and deny Allah's signs (*jahadu bima anzaltu wa bima arsaltu*) are the companions of Hellfire, dwelling therein forever.
- *Al-jahim* — one of the names of Hellfire, meaning "the flaming fire."

## Grammar and Language Notes

### 1. *Ala* — Harf Tanbi
The word **أَلَا** (*ala*) that opens certain ayat is categorized in *i'rab* as **harfu tanbi** — a particle of alert/notice. It is not a verb, not a noun; it is simply a letter (*harf*) that draws attention. It has no position in *tarkib* (grammatical structure).

### 2. Verb Form: *Usjudu* / *Kula*
- *Usjudu*: from *sajada yasjudu*, the *fi'l amr* (command form).
- *Kula*: from *akala ya'kulu aklan* — to eat.
  - Singular masculine: **كُلْ** (*kul*)
  - Singular feminine: **كُلِي** (*kuli*)
  - Dual: **كُلَا** (*kula*) ← used in this ayah (*wa kula minha raghadan*)
  - Plural: **كُلُوا** (*kulu*)

### 3. Dual Form (*Tasniyah*) Throughout Ayah 36
The consistent use of the dual pronoun *huma* (them both) and dual verb forms throughout the ayat covering the temptation and exit from Jannah grammatically and textually confirms that both Adam AND Hawa were involved — not Hawa alone.

### 4. *Taba ila* vs. *Taba 'ala*
- **تَابَ إِلَى** (*taba ila*): to repent to Allah — the act of the servant turning back to Allah.
- **تَابَ عَلَى** (*taba 'ala*): to accept repentance — an act attributed **only to Allah**.
- *Fataba alayhi* in the ayah uses *'ala*, indicating Allah accepted Adam's repentance.

### 5. *Tawwab* — Ism al-Mubalagha
*Tawwabun* is on the *fa''al* pattern (*Ism al-Mubalagha*), denoting an intensive/superlative degree: Allah is *kasiru al-qabul lit-tawbah* — extremely, abundantly accepting of repentance.

### 6. Plural in *Ihbitu*
The plural command *اهْبِطُوا* (ihbitu — descend, you all) as opposed to a dual, signals that **three** parties were being addressed: Adam, Hawa, and Iblis.

## Lessons and Practical Takeaways

1. **The honor of humanity:** The command to the angels to prostrate to Adam is the clearest manifestation of the dignity Allah has given to humans. This should cultivate self-respect and a sense of responsibility in every believer.

2. **Arrogance is the root of kufr:** Iblis had immense worship and proximity to Allah, yet a single act of arrogance and refusal of Allah's command led him to kufr. Arrogance — *istikbar* — is among the most dangerous spiritual diseases.

3. **Sujud of worship is exclusively for Allah:** The angels' prostration to Adam was *tahiyyah* (greeting/respect). The *sujud 'ibadah* (worship) is *haram* for anyone other than Allah.

4. **The best supplications are from the Quran and Sunnah:**
   - Supplications of the Anbiya as mentioned in the Quran (Adam, Ayyub, Yunus — *la ilaha illa anta subhanaka inni kuntu min al-zalimin*) are the highest.
   - Supplications from authenticated hadith of the Prophet (SAW) for every time of day and night.
   - If a person in distress is granted a specific supplication in their heart, that too is a gift from Allah.

5. **Seek forgiveness at blessed times:** Tahajjud, after fard salah, on Fridays, in the evenings at Maghrib — these are times specifically mentioned in hadith for accepted du'a.

6. **Repentance is always open:** Adam (AS) sinned, was expelled from Jannah, yet Allah accepted his tawbah. The door of tawbah is open as long as we are alive. *Innallaha huwa at-tawwabur-rahim.*

7. **Shaytan is our enemy — treat him accordingly:** *"Inna ash-shaytana lakum 'aduwwun fattakhidhuhu 'aduwwa"* — Take him as an enemy. Don't rationalize or normalize his whispers.

8. **The world is temporary:** *Mata'un ila hin* — the enjoyment of this world is only until the appointed time. Our real home, the goal, is Jannah.

9. **Guidance = no fear, no grief:** Following Allah's guidance is the only guarantee of security in the Hereafter. Denying it leads to eternal loss.

10. **Do not add to Quranic narratives without evidence:** Stories about how Shaytan tempted Adam and Hawa (becoming a snake, old man, etc.) are not established. We must restrict ourselves to what the Quran and sahih hadith state and present other things only as possible interpretations, not established facts.

## Historical Context and Stories Mentioned

- **Iblis's origin:** Iblis was from the *jinn*, not the angels, as established in Surah Al-Kahf. Despite this, he had elevated himself through worship to reside with the angels in the heavens, until the test of Adam's creation exposed his arrogance.

- **The forbidden tree:** Multiple scholarly opinions exist — Ibn Abbas (RA) identified it as *al-karmah* (grapevine); others said wheat. The Quran and sahih hadith do not definitively name it. Allah knows best.

- **Adam's descent on earth:** Traditional scholarly opinion holds that Adam (AS) descended to what is today Sri Lanka (historically part of the Indian subcontinent). This is a scholarly interpretation, not definitively established.

- **The supplication of Adam:** Identified by the mufassirun as the du'a in Surah Al-A'raf 7:23 — *"Rabbana zalamna anfusana..."* — which was revealed to Adam to teach him how to seek Allah's forgiveness.

## Q&A Highlights

**Q: Was it only Hawa (AS) who ate from the tree, or both Adam and Hawa?**
> **A:** Both. The Quran uses the dual form *huma* throughout — *fa-azallahuma*, *fa-aghwahuma* — and Surah Al-A'raf states *fa-akala minha* (dual). Furthermore, Surah Ta-Ha explicitly states *"wa 'asa adamu rabbahu fa-ghawa"* — Adam disobeyed his Lord. Making Hawa solely responsible has no Quranic or hadith basis and is a wrong interpretation.

**Q: Did Shaytan take the form of a snake or old man to tempt them?**
> **A:** This is not established in the Quran or a sahih hadith. Shaytan does have the God-given ability to change forms, so it is *possible*, but we cannot state it with certainty. We must adhere strictly to what is textually established. Adding unverified details to Quranic stories can create confusion and false conclusions — as with the incorrect attribution of blame to Hawa.

**Q: Was Iblis not already expelled from Jannah before Adam and Hawa were told to descend?**
> **A:** Iblis's original station was in the highest heavens alongside the angels. The place where Adam and Hawa were settled and from which all three were commanded to descend (*ihbitu jami'an*) was Jannah — that same elevated realm. Different ayat address the command to Iblis and to Adam/Hawa in different ways (sometimes separately, sometimes together), but all were commanded to leave.

**Q: What does *al-jahim* mean?**
> **A:** *Al-jahim* is one of the names of Hellfire, meaning the intensely flaming fire. *Jahannam* is another name for Hellfire. These names appear throughout the Quran referring to the same reality.

**Q: In the last ayah, can "those are the people of the fire" be translated as referring to people generally?**
> **A:** Yes — it refers broadly to all who disbelieve and deny Allah's signs, not just one group. They are *ashab al-nar* — the companions of the Fire — dwelling therein forever.
