## Session Overview

This session continued the study of **المنصوبات** (mansoobat — the mansub nouns), specifically focusing on two major topics: the obligatory dropping of the fi'l (*wujub al-hazf*) in the cases of **التحذير** (tahdhir — warning) and **الاشتغال** (ishtighaal — the occupied verb construction), followed by a detailed treatment of **المنادى** (al-munadha — the vocative), including its five categories and their respective i'rab rules. The session also covered لام الاستغاثة (lam al-istigatha) and ألف الاستغاثة (alif al-istigatha).

---

## Grammar Rules Covered

### 1. المنصوبات (Al-Mansoobat — The Mansub Nouns)
- There are **twelve** mansoobat in total.
- Previously covered: مفعول مطلق (maf'ool mutlaq) and مفعول به (maf'ool bihi).
- The first of the maf'ool bihi cases is **سماعي** (sami'i — heard/irregular); the remaining are **قياسي** (qiyasi — analogical/regular).

---

### 2. وجوب حذف الفعل (Wujub Hazf al-Fi'l — Obligatory Dropping of the Verb)

The verb (*fi'l*) must be dropped in four places (وجوبًا في أربعة مواضع). Two are covered this session:

---

#### أ. التحذير (At-Tahdhir — Warning/Alerting)

**Definition:** To warn someone of a danger, using a noun that is the مفعول به (maf'ool bihi) of the dropped verb **اِتَّقِ** (ittaqi — beware of / protect yourself from).

**Why the fi'l is dropped:** There is urgency — no time to mention the full verb.

**Two methods of Tahdhir:**

| Method | Example | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| إياك + والـ + محذَّر منه | إِيَّاكَ وَالأَسَدَ | "Protect yourself from the lion" |
| Repetition of محذَّر منه | الطَّرِيقَ الطَّرِيقَ | "The road! The road!" |

**Grammatical analysis of إِيَّاكَ وَالأَسَدَ:**
- Original sentence: اِتَّقِ وَالأَسَدَ (with fi'l ittaqi present)
- كَ (ka) is a **ضمير متصل** (zameer mutasil — attached pronoun); since the fi'l was dropped, it could not remain attached, so it was converted to a **ضمير منفصل** (zameer munfasil — detached pronoun) → إِيَّاكَ
- إِيَّاكَ = maf'ool bihi of the dropped fi'l muqaddar (اِتَّقِ)

**Three elements of Tahdhir:**

| Term | Arabic | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Warner | مُحَذِّر | The one giving the warning |
| Person warned | مُحَذَّر | The one being warned |
| Thing warned from | مُحَذَّر مِنْه | The danger itself (e.g., الأسد, الكلب, الطريق) |

---

#### ب. الاشتغال (Al-Ishtighaal — The Occupied Verb Construction)

**Definition:** When a noun appears at the beginning of the sentence in the mansub position and the verb that follows is already "occupied" (مشغول) with a pronoun referring back to that noun, a separate amil (governing verb) must be supplied for the initial noun — and then dropped obligatorily.

**Example:**

زَيْدًا ضَرَبْتُهُ

- **زَيْدًا** = maf'ool bihi (mansub) — requires an amil
- **ضَرَبْتُهُ** = the fi'l is occupied with **هُ** (the pronoun referring to Zaid) → cannot also govern Zaid
- Solution: mentally supply a second fi'l as amil → ضَرَبْتُ زَيْدًا ضَرَبْتُهُ
- The supplied fi'l creates unpleasant repetition, so it is dropped obligatorily (حذف وجوبًا)
- Result: زَيْدًا ضَرَبْتُهُ

**Second example:**

زَيْدًا رَأَيْتُهُ (Zaidan ra'aytuhu — "Zaid, I saw him")

**Key rule — الضمير لا يتقدم على مرجعه:**
> A pronoun (ضمير) cannot precede its referent (مرجع).

- **مرجع** (marja') = the noun the pronoun refers back to → must come first (مقدَّم)
- **ضمير** = must come after (مؤخَّر)
- This is why we cannot simply reorder: هُ refers to زيد, so زيد must appear before هُ

**Sentence pattern that signals ishtighaal:** Any sentence of the form **Noun + Verb + Pronoun** where the pronoun refers back to the initial noun.

### 3. المنادى (Al-Munadha — The Vocative)

**Definition:** The noun addressed when calling someone. It is the maf'ool bihi of the dropped verb **أَدْعُو** (ad'u — I call).

**Harf An-Nida (حرف النداء — Particle of Calling):**
- The most common is **يَا** (ya)
- Harf nida stands in place of (قائم مقام) the dropped fi'l أَدْعُو

**When Harf Nida is dropped:**
- When **فعل أمر** (Amr) or **فعل نهي** (Nahi) follows the munada, the harf nida may be omitted because the meaning of calling is already implicit in direct address.
- Quranic example: **يُوسُفُ أَعْرِضْ عَنْ هَذَا** — originally يَا يُوسُفُ; the يَا is dropped because the Amr أَعْرِضْ carries its meaning.

---

### 4. أحكام المنادى (Ahkam al-Munadha — Rules Governing the Vocative)

The munada takes one of two states depending on its type:

#### أ. مبني على الضمة (Mabni on Damma — "Marfu'")
When the munada is:

**1. مفرد معرفة (Mufrad Ma'rifa — Non-Muzaf and Definite)**
- **مفرد** here does NOT mean singular only; it means **not muzaf** — includes singular, dual (تسنية), and plural (جمع)
- **معرفة** includes both proper nouns and a nakirah maqsuda (a specifically intended person — see below)

| Example | Notes |
|---|---|
| يَا زَيْدُ | Singular proper noun |
| يَا زَيْدَانِ | Dual — still "mufrad" here because not muzaf |
| يَا زَيْدُونَ | Plural — still "mufrad" here because not muzaf |
| يَا رَجُلُ | Nakirah, but maqsuda (specific person addressed) → treated as ma'rifa |

> **Note:** يَا رَجُلُ is grammatically nakirah, yet it is marfu because you are addressing a specific, identified individual — making it effectively definite in the context of address.

---

#### ب. منصوب (Mansub)
When the munada is any of the following:

**2. مضاف (Muzaf — in construct state)**

| Example | Meaning |
|---|---|
| يَا عَبْدَ اللهِ | O servant of Allah |
| يَا ابْنَ زَيْدٍ | O son of Zaid |
| يَا ابْنَ آدَمَ | O son of Adam |

**3. مشابه المضاف (Mushabbah bil-Muzaf — resembling the muzaf)**
- A word that is incomplete in meaning without what follows it, just as a muzaf is incomplete without its mudaf ilayh.

| Example | Meaning |
|---|---|
| يَا طَالِعًا جَبَلًا | O one climbing a mountain |

**4. ناكرة غير معيّنة (Nakirah Ghayr Mu'ayyana — indefinite, non-specific)**
- When the caller cannot specify a particular individual.

| Example | Meaning | Context |
|---|---|---|
| يَا رَجُلًا خُذْ بِيَدِي | O (some) man, take my hand | Blind person calling for any helper |
| يَا طَالِبًا افْتَحِ الْبَابَ | O (some) student, open the door | Calling any student generally |

**5. المعرَّف بـ(ال) (Mu'arraf bil-Lam — having the definite article)**
- Harf nida cannot directly precede a noun with الـ.
- A **فاصل** (separator) must be inserted between them:
  - **أَيُّهَا** for muzakkar (masculine)
  - **أَيَّتُهَا** for mu'annas (feminine)

| Example | Meaning |
|---|---|
| يَا أَيُّهَا الرَّجُلُ | O the man |
| يَا أَيَّتُهَا الْمَرْأَةُ | O the woman |

---

### 5. لام الاستغاثة وألف الاستغاثة (Lam & Alif al-Istigatha)

**الاستغاثة** = seeking help/rescue

**لام الاستغاثة:**
- Used as a harf jarr before the munada when seeking help
- Makes the munada مجرور (majroor)
- Example: يَا لَزَيْدٍ (Ya la-Zaydin — O [calling upon] Zaid [for help]!)

**ألف الاستغاثة:**
- Alif + ها is added to the end of the munada
- Munada becomes مفتوح (maftooh — with fatha)
- Example: يَا زَيْدَاهُ (Ya Zaydahu!)

## I'rab (Sentence Analysis) Examples

### Example 1 — Tahdhir with إياك
**Sentence:** إِيَّاكَ وَالأَسَدَ

| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| إِيَّاكَ | ضمير منفصل، مفعول به، منصوب، وعلامة نصبه الفتحة — maf'ool bihi of the muqaddar fi'l اِتَّقِ |
| وَ | عاطفة (coordinating waw) |
| الأَسَدَ | معطوف على إياك، منصوب |

---

### Example 2 — Ishtighaal
**Sentence:** زَيْدًا ضَرَبْتُهُ

| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| زَيْدًا | مفعول به، منصوب، وعلامة نصبه الفتحة — governed by the muqaddar fi'l ضَرَبْتُ (dropped obligatorily) |
| ضَرَبْتُ | فعل ماضٍ، مشغول بالضمير هُ — cannot govern زيد |
| هُ | ضمير متصل، مفعول به لـ ضَرَبَ — refers to زيد |

---

### Example 3 — Munadha: مفرد معرفة
**Sentence:** يَا زَيْدُ

| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| يَا | حرف نداء، مبني على السكون، لا محل له من الإعراب |
| زَيْدُ | مُنَادى، مفرد معرفة، مبني على الضمة في محل نصب |

---

### Example 4 — Munadha: مضاف
**Sentence:** يَا عَبْدَ اللهِ

| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| يَا | حرف نداء |
| عَبْدَ | مُنَادى مضاف، منصوب، وعلامة نصبه الفتحة |
| اللهِ | مضاف إليه، مجرور |

---

### Example 5 — Munadha: معرَّف بالـ
**Sentence:** يَا أَيُّهَا الرَّجُلُ

| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| يَا | حرف نداء |
| أَيُّ | مُنَادى، مبني على الضمة في محل نصب |
| هَا | حرف تنبيه (attention particle) |
| الرَّجُلُ | بدل أو عطف بيان، مرفوع |

---

### Example 6 — Nakirah Ghayr Mu'ayyana
**Sentence:** يَا رَجُلًا خُذْ بِيَدِي

| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| يَا | حرف نداء |
| رَجُلًا | مُنَادى ناكرة غير معيّنة، منصوب، وعلامة نصبه الفتحة التنوينية |
| خُذْ | فعل أمر |
| بِيَدِي | جار ومجرور |

## Practice Examples

- يَا زَيْدُونَ — O Zaids!

- يَا عَبْدَ اللهِ — O servant of Allah!
- يَا ابْنَ زَيْدٍ — O son of Zaid!
- يَا ابْنَ آدَمَ — O son of Adam!

**Munadha — mansub (mushabbah bil-muzaf):**
- يَا طَالِعًا جَبَلًا — O one climbing a mountain!

- يَا رَجُلًا خُذْ بِيَدِي — O (some) man, take my hand!

- يَا أَيُّهَا الْمُسْلِمُونَ — O Muslims!
- يَا أَيَّتُهَا النَّفْسُ الْمُطْمَئِنَّةُ — O tranquil soul!

- يَا لَزَيْدٍ — O [calling upon] Zaid for help!
- يَا زَيْدَاهُ — O Zaid! (with alif al-istigatha)

## Key Takeaways

1. **Tahdhir and ishtighaal are both cases where the fi'l is dropped wujuban (obligatorily)** — not optionally. Any sentence showing the pattern of tahdhir or ishtighaal with the fi'l present is considered malformed.

2. **إياك is not an original munfasil pronoun** — it originated as the attached pronoun كَ which had to be detached when the governing fi'l اِتَّقِ was dropped, since an attached pronoun cannot float without a host.

3. **"Mufrad" in the context of munadha does not mean singular** — it means "not muzaf." A dual or plural munada that is not in construct state is still called mufrad for the purposes of this rule and takes the marfu/mabni form.

4. **A pronoun (ضمير) can never precede its referent (مرجع)** — this is the structural reason ishtighaal requires the initial noun to appear before the verb+pronoun cluster, and why we cannot simply reorder the sentence.

5. **The nakirah maqsuda (ناكرة مقصودة) behaves like a ma'rifa** — when you address a visually identifiable, specific person, even a common noun like رجل takes damma (يَا رَجُلُ), because your intention makes it definite. The nakirah ghayr mu'ayyana (calling any unknown person) takes fatha/tanwin (يَا رَجُلًا).

6. **يَا أَيُّهَا / يَا أَيَّتُهَا is obligatory** when the munada carries الـ — harf nida cannot directly precede الـ without this separator.

7. **The harf nida يَا may be dropped** when followed by Amr or Nahi, because direct address already carries its meaning (as seen in the Quranic example: يُوسُفُ أَعْرِضْ عَنْ هَذَا).
