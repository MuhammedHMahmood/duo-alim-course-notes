## Grammar Rules Covered

### 1. Definition of Mustasna

**Mustasna** is the noun mentioned after **إِلَّا (Illa)** and its sisters. It is the word that is *excluded* from the ruling (Hukm) applied to what comes before Illa.

- What comes **before** Illa → **Mustasna Minhu** (the thing excluded from)
- What comes **after** Illa → **Mustasna** (the thing being excluded)
- Illa and its sisters → **Adatul Istisna** (particle of exception)

**Example:**
> جَاءَ الْقَوْمُ إِلَّا زَيْدًا
> *The people came except Zayd.*

- **الْقَوْم** = Mustasna Minhu
- **إِلَّا** = Adatul Istisna
- **زَيْدًا** = Mustasna

The purpose of using Mustasna is to give complete, accurate information — to prevent the listener from being under a false assumption. Without it, the listener assumes the ruling applies to everyone/everything in the Mustasna Minhu.

---

### 2. Types of Mustasna

Mustasna is of **two types**:

#### a. Muttasil (مُتَّصِل — Connected)
When the Mustasna **belongs to the same jins (category/type)** as the Mustasna Minhu — i.e., the excepted thing is genuinely part of the group it is being excluded from.

**Example:**
> جَاءَ الْقَوْمُ إِلَّا زَيْدًا
> *The people came except Zayd.*
- Zayd is a person; al-Qawm is a group of people → same jins → **Muttasil**

#### b. Munqati' (مُنْقَطِع — Disconnected)
When the Mustasna **does not belong to the same jins** as the Mustasna Minhu — i.e., the excepted thing was never actually part of the group.

**Example:**
> جَاءَ الْقَوْمُ إِلَّا حِمَارًا
> *The people came except a donkey.*
- Al-Qawm = humans; Himar = animal → different jins → **Munqati'**

**Example:**
> رَأَيْتُ الطُّلَّابَ إِلَّا قِطَّةً
> *I saw the students except a cat.*
- Students = humans; cat = animal → different jins → **Munqati'**

---

### 3. Particles of Exception (Adatul Istisna — أَدَوَاتُ الِاسْتِثْنَاء)

In addition to **إِلَّا (Illa)**, the following words function as particles of exception. When Mustasna follows any of these, it is **Mansoob**:

| Particle | Notes |
|---|---|
| خَلَا | Used in place of Illa |
| عَدَا | Used in place of Illa |
| مَا خَلَا | مَا + خَلَا |
| مَا عَدَا | مَا + عَدَا |
| لَيْسَ | |
| لَا يَكُونُ | |

**Examples:**
> جَاءَ الْقَوْمُ خَلَا زَيْدًا
> جَاءَ الْقَوْمُ عَدَا زَيْدًا
> جَاءَ الْقَوْمُ مَا خَلَا زَيْدًا
> جَاءَ الْقَوْمُ مَا عَدَا زَيْدًا
> جَاءَ الْقَوْمُ لَيْسَ زَيْدًا
> جَاءَ الْقَوْمُ لَا يَكُونُ زَيْدًا

In all these cases, **Zaydun is Mansoob**.

---

### 4. I'rab of Mustasna — Four Categories (Two Covered This Session)

#### Category 1: Mustasna is Mansoob (Nasb is Wajib)

**Conditions — any one of the following must apply:**

1. It is **Muttasil**, comes after **Illa**, in **Kalam-e-Mujab** (affirmative sentence — no Nafi, Nahi, or Istifham)
2. It is **Munqati'**, comes after **Illa**, in **Kalam-e-Mujab** (same rule applies — the Muttasil/Munqati' distinction does not matter here, kama marra)
3. **Mustasna is Muqaddam** (placed before Mustasna Minhu — the reverse of normal order)
   - Normal order: Mustasna Minhu → Illa → Mustasna
   - Reversed: **مَا جَاءَنِي إِلَّا زَيْدًا أَحَدٌ** (Mustasna precedes Mustasna Minhu)
4. After **خَلَا / عَدَا**
5. After **مَا خَلَا / مَا عَدَا / لَيْسَ / لَا يَكُونُ**

> **Summary of Category 1:** Mustasna = **Mansoob** (obligatory/only option)

**Example:**
> جَاءَ الْقَوْمُ إِلَّا زَيْدًا
- Kalam Mujab, Muttasil, after Illa → Zaydun is **Mansoob**

---

#### Category 2: Two Options Allowed (Nashb OR Badal)

**Conditions:**
- Comes after **Illa**
- Sentence is **Kalam-e-Ghayr Mujab** (non-affirmative — contains Nafi, Nahi, or Istifham)
- **Mustasna Minhu is Mazkur** (mentioned, not omitted)

**Two I'rab options:**

**Option A — Nasb (Mansoob as Mustasna)**
The default I'rab of Mustasna is Nasb. This is always valid.

**Option B — Badal (Mubdal Minhu's Arab is followed)**
The Mustasna is treated as a **Badal** (substitute) of the Mustasna Minhu. Since Badal is a type of Tabi' (dependent I'rab), it follows the I'rab of its **Mubdal Minhu**:
- Mubdal Minhu Marfu → Badal is Marfu
- Mubdal Minhu Mansoob → Badal is Mansoob
- Mubdal Minhu Majroor → Badal is Majroor

**Examples:**

> مَا جَاءَنِي أَحَدٌ إِلَّا زَيْدًا / زَيْدٌ
- Kalam Ghayr Mujab (مَا نَافِيَة), Mustasna Minhu (أَحَدٌ) is Mazkur and **Marfu**
- Option A: **زَيْدًا** (Mansoob as Mustasna)
- Option B: **زَيْدٌ** (Marfu as Badal — follows Mubdal Minhu أَحَدٌ which is Marfu)

> مَا رَأَيْتُ طَالِبًا إِلَّا زَيْدًا
- Kalam Ghayr Mujab, Mustasna Minhu (طَالِبًا) is Mazkur and **Mansoob**
- Option A: **زَيْدًا** (Mansoob as Mustasna)
- Option B: **زَيْدًا** (Mansoob as Badal — follows Mubdal Minhu طَالِبًا which is Mansoob)
- Both options coincidentally give **Mansoob** here

> مَا مَرَرْتُ بِطَالِبٍ إِلَّا زَيْدًا / زَيْدٍ
- Kalam Ghayr Mujab, Mustasna Minhu (طَالِبٍ) is Mazkur and **Majroor**
- Option A: **زَيْدًا** (Mansoob as Mustasna)
- Option B: **زَيْدٍ** (Majroor as Badal — follows Mubdal Minhu طَالِبٍ which is Majroor)

---

### Key Rule on Badal

**Badal** is a type of dependent (غير مستقل) I'rab — it has no independent I'rab of its own. It always follows the I'rab of its **Mubdal Minhu**. The same applies to all Tawabi' (dependents), which will be covered in a dedicated upcoming chapter.

## Important Vocabulary

| Arabic Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| مُسْتَثْنَى (Mustasna) | The excepted noun — what is mentioned after Illa and excluded |
| مُسْتَثْنَى مِنْهُ (Mustasna Minhu) | The noun from which something is excluded; comes before Illa |
| أَدَاةُ الِاسْتِثْنَاء (Adatul Istisna) | Particle of exception (Illa and its sisters) |
| مُتَّصِل (Muttasil) | Connected exception — Mustasna is of the same jins as Mustasna Minhu |
| مُنْقَطِع (Munqati') | Disconnected exception — Mustasna is of a different jins than Mustasna Minhu |
| جِنْس (Jins) | Type, category, genus |
| كَلَامٌ مُوجَب (Kalam Mujab) | Affirmative sentence — no negation, prohibition, or interrogation |
| كَلَامٌ غَيْرُ مُوجَب (Kalam Ghayr Mujab) | Non-affirmative sentence — contains Nafi, Nahi, or Istifham |
| نَفْي (Nafi) | Negation |
| نَهْي (Nahi) | Prohibition |
| اسْتِفْهَام (Istifham) | Interrogation / questioning |
| مُقَدَّم (Muqaddam) | Placed before / fronted |
| مُؤَخَّر (Muakhar) | Placed after / deferred |
| مَذْكُور (Mazkur) | Mentioned, present in the sentence (not omitted) |
| مَحْذُوف (Mahdhuf) | Omitted, dropped from the sentence |
| بَدَل (Badal) | Substitute — a type of Tabi' that follows its Mubdal Minhu in I'rab |
| مُبْدَلٌ مِنْهُ (Mubdal Minhu) | The word the Badal substitutes; determines the Badal's I'rab |
| تَابِع (Tabi') | A dependent word whose I'rab follows another word |
| وَجْهَانِ (Wajhaan) | Two permissible options / two valid I'rab possibilities |
| إِعْرَاب مُسْتَقِل (I'rab Mustaqil) | Independent I'rab — word has its own fixed case ending |
| إِعْرَاب غَيْرُ مُسْتَقِل (I'rab Ghayr Mustaqil) | Dependent I'rab — word's case ending depends on another word |

## I'rab (Sentence Analysis) Examples

**Example 1: Kalam Mujab — Category 1**
> جَاءَ الْقَوْمُ إِلَّا زَيْدًا

| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| الْقَوْمُ | Mustasna Minhu — Marfu (Fa'il) |
| إِلَّا | Adatul Istisna — particle, no I'rab |
| زَيْدًا | Mustasna — **Mansoob** (Category 1: Mujab, Muttasil, after Illa) |

---

**Example 2: Kalam Ghayr Mujab — Category 2, Mubdal Minhu Marfu**
> مَا جَاءَنِي أَحَدٌ إِلَّا زَيْدًا / زَيْدٌ

| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| مَا | Nafi — negation particle |
| أَحَدٌ | Mustasna Minhu — Marfu (Fa'il) |
| إِلَّا | Adatul Istisna |
| زَيْدًا | Option A: Mustasna — **Mansoob** |
| زَيْدٌ | Option B: Badal of أَحَدٌ — **Marfu** (follows Mubdal Minhu) |

---

**Example 3: Kalam Ghayr Mujab — Category 2, Mubdal Minhu Mansoob**
> مَا رَأَيْتُ طَالِبًا إِلَّا زَيْدًا

| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| مَا | Nafi |
| طَالِبًا | Mustasna Minhu — Mansoob (Maf'ul Bihi) |
| إِلَّا | Adatul Istisna |
| زَيْدًا | Option A: Mustasna — Mansoob; Option B: Badal of طَالِبًا — also **Mansoob** (both coincide) |

---

**Example 4: Kalam Ghayr Mujab — Category 2, Mubdal Minhu Majroor**
> مَا مَرَرْتُ بِطَالِبٍ إِلَّا زَيْدًا / زَيْدٍ

| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| مَا | Nafi |
| بِطَالِبٍ | Mustasna Minhu — Majroor (by بِ) |
| إِلَّا | Adatul Istisna |
| زَيْدًا | Option A: Mustasna — **Mansoob** |
| زَيْدٍ | Option B: Badal of طَالِبٍ — **Majroor** (follows Mubdal Minhu) |

## Key Takeaways

1. **Mustasna** is the noun after Illa (and its sisters) that is excluded from the ruling applied to the Mustasna Minhu before Illa.

2. The **three-way terminology** to know: Mustasna / Mustasna Minhu / Adatul Istisna (parallels: Hal/Sahib al-Hal, Tamyiz/Mumayyaz).

3. **Muttasil vs. Munqati'** depends entirely on whether Mustasna shares the same jins as Mustasna Minhu — same jins = Muttasil, different jins = Munqati'.

4. **Nasb is the original, default I'rab** of Mustasna — it is the "copyright" I'rab. It applies obligatorily in Category 1.

5. In **Category 2** (Kalam Ghayr Mujab, Mustasna Minhu Mazkur), **two options** are valid: Nasb (as Mustasna) or following the Mubdal Minhu's I'rab (as Badal). The Badal option produces different endings depending on the Mubdal Minhu's case.

6. **Badal is a dependent I'rab** — it has no fixed I'rab of its own. It always mirrors its Mubdal Minhu. This principle will be elaborated in the upcoming Tawabi' chapter.

7. Categories 3 and 4 of Mustasna I'rab are deferred to the next session.
