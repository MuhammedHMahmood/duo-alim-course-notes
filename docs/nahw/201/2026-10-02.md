## Session Overview

This session covered two major topics: (1) the types of Khabar (predicate) and the rules governing them, including a detailed discussion of Sifat acting as Mubtada after Harf Nafi — a special exception to the general rule that Musnad cannot become Mubtada; and (2) an introduction to the Nawasikh (specifically Inna and her sisters), their effect on Mubtada and Khabar, and the restrictions on Taqdim (fronting) of Khabar in that construction.

---

## Grammar Rules Covered

### 1. Types of Khabar (الخبر)

Khabar is not always a single word (Mufrad). It can take four forms:

| Type | Example | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| **Jumlah Ismiyya** | زَيْدٌ أَبُوهُ قَائِمٌ | Zaid = Mubtada; "Abuhu Qa'imun" = Jumlah Ismiyya acting as Khabar |
| **Jumlah Fi'liyya** | زَيْدٌ قَامَ أَبُوهُ | Zaid = Mubtada; "Qama Abuhu" = Jumlah Fi'liyya acting as Khabar |
| **Jumlah Shartiyya** | زَيْدٌ إِنْ جَاءَنِي فَأَكْرَمْتُهُ | Zaid = Mubtada; the Shart + Jaza' = Khabar |
| **Zarfiyya / Jar-Majrur** | زَيْدٌ خَلْفَكَ / عَمْرٌو فِي الدَّارِ | Zarf or Jar-Majrur as Khabar; the Fi'l "اسْتَقَرَّ" is Mahzoov (implied) |

> **Key note on Zarfiyya:** When Khabar is a Zarf or Jar-Majrur, the Zarf is Muta'alliq (linked) to an implied Jumlah — typically **اسْتَقَرَّ** (meaning "to be present / to stay"). So زَيْدٌ فِي الدَّارِ = زَيْدٌ اسْتَقَرَّ فِي الدَّارِ.

---

### 2. Rule: Damir Must Be Present in Khabar-Jumlah

When the Khabar is a Jumlah (any of the four types above), there **must be a Damir (pronoun) inside the Khabar that refers back to the Mubtada**.

| Example | Damir in Khabar | Refers to |
|---|---|---|
| زَيْدٌ أَبُوهُ قَائِمٌ | **هُ** in أَبُوهُ | زَيْد |
| زَيْدٌ قَامَ أَبُوهُ | **هُ** in أَبُوهُ | زَيْد |
| زَيْدٌ إِنْ جَاءَنِي فَأَكْرَمْتُهُ | **هُ** in أَكْرَمْتُهُ | زَيْد |

---

### 3. Khabar Can Be Muqaddam (Fronted Before Mubtada)

It is **Ja'iz** (permissible) to place the Khabar before the Mubtada.

- Example: **فِي الدَّارِ زَيْدٌ** (instead of زَيْدٌ فِي الدَّارِ)
- This is valid; Khabar can precede Mubtada.

---

### 4. One Mubtada Can Have Multiple Khabar

A single Mubtada may have more than one Khabar simultaneously.

- Example: **زَيْدٌ عَالِمٌ فَاضِلٌ عَاقِلٌ**
- Here, عَالِمٌ، فَاضِلٌ، عَاقِلٌ are three separate Khabar for زَيْد.

---

### 5. Special Mubtada: Sifat After Harf Nafi (Exception to the Musnad Rule)

**Background principle:** Mubtada must be Musnad Ilayhi. Normally, Musnad (which includes Sifat like Ism Fa'il) **cannot** become Mubtada. However, there is one scenario where this exception applies.

#### Three Conditions for Sifat to Become Mubtada:

| # | Condition | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Must be Sighat Sifat** | Ism Fa'il, Ism Maf'ul, Sifat Mushabbaha, or Ism Tafdhil |
| 2 | **Must come after Harf Nafi or Istifham** | e.g., مَا، هَلْ — the Sifat follows the negation/question particle |
| 3 | **Must give Rafa' to the following Ism Zahir** | The Sifat acts like a Fi'l (verb) and causes Rafa' in the noun that follows it |

When all three conditions are met:
- The **Sifat becomes Mubtada**
- The **Ism Zahir after it becomes Khabar**

#### Examples:

| Sentence | I'rab |
|---|---|
| مَا قَائِمٌ زَيْدٌ | قَائِمٌ = Mubtada; زَيْدٌ = Khabar |
| مَا قَائِمٌ الزَّيْدَانِ | قَائِمٌ = Mubtada (stays Wahid/singular); الزَّيْدَانِ = Khabar |
| مَا قَائِمٌ الزَّيْدُونَ | قَائِمٌ = Mubtada (stays Wahid); الزَّيْدُونَ = Khabar |

#### Critical Distinguishing Rule — Amal vs. Khabar:

When Sifat **acts as Amil** (causes Rafa', i.e., is Mubtada in this construction):
- It **does NOT match** its Ma'mul in number or gender — it stays **Wahid (singular)**
- الزَّيْدَانِ is Tasniyah but قَائِمٌ remains singular → this confirms قَائِمٌ is Mubtada

When Sifat **matches** the noun (in gender/number), it is **Khabar**, not Mubtada:
- مَا قَائِمَتَانِ الْبِنْتَانِ → قَائِمَتَانِ matches الْبِنْتَانِ (both Tasniyah) → قَائِمَتَانِ = **Khabar**, الْبِنْتَانِ = **Mubtada**

> **Analogy:** Just as ذَهَبَ (Fi'l) does not change for its Fa'il in number (ذَهَبَ الزَّيْدَانِ / ذَهَبَ الزَّيْدُونَ), Sifat acting as Amil similarly stays Wahid regardless of what follows.

### 6. Nawasikh — Inna and Her Sisters (إِنَّ وَأَخَوَاتُهَا)

These **Huruf** enter upon the Mubtada-Khabar structure and change the I'rab:

| Effect | Term | I'rab |
|---|---|---|
| Gives **Nasb** to Mubtada | اسم إِنَّ | Mansoob |
| Gives **Rafa'** to Khabar | خَبَر إِنَّ | Marfoo' |

**Example:** إِنَّ زَيْدًا قَائِمٌ
- زَيْدًا = Ism Inna (Mansoob)
- قَائِمٌ = Khabar Inna (Marfoo')

#### Ahkam of Khabar Inna:

All rules learned for Khabar of Mubtada apply equally to Khabar of Inna:
- Can be Mufrad, Jumlah Ismiyya, Jumlah Fi'liyya, Shartiyya, or Zarfiyya
- These rules remain unchanged when Inna (or Kana) is introduced

#### Exception — Taqdim of Khabar Inna:

**Khabar of Inna CANNOT be Muqaddam** (placed before Ism Inna), **except** when the Khabar is a **Zarf** (or Jar-Majrur).

- **Not allowed:** ❌ إِنَّ قَائِمًا زَيْدًا (fronting a non-Zarf Khabar)
- **Allowed:** ✅ إِنَّ فِي الدَّارِ زَيْدًا (Zarf Khabar may be fronted — Zuruf are flexible/broad)

> Contrast this with regular Mubtada-Khabar, where Khabar can be freely fronted.

## Important Vocabulary

| Arabic Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| الْمُبْتَدَأ (Mubtada) | Subject / Topic of the sentence (Musnad Ilayhi) |
| الْخَبَر (Khabar) | Predicate / Comment (Musnad) |
| مُفْرَد (Mufrad) | Single word (not a sentence) |
| جُمْلَة إِسْمِيَّة (Jumlah Ismiyya) | Nominal sentence (Mubtada + Khabar) |
| جُمْلَة فِعْلِيَّة (Jumlah Fi'liyya) | Verbal sentence (Fi'l + Fa'il) |
| جُمْلَة شَرْطِيَّة (Jumlah Shartiyya) | Conditional sentence (Shart + Jaza') |
| ظَرْفِيَّة (Zarfiyya) | Adverbial (Zarf or Jar-Majrur) |
| الظَّرْف (Zarf) | Adverb of time or place |
| مُتَعَلِّق (Muta'alliq) | Linked/connected to (a Zarf is Muta'alliq to a Jumlah/Fi'l) |
| اسْتَقَرَّ (Istaqarra) | To be present, to reside, to stay (the implied Fi'l for Zarfiyya Khabar) |
| مَحْذُوف (Mahzoov) | Omitted / implied (not explicitly stated) |
| ضَمِير (Damir) | Pronoun |
| مُقَدَّم (Muqaddam) | Fronted / placed before its normal position |
| مُؤَخَّر (Mu'akhkhar) | Placed after / in its delayed position |
| مُسْنَد (Musnad) | The thing being attributed (predicate side) |
| مُسْنَد إِلَيْهِ (Musnad Ilayhi) | The thing being spoken about (subject side) |
| صِفَة (Sifat) | Descriptive word; includes Ism Fa'il, Ism Maf'ul, Sifat Mushabbaha, Ism Tafdhil |
| اسم فَاعِل (Ism Fa'il) | Active participle (e.g., قَائِم — one who stands) |
| اسم مَفْعُول (Ism Maf'ul) | Passive participle (e.g., مَضْرُوب — one who is struck) |
| صِفَة مُشَبَّهَة (Sifat Mushabbaha) | Adjective resembling active participle |
| اسم تَفْضِيل (Ism Tafdhil) | Elative/comparative adjective (e.g., أَكْبَر) |
| حَرْف نَفِي (Harf Nafi) | Particle of negation (e.g., مَا، لَا) |
| حَرْف اسْتِفْهَام (Harf Istifham) | Particle of interrogation (e.g., هَلْ) |
| عَامِل (Amil) | The governing word that causes a grammatical effect |
| مَعْمُول (Ma'mul) | The word upon which grammatical effect is caused |
| وَاحِد / وَحِيد (Wahid) | Singular |
| تَثْنِيَة (Tasniyah) | Dual |
| جَمْع (Jam') | Plural |
| النَّوَاسِخ (Nawasikh) | Abrogators — words that change the I'rab of Mubtada-Khabar |
| إِنَّ وَأَخَوَاتُهَا (Inna wa Akhwatuha) | "Inna and her sisters" — a group of Huruf that give Nasb to Mubtada |
| اسم إِنَّ (Ism Inna) | The Mubtada after Inna (now Mansoob) |
| خَبَر إِنَّ (Khabar Inna) | The Khabar after Inna (remains Marfoo') |
| مُضَاف (Mudaf) | The first noun in an Idafa (possessive construct) |
| مُضَاف إِلَيْهِ (Mudaf Ilayhi) | The second noun in an Idafa (in Jarr) |
| جَائِز (Ja'iz) | Permissible |
| أَمَل (Amal) | Grammatical action/effect |

## I'rab (Sentence Analysis) Examples

### Example 1: زَيْدٌ أَبُوهُ قَائِمٌ
| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| زَيْدٌ | Mubtada, Marfoo' |
| أَبُوهُ | Mudaf (Mubtada Thani), Marfoo'; هُ = Mudaf Ilayhi (Damir referring to زَيْد) |
| قَائِمٌ | Khabar of أَبُو, Marfoo'; together أَبُوهُ قَائِمٌ = Jumlah Ismiyya acting as Khabar for زَيْد |

---

### Example 2: زَيْدٌ قَامَ أَبُوهُ
| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| زَيْدٌ | Mubtada, Marfoo' |
| قَامَ | Fi'l Madi |
| أَبُوهُ | Fa'il, Marfoo'; هُ = Mudaf Ilayhi (Damir referring to زَيْد) |
| قَامَ أَبُوهُ (whole) | Jumlah Fi'liyya acting as Khabar for زَيْد |

---

### Example 3: زَيْدٌ إِنْ جَاءَنِي فَأَكْرَمْتُهُ
| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| زَيْدٌ | Mubtada, Marfoo' |
| إِنْ | Harf Shart |
| جَاءَنِي | Fi'l Shart; ني = Maf'ul Bih |
| فَأَكْرَمْتُهُ | Jaza' (response to Shart); هُ = Damir referring back to زَيْد |
| إِنْ جَاءَ... فَأَكْرَمْتُهُ | Jumlah Shartiyya = Khabar for زَيْد |

---

### Example 4: عَمْرٌو فِي الدَّارِ
| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| عَمْرٌو | Mubtada, Marfoo' |
| فِي | Harf Jarr |
| الدَّارِ | Majroor |
| فِي الدَّارِ | Jar-Majrur = Khabar (Zarfiyya); full meaning: عَمْرٌو اسْتَقَرَّ فِي الدَّارِ |

---

### Example 5: مَا قَائِمٌ زَيْدٌ (Sifat as Mubtada)
| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| مَا | Harf Nafi |
| قَائِمٌ | Sighat Sifat (Ism Fa'il), Mubtada, Marfoo' — acts as Amil giving Rafa' to زَيْد |
| زَيْدٌ | Khabar, Marfoo' (Fa'il in meaning) |

*All three conditions met: (1) Sifat ✓, (2) after Harf Nafi ✓, (3) gives Rafa' to Ism Zahir ✓*

---

### Example 6: مَا قَائِمَتَانِ الْبِنْتَانِ (Sifat matching = Khabar, NOT Mubtada)
| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| مَا | Harf Nafi |
| الْبِنْتَانِ | Mubtada, Marfoo' (Tasniyah) |
| قَائِمَتَانِ | Khabar, Marfoo' (Tasniyah — matches Mubtada in number and gender) |

*Matching in Tasniyah confirms قَائِمَتَانِ is Khabar, not Mubtada — Amil does not match Ma'mul.*

### Example 7: إِنَّ زَيْدًا قَائِمٌ (Inna)
| Word | I'rab |
|---|---|
| إِنَّ | Harf from Inna's group, Mabni |

## Practice Examples

**Identify whether the Sifat is Mubtada or Khabar in each sentence:**

1. مَا ضَارِبٌ زَيْدٌ
   - Answer: ضَارِبٌ = Mubtada (Ism Fa'il, after مَا, gives Rafa' to زَيْد; does not match زَيْد — both Mufrad coincidentally, but Amil-Mamul relationship holds); زَيْدٌ = Khabar

2. مَا مَضْرُوبٌ صَدِيقُهُ
   - Answer: مَضْرُوبٌ = Mubtada (Ism Maf'ul = Sifat, after مَا, gives Rafa' to صَدِيقُهُ); صَدِيقُهُ = Khabar

3. مَا قَائِمَانِ الزَّيْدَانِ
   - Answer: قَائِمَانِ matches الزَّيْدَانِ (both Tasniyah) → قَائِمَانِ = **Khabar**, الزَّيْدَانِ = **Mubtada**

**Construct sentences using Jumlah as Khabar (ensure Damir is present):**
- Make زَيْد the Mubtada with a Jumlah Fi'liyya Khabar: زَيْدٌ سَافَرَ أَبُوهُ
- Make بَكْر the Mubtada with a Jumlah Shartiyya Khabar: بَكْرٌ إِنْ جَاءَ فَأَكْرِمْهُ

## Key Takeaways

1. **Khabar has four forms:** Mufrad (single word), Jumlah Ismiyya, Jumlah Fi'liyya, Jumlah Shartiyya, and Zarfiyya/Jar-Majrur — all are valid Khabar.

2. **The Damir rule is non-negotiable:** whenever Khabar is a Jumlah, a pronoun (Damir) must exist within it, referring back to the Mubtada. This is what logically links the Khabar sentence to the Mubtada.

3. **For Zarfiyya Khabar**, always remember the implied verb اسْتَقَرَّ that is Mahzoov — the Zarf is Muta'alliq to it.

4. **The Sifat-as-Mubtada rule is an exception**, not the norm. Memorize the three conditions: Sighat Sifat + after Harf Nafi/Istifham + gives Rafa' to Ism Zahir.

5. **The key diagnostic test** for whether Sifat is Mubtada or Khabar: **matching in number/gender = Khabar; not matching (stays Wahid) = Mubtada acting as Amil**. This mirrors how a Fi'l stays Wahid regardless of its Fa'il's number.

6. **Inna does not fundamentally change the nature of Khabar** — all the same rules (Mufrad, Jumlah types, etc.) still apply to Khabar Inna. The only major restriction is that Khabar Inna cannot be Muqaddam over Ism Inna unless the Khabar is a Zarf.
