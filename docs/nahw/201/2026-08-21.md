## Grammar Rules Covered

### Category 5 — I'rab of Tasniyah, Kila/Kilta, Isnan/Isnatan

**Rule:** This I'rab (Alif in Rafa', Ya maqabl al-Fathah in Nasb/Jarr) is exclusive (khas) to three things:
1. **Tasniyah (dual)** — e.g., Muslimani / Muslimayn
2. **Kila and Kilta** — when Mudaf to a Damir (pronoun): Kilahuma / Kilayhima, Kiltahuma / Kiltayhima
3. **Isnan and Isnatan** — the words for "two" themselves

| Haala | Muzakkar | Mu'annath |
|---|---|---|
| Rafa' | جاء رجلانِ (Jaa rajulani) | جاءت امرأتانِ (Jaat imra'atani) |
| Nasb | رأيت رجلينِ (Ra'aytu rajulayni) | رأيت امرأتينِ (Ra'aytu imra'atayni) |
| Jarr | مررت برجلينِ (Marartu birajulayni) | مررت بامرأتينِ (Marartu biimra'atayni) |

**Caution:** Tasniyah and Jam' Muzakkar Salim share the same Ya in Nasb/Jarr, differing only in Harakah before it:
- رأيت مسلمَيْن (Ra'aytu Muslimayni) — I saw two Muslims
- رأيت مسلمِيْن (Ra'aytu Muslimina) — I saw many Muslims

Confusing the Fathah and Kasrah before the Ya changes the meaning entirely.

### Category 6 — I'rab of Jam' Muzakkar Salim, Ulu, and 'Ishrun wa Akhawatiha

**Rule:** Rafa' with Waw; Nasb and Jarr with Ya. This I'rab is given to:

1. **Jam' Muzakkar Salim** — e.g., Muslimun / Muslimin, Mu'minun / Mu'minin, Salihun / Salihin
2. **Ulu** — meaning "possessors of / those with" (equivalent to Dhu/Dhawi); used for plural persons only
   - Rafa': اولو (Ulu) — e.g., اولو الألباب (Ulu al-albab), اولو بأس شديد (Ulu ba'sin shadid)
   - Nasb/Jarr: اولي (Uli)
   - Note: Ulu is inherently plural; it has no singular form in this pattern
3. **'Ishrun wa Akhawatiha** — the tens (20, 30, 40...90); these are Alfaz al-'Uqud
   - Rafa': عشرون ('ishrun) — e.g., جاء عشرون رجلاً
   - Nasb/Jarr: عشرين ('ishrina)

**Key distinction — Noon in Tasniyah vs. Jam' Muzakkar Salim:**

| Form | Noon type | Harakah on Noon |
|---|---|---|
| Tasniyah (e.g., Muslimani) | Noon al-Tasniyah | Always Maksur (كسرة) |
| Jam' Muzakkar Salim (e.g., Muslimuna) | Noon al-Jam' | Always Maftuh (فتحة) |

**Rule of Noon dropping with Idafah:**
When Tasniyah or Jam' Muzakkar Salim is made Mudaf, the Noon drops:
- غلاما زيدٍ (Ghulama Zaydin) — the two boys of Zayd
- مسلمو مصرَ (Muslimuu Misra) — the Muslims of Egypt

### Category 7 — I'rab of Ism Maqsur and Muzaf ilaa Ya' al-Mutakallim

**Definition of Ism Maqsur:** A noun ending in Alif Maqsura (short Alif written as ى without dots), e.g., عصى ('Asa), موسى (Musa), عيسى ('Isa).

**Rule:** All three I'rabs (Rafa', Nasb, Jarr) are **hidden (Taqdiree/Muqaddarah)** — the word's form does not visibly change.

| Haala | Example |
|---|---|
| Rafa' | جاء عيسى (Jaa 'Isa) |
| Nasb | رأيت عيسى (Ra'aytu 'Isa) |
| Jarr | مررت بعيسى (Marartu bi-'Isa) |

**Muzaf ilaa Ya' al-Mutakallim (except Jam' Muzakkar Salim):**
Any noun made Mudaf toward Ya' al-Mutakallim (possessive "my") also has all I'rabs hidden in all three cases.

| Example | Meaning |
|---|---|
| جاء ولدي (Jaa waladee) | My son came |
| رأيت ولدي (Ra'aytu waladee) | I saw my son |
| مررت بولدي (Marartu biwaladee) | I passed by my son |
| جاء أبي (Jaa abee) | My father came |
| جاء صديقي (Jaa sadeeqee) | My friend came |

---

### Category 8 — I'rab of Ism Manqus

**Definition of Ism Manqus:** A noun ending in Ya (ي) that has a Kasrah before it, where the Ya is original (part of the root), not added. e.g., القاضي (Al-Qadi — judge).

**Rule:**
- **Rafa':** Hidden (Taqdiree Dhammah) — the Dhammah is not visibly shown
- **Nasb:** Visible Fathah — this is the only case where I'rab appears
- **Jarr:** Hidden (Taqdiree Kasrah)

| Haala | Example | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Rafa' | جاء القاضي (Jaa al-Qadi) | Dhammah hidden |
| Nasb | رأيت القاضيَ (Ra'aytu al-Qadiya) | Fathah visible |
| Jarr | مررت بالقاضي (Marartu bil-Qadi) | Kasrah hidden |

---

### Category 9 — I'rab of Jam' Muzakkar Salim Muzaf ilaa Ya' al-Mutakallim

**Rule:** When Jam' Muzakkar Salim is made Mudaf to Ya' al-Mutakallim, its I'rab is:
- **Rafa':** Hidden (Taqdiree)
- **Nasb and Jarr:** Ya (Lafzi — visible)

**Morphological derivation of مسلميَّ (Muslimiyya):**

This is a detailed process the teacher explained step by step:

1. Start: **مسلمون** (Muslimuna)
2. Make Mudaf → Noon drops: **مسلموي** (Muslimu + Ya)
3. Waw and Ya come together, Waw has Sukun → Waw converts to Ya (rule of Taalil): **مسلميي** (Muslimiyyi)
4. Two identical letters (Ya + Ya) merge into one with Shaddah: **مسلميَّ** (Muslimiyya)
5. Ya requires Kasrah before it → Dhammah on Meem changes to Kasrah: **مُسْلِمِيَّ** (Muslimiyya)

| Haala | Form |
|---|---|
| Rafa' | جاء مسلميَّ (Jaa Muslimiyya) — Dhammah hidden |
| Nasb | رأيت مسلميَّ (Ra'aytu Muslimiyya) |
| Jarr | مررت بمسلميَّ (Marartu bi-Muslimiyya) |

Similarly for Nasb/Jarr when made Mudaf:
- Base: مسلمين (Muslimina) — Noon drops → مسلمي + Ya → two Ya merge → مسلميَّ

## I'rab (Sentence Analysis) Examples

**Jam' Muzakkar Salim — basic:**
- جاء المسلمون — Jaa al-Muslimuna — Rafa' with Waw; فاعل مرفوع
- رأيت المسلمين — Ra'aytu al-Muslimina — Nasb with Ya; مفعول به منصوب
- مررت بالمسلمين — Marartu bil-Muslimina — Jarr with Ya; مجرور بالباء

**Ism Maqsur:**
- جاء موسى — Jaa Musa — فاعل مرفوع بضمة مقدرة على الألف (hidden Dhammah on Alif)
- رأيت موسى — Ra'aytu Musa — مفعول به منصوب بفتحة مقدرة (hidden Fathah)
- مررت بموسى — Marartu bi-Musa — مجرور بكسرة مقدرة (hidden Kasrah)

**Ism Manqus:**
- جاء القاضي — Jaa al-Qadi — فاعل مرفوع بضمة مقدرة (hidden Dhammah)
- رأيت القاضيَ — Ra'aytu al-Qadiya — مفعول به منصوب بالفتحة الظاهرة (visible Fathah)
- مررت بالقاضي — Marartu bil-Qadi — مجرور بكسرة مقدرة (hidden Kasrah)

**Jam' Muzakkar Salim + Idafah:**
- جاء مسلمو مصرَ — Jaa Muslimuu Misra — Noon dropped for Idafah; مرفوع بالواو
- رأيت مسلمي مصرَ — Ra'aytu Muslimii Misra — Noon dropped; منصوب بالياء

**Jam' Muzakkar Salim + Ya' al-Mutakallim:**
- جاء مسلميَّ — Jaa Muslimiyya — Rafa'; Dhammah hidden due to Taalil process
- رأيت مسلميَّ — Ra'aytu Muslimiyya — Nasb with Ya (Lafzi)
- مررت بمسلميَّ — Marartu bi-Muslimiyya — Jarr with Ya (Lafzi)

- كلتاهما — Kiltahuma (Rafa') / كلتيهما — Kiltayhima (Nasb/Jarr) — Both of them (f.)

- جاء أولو المال — Jaa Ulu al-Mal — The wealthy came (Rafa')

- مررت بأولي الألباب — Marartu bi-Uli al-Albab — I passed by those of understanding (Jarr)

- رأيت مسلمِيْن (Kasrah before Ya) — I saw many Muslims

## Key Takeaways

1. **Nine categories, not nine types of I'rab.** I'rab itself remains four (Rafa', Nasb, Jarr, Jazm). The nine categories describe how and where those I'rabs are expressed — with primary markers (Harakah), secondary markers (letters like Waw/Ya/Alif), or are hidden (Taqdiree).

2. **Two categories have fully hidden I'rab:** Ism Maqsur (all three cases hidden) and Muzaf ilaa Ya' al-Mutakallim (all three cases hidden). Ism Manqus has two hidden (Rafa' and Jarr) and one visible (Nasb with Fathah).

3. **Noon of Tasniyah is always Maksur (Kasrah); Noon of Jam' Muzakkar Salim is always Maftuh (Fathah).** This distinction is critical and must be memorized to avoid changing meaning.

4. **Noon drops in Idafah** — both Tasniyah and Jam' Muzakkar Salim lose their Noon when made Mudaf to anything.

5. **The morphological process of Jam' Muzakkar Salim + Ya' al-Mutakallim** involves four steps: Noon drops → Waw+Ya come together → Waw converts to Ya (Taalil) → Two Ya merge with Shaddah → Dhammah before Ya changes to Kasrah (since Ya demands Kasrah before it). Result: مسلميَّ.

6. **Ulu and Dhu are synonymous** (both mean "possessor of"); Ulu behaves like Jam' Muzakkar Salim grammatically and is inherently plural — it has no Mufrad form in this pattern.

7. **Practical study advice from the teacher:** When reading any Arabic text (e.g., Al-Qira'ah al-Rashidah, Qasas al-Nabiyyin), do not just read for meaning. Stop at every word and ask: why is this Marfu'? Why Mansub? How is this I'rab being expressed — Waw, Alif, Ya, or hidden? Active analysis of Harakah is what differentiates someone who knows Arabic from someone who merely reads the script.
